Andrew is a personal finance expert, DIY investor and life optimizer based in Toronto, Canada. With over 19 years of corporate experience at a leading Canadian company, Andrew combines deep industry knowledge with a passion for technology to help others navigate personal finance and streamline their daily lives.

What Andrew runs

Andrew Carrothers runs Saving Optimizer, a Canadian money-saving brand with practical guides for household spending across 22 everyday categories. Saving Optimizer guides carry Andrew's byline. Andrew also runs a small group of related Canadian sites:

  • CanadianOptimizer.com: tax, investing, and retirement strategies for Canadians.
  • MakeMoneyOptimizer.com: side hustle and extra-income guides for Canadians.
  • CanadianCreditCardFinder.com: a tool for comparing Canadian credit cards.
  • LatestMortgageRates.ca: Canadian mortgage rate comparisons.
  • CanadianDataInsights.com: interactive Statistics Canada census insights.

Saving Optimizer also publishes videos on YouTube at @SavingOptimizer.

How guides with this byline are made

Guides on Saving Optimizer are researched and drafted with AI assistance and checked against official Canadian sources such as the Canada Revenue Agency, canada.ca, provincial government sites, and regulators. Examples use labelled made-up inputs, not invented figures. The full process is on How we create content.
