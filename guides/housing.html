Housing

Housing savings for Canadian households

Housing is usually the largest line on a Canadian household budget. Whether you rent or own, the wins come from all-in stacks (not viral charts), lawful deposits and increases, lease inclusions, and documents — status certificates, not listing slogans. Start with rent vs buy — including Toronto land-transfer-tax years and Vancouver strata fees — then tenant money, insurance shopping, mortgage path, roommate math, condo vs freehold shelter cost, right-sizing and multi-gen living, space-versus-rent math, and property-tax credits or escrow.
