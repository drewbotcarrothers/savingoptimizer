Internet

Internet savings for Canadian households

Canadian internet bills cliff when a promo dies, not when your usage changes. Shop the civic address, normalize 24-month CAD, and treat wireless “no ETF” headlines as a different code. Independents and flankers often ride the same last mile as the incumbent. Gear and Connecting Families are separate levers. Rural households check funded fibre before they buy a dish.
