Subscriptions

Subscriptions savings for Canadian households

Subscriptions are quiet. Streaming, app-store trials, Prime, a gym draft, and an Adobe plan can sit on different bills and still add up. These guides are the household method: export the charges, rotate Netflix, Disney+, and Crave, cancel a trial before it bills, and write down a gym or software fee before you agree to it. The same shelf covers a break-even before you prepay, a Microsoft 365 seat check, a shared renewal calendar, streaming lines on a Rogers or Bell bill, a household cap, and one cloud for photos. Education only. Prices are date-stamped in each guide.
