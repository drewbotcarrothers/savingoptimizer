Subscriptions

Subscriptions savings for Canadian households

Subscriptions are quiet. Streaming, app-store trials, Prime, a gym draft, and an Adobe plan can sit on different bills and still add up. These guides are the household method: export the charges, rotate Netflix, Disney+, and Crave, cancel a trial before it bills, and write down a gym or software fee before you agree to it. The same method covers Microsoft 365 auto-renew, duplicate software, a VPN you cannot remember using, the Netflix tier, Crave and Disney+ billers, a one-month Paramount+ or Apple TV visit, and Subscribe and Save items that outlived the cupboard. Education only. Prices are date-stamped in each guide.
