Subscriptions

Subscriptions savings for Canadian households

Subscriptions are quiet. Streaming, app-store trials, Prime, a gym draft, and an Adobe plan can sit on different bills and still add up. These guides are the household method: export the charges, rotate Netflix, Disney+, and Crave, cancel a trial before it bills, and write down a gym or software fee before you agree to it. Family plans cover Spotify’s same-address rule, Apple Family Sharing, a Netflix Extra Member, and one cloud pool. Phone cancel paths cover Apple and Google Play. Console math covers Game Pass and PlayStation Plus. The same page covers Chef’s Plate and Goodfood skip clocks, Spotify and Apple Music plan math, and a library card before another news or streaming charge. The same method covers Microsoft 365 auto-renew, duplicate software, a VPN you cannot remember using, the Netflix tier, Crave and Disney+ billers, a one-month Paramount+ or Apple TV visit, and Subscribe and Save items that outlived the cupboard. The same shelf covers a break-even before you prepay, a Microsoft 365 seat check, a shared renewal calendar, streaming lines on a Rogers or Bell bill, a household cap, and one cloud for photos. Education only. Prices are date-stamped in each guide.

Console memberships, a discount-gym contract, kids’ app renewals, and a meal-kit skip deadline sit on that same card. The guides further down are those decisions.
