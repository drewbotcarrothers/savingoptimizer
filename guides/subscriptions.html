Subscriptions

Subscriptions savings for Canadian households

Subscriptions are quiet. Streaming, app-store trials, Prime, a gym draft, and an Adobe plan can sit on different bills and still add up. These guides are the household method: export the charges, rotate Netflix, Disney+, and Crave, cancel a trial before it bills, and write down a gym or software fee before you agree to it. The same page covers Chef’s Plate and Goodfood skip clocks, Spotify and Apple Music plan math, and a library card before another news or streaming charge. Education only. Prices are date-stamped in each guide.
