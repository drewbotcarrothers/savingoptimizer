Utilities

Utilities savings for Canadian households

In a Canadian house or condo, the utility bill is a heating, delivery, and rate-plan story. Stop heated air from leaving, use less of what remains, then pay a fair commodity price. Equal billing is cashflow, not a discount. Gadgets and demand-response credits come after the basics. Federal Greener Homes and OHPA intakes are closed — date-stamp every rebate before you sign. Solar credits follow your utility’s tariff, not a U.S. net-metering brochure. Municipal water and sewer usually track the same meter. Ontario’s winter disconnection ban pauses the cut-off, not the balance. Read the delivery line before you chase a ¢; Prairie and Atlantic bills have fewer clock tricks; Alberta gas has a monthly default that is not the electricity RoLR. After the envelope: laundry and hot water, leftover lighting, honest phantom-load hunts, and a call-early path if a bill or a disconnection notice lands.
