How Vancouver Residential Parking Permits Fit a Car-Lite Budget

The permit is cheap; the car is the expensive part · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

In most Vancouver zones, a residential parking permit is only $105 a year, so it isn't the expensive part of keeping a car. In the West End it's $497.73. A permit doesn't guarantee a spot, so compare it with a rented stall, and remember that insurance, fuel and repairs cost far more. If you drive only a few times a month, Evo or Modo may beat owning a car at all.

Key takeaways

  • Most zones: $105 a year (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Fairview Slopes, Oak West, VGH: $157.50; Robson North: $210; West End: $497.73.
  • You must live in the zone, with your ICBC insurance showing that address.
  • Visitor permits: $17.33 a week for out-of-town guests.
  • Car-share vehicles like Evo and Modo can park in permit zones.

How to apply

  • Check that your address is in a permit zone.
  • Make sure your ICBC insurance shows your current address.
  • Buy online or through 3-1-1 with a credit card.
  • Wait 3 hours before parking in the zone.
  • Stick the decal on the windshield.

Permit vs stall vs car share: an example

Example: a $105 permit is far cheaper than a rented stall at an example $150 a month ($1,800 a year). But if you drive only a few times a month, car share can beat owning the car entirely. See Evo and Modo vs owning and Compass monthly vs stored value.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a Vancouver residential parking permit?

$105 a year in most zones in 2026, and up to $497.73 in the West End.

Who can get a Vancouver parking permit?

Residents of the zone whose ICBC insurance shows that address.

Can visitors park in a permit zone?

Out-of-town guests can use a visitor permit, $17.33 a week in most zones.

Does a permit guarantee a parking space?

No. It lets you park in your zone if there's room.

Can Evo or Modo park in permit zones?

Yes, car-share vehicles can park there.
