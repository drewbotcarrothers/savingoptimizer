When Peer-to-Peer Car Rental Makes Sense for Canadians—and When It Does Not

Pick the protection plan before you pick the car · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Peer-to-peer car rental, like Turo, makes sense for weekend trips, when you need a specific vehicle, or where rental counters are expensive or far away. It doesn't make sense for short city errands, where car share is usually cheaper, or if you'd skip protection to save money. On Turo in Canada, you must be 23 or older, and the plan you pick sets how much you'd pay if the car is damaged.

Key takeaways

  • Turo: you must be 23 or older to book in Canada (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Search prices include all mandatory fees.
  • Damage responsibility: $0 (Premier, where available), $500 (Standard), $2,000 (Minimum), or no limit if you decline.
  • Every trip includes $2 million in third-party liability insurance.
  • Take photos at pickup and return.

When it makes sense, and when it doesn't

  • Makes sense: weekend trips, a truck or minivan for a day, towns without rental counters.
  • Doesn't: short errands in a city with car share, or if you'd decline protection.
  • Check kilometre limits, fuel or charging rules, and late-return fees.
  • Red flags: a host asking to pay or communicate outside the platform.

A weekend: an example

Example: a two-day trip at an example $70 a day plus an example $40 for the Standard plan is $180. An example rental counter quote of $220 with similar coverage costs more. Compare the total at checkout, not the daily rate.

Frequently asked questions

How old do you have to be to rent on Turo in Canada?

23 or older, and older for some vehicle classes.

Does Turo include insurance?

Every trip includes $2 million in third-party liability insurance. Damage to the host's car depends on your protection plan.

What if I decline protection?

You're responsible for all damage costs with no maximum.

Is Turo cheaper than a rental car?

Sometimes, especially for weekends or specific vehicles. Compare the total at checkout.

Is car share better than Turo?

For short city trips, car share is often cheaper and simpler.
