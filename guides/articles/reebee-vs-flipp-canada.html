Reebee vs Flipp for Canadian Flyer Hunting: Which Should You Use?

Same company, mostly the same flyers: pick the layout you like and use it every week · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

You only need one. Flipp bought reebee in 2022, and both free apps now show largely the same weekly flyers. Flipp has the bigger name and item search across every flyer near you; reebee organizes your list by store, which some shoppers prefer. Pick the layout you like, and use it the same way every week.

Key takeaways

  • Flipp acquired reebee in June 2022 and kept both apps.
  • Since June 2023, reebee.com and Flipp share one website; the apps stay separate.
  • Both are free, with flyers by postal code, item search, lists and watch lists.
  • Either app's flyer can be shown as price-match proof at stores that match.
  • Example: five matches a week at $1.50 each saves $7.50.

A weekly routine

  • Check new flyers when they drop; most grocery flyers start Thursday.
  • Search your staples by name, not by store.
  • Clip the best price for each item to your list.
  • At a store that price matches, show the clip at the till.
  • Note the regular price of staples so you know a real sale.

What neither app tells you

A flyer calls every price a deal. Neither app shows whether this week's price is low for that item, so keep a simple price book of your 20 most-bought staples.

Frequently asked questions

Is reebee the same as Flipp?

Same company. Flipp bought reebee in 2022, and both apps show largely the same flyers.

Do I need both Flipp and reebee?

No. Pick the one whose layout you prefer.

Are Flipp and reebee free?

Yes, both apps are free.

Can I use reebee or Flipp to price match?

Yes, at stores that price match, show the current flyer in the app at the till.

What happened to reebee.com?

Since June 2023, reebee and Flipp share one website. The reebee app still exists.
