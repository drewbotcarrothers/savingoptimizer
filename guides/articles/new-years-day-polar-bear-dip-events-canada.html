New Year's Day Events: A Canadian Cost Guide

Start the year with a free plunge or a levee · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Many New Year's Day events in Canada are free. Vancouver's 107th Polar Bear Swim at English Bay returns on 1 January 2027 as a free family event, and Oakville's 42nd Courage Polar Bear Dip at Coronation Park is free to register, with optional pledges for clean water. New Year's levees, outdoor skating and winter walks are other free ways to start the year.

Key takeaways

  • Vancouver Polar Bear Swim: 1 January 2027 at English Bay, a free family event. Register to swim (City of Vancouver, checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Courage Polar Bear Dip, Oakville: 1 January 2027 at Coronation Park, free to register, raising money for clean water.
  • New Year's levees hosted by lieutenant governors, mayors and military units are traditionally free and open to the public.
  • Outdoor rinks and trails are free; skate rentals cost extra where available.
  • Safety: go with others, follow organizers' instructions and bring warm, dry clothes.

Polar bear dips

Two large 2027 New Year's Day dips. Checked 24 Nov 2026. As of 24 Nov 2026.
Event Where Cost
Vancouver Polar Bear Swim (107th) English Bay Beach Free; register to swim
Courage Polar Bear Dip (42nd) Coronation Park, Oakville Free to register; pledges optional

The Courage dip lists registration from 12:30 p.m., a costume contest at 1:15 p.m. and the dip at 2 p.m. Pledge fundraising is optional. If you give, ask whether the charity issues tax receipts; our donation tax credit guide explains the credit.

Levees and other free events

  • New Year's levees: receptions hosted by lieutenant governors, municipal leaders and Canadian Armed Forces units, traditionally free and open to the public.
  • Outdoor skating at city rinks.
  • Guided winter walks or hikes in parks.
  • Library or museum family programs, where open.
  • Community first-day runs and walks, some with small entry fees.

Safety

  • Only dip at organized events with lifeguards and first aid.
  • Go in with a friend and stay in briefly.
  • Bring towels, warm dry clothes, a hat and footwear.
  • Skip alcohol before or after cold water.
  • People with heart conditions should ask a doctor first.

Plan: an example

A family of four goes to a polar bear dip. Registration is free. They bring a thermos and towels from home and take transit at an example $10. Total: $10. Buying hot drinks and food there could add an example $30.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Vancouver Polar Bear Swim free?

Yes. It's a free family event on 1 January 2027 at English Bay. Register to swim.

Is the Courage Polar Bear Dip free?

Registration is free. Pledges for clean water are optional.

What is a New Year's levee?

A traditional New Year's Day reception hosted by lieutenant governors, mayors and military units, usually free and open to the public.

Is a polar bear dip safe?

At organized events with lifeguards and first aid, for healthy people who stay in briefly. Ask a doctor if you have a heart condition.

What should I bring to a polar bear dip?

Towels, warm dry clothes, a hat, footwear and a warm drink.
