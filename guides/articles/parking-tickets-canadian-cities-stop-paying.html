How to Stop Bleeding Money on Parking Tickets in Canadian Cities

Read the whole pole, set a timer, and know your deadlines · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Most parking tickets come from a sign you didn't read, a meter that ran out, or a snow rule you didn't know about. Read every sign on the pole, use your parking app's timer, and know your deadlines. In Toronto, you have 15 days to pay or dispute. In Vancouver, paying within 14 days cuts the fine by 30%. If you're getting a ticket every couple of months, a permit or another way of getting around may be cheaper.

Key takeaways

  • Toronto: parking without paying is $50, up from $30 in 2024 (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Toronto: 15 days to pay or dispute; screening can take up to 10 months.
  • Paying a Toronto ticket ends your right to dispute it.
  • Vancouver: $150 for no permit in a permit zone, 30% off if paid within 14 days.
  • Example: six $50 tickets a year is $300.

Habits that prevent tickets

  • Read every sign on the pole, including arrows and times of day.
  • Use the city's parking app and set its reminder.
  • Check snow-route and street-cleaning rules in winter and spring.
  • Take a photo of where you parked and the signs.
  • Don't park near hydrants, crosswalks or driveways.

When to dispute

Dispute when you have evidence, like a photo of a missing or confusing sign or a broken meter. In Toronto, you can request a screening review online within 15 days, and you don't pay until a decision is made. Undue hardship claims need documents, such as a CRA Notice of Assessment.

Frequently asked questions

How long do I have to pay a Toronto parking ticket?

15 days from the notice date to pay or request a screening review.

Can I dispute a Toronto parking ticket?

Yes, online within 15 days. Screening currently takes up to 10 months, and you don't pay until a decision.

Is there a discount for paying a Vancouver parking ticket early?

Yes. Many tickets drop 30% if paid within 14 days.

What's the fine for parking without paying in Toronto?

$50, up from $30 in 2024.

How do I avoid parking tickets?

Read every sign on the pole, use the parking app timer and check winter rules.
