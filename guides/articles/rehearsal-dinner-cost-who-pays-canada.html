Rehearsal Dinners: A Canadian Cost Guide

Keep the night before the wedding relaxed and on budget · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

A rehearsal dinner for 20 people costs about $1,700 at a mid-range steakhouse with tax and tip, or a few hundred dollars at home. The groom's family traditionally pays, but today the couple, either family or both often share it. Keep the guest list to the people at the rehearsal, and pick a set menu so the bill is predictable.

Key takeaways

  • Traditionally the groom's family hosts; now anyone can.
  • Invite the wedding party, immediate family, the officiant and their partners.
  • The Keg's group dinner menus start at $66 a person (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • A set menu keeps the bill predictable.
  • Example: 20 guests, about $1,729 at a restaurant or $300 at home.

Who pays

There's no rule. Whoever hosts pays, so agree early. If a family offers, let them choose the venue within a budget you're comfortable with.

Who to invite

  • Everyone in the rehearsal: wedding party, readers, officiant.
  • Parents and siblings, and their partners.
  • Out-of-town guests are optional; a casual welcome drink can cover them.

Ways to save

  • Host a lunch instead of a dinner.
  • Choose a set menu with soft drinks included, and a cash bar for alcohol.
  • Book a weeknight if the wedding is on a Friday or Sunday.
  • Use a family member's backyard and order trays.

For the wedding meal itself, see wedding catering per person and open bar vs cash bar.

Sources

Frequently asked questions

Who pays for the rehearsal dinner?

Traditionally the groom's family. Today the couple, either family or both often share it.

Who should be invited to the rehearsal dinner?

The wedding party, immediate family, the officiant and their partners.

How much does a rehearsal dinner cost?

In this example, about $1,729 for 20 guests at a restaurant with tax and tip, or $300 at home.

Do we need a rehearsal dinner?

No. It's optional; a casual meal or drinks works too.

When is the rehearsal dinner?

Usually the evening before the wedding, after the rehearsal.
