GST/HST credit 2026–27: CGEB payment dates and amounts

New benefit name, same quarterly structure · About 8 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

If you searched for the GST/HST credit 2026–27, the name on CRA’s current page is different: the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (CGEB) replaced it in July 2026. This is the same quarterly benefit structure, not a second grocery cheque to add to the old credit. The annual amount increased by 25% for five years, from 2026 to 2031. This guide uses the new name and keeps the older name in the title so you can find the right program.

Key takeaways

  • The July 2026–June 2027 federal maximum is $679 for a single individual, $890 for a couple, and $234 per eligible child under 19. Those are annual amounts, not a quarterly cheque.
  • The benefit year uses your 2025 tax return. File even with no income; keep your marital status, address and direct deposit up to date.
  • The June 5, 2026 top-up was a separate one-time payment based on the earlier benefit year.
  • Check CRA My Account for your personal payment amount. A national maximum is not a promise to your household.

Payment dates: old name, new name, same quarterly rhythm

The official calendar checked 24 Nov 2026 lists January 5 and April 2 under the GST/HST credit, then July 3 and October 5 under CGEB. The July payment starts a new benefit year. None of those is a December 2026 grocery bonus.

If a payment is missing, CRA’s payment-dates page says to wait 10 working days before contacting it for this benefit. First check whether your spouse received the household payment, whether a return is unfiled, or whether CRA used the payment against a balance. Use the CRA website you navigate to yourself, not a link in a surprise text promising a rebate.

Annual amounts for July 2026 to June 2027

CRA recalculates each July. Adjusted family net income (AFNI) generally starts with line 23600 for you and your spouse or common-law partner, with the specific UCCB/RDSP adjustments CRA lists. This is a family-income calculation, not an individual salary test for a couple.

The single maximum includes an income-tested additional amount: a single person with no children and very low income does not automatically get $679. CRA’s chart shows $445 below $11,564 AFNI and $679 at $25,000. Above the $46,432 phase-out threshold, entitlement falls. At $50,000 AFNI, the official chart shows $500.60 for a single person with no children and $711.60 for a couple with no children. Use the calculator or your notice rather than guessing a uniform maximum.

The June top-up is not part of each future cheque

The one-time payment generally equalled 50% of the annual federal GST/HST credit for July 2025–June 2026. It was based on 2024 AFNI and the family situation in January 2026. Maximums included $267 for a single person without children, $349 for a couple without children, or $533 for a family with two children. Provincial/territorial amounts were excluded. These are past one-time maximums, not recurring quarterly rates.

How to make sure you are considered

  1. File your 2025 income tax return, even if income was zero. Both partners should file.
  2. Check your benefit notice in CRA My Account. It shows your annual entitlement and quarterly schedule.
  3. Report marital-status and custody changes promptly. A partner’s income or a child turning 19 can change the calculation.
  4. If you became a Canadian resident recently, follow CRA’s new-resident application instructions rather than waiting for the next tax season.
  5. Build your grocery budget around the amount actually on the notice. Keep any top-up outside the recurring monthly income line.

Example: a household entitled to $1,358 for this benefit year can treat the $339.50 quarterly equivalent as roughly $113.17 a month in its budget. It is still paid quarterly, so leave part of each deposit for the next two months. This is a budgeting example, not an entitlement assessment.

Frequently asked questions

What happened to the GST/HST credit in July 2026?

The Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (CGEB) replaced it. CRA says eligibility and the payment structure remain the same, with a 25% increase in the benefit amount from July 2026 for five years.

How much can I receive for July 2026 to June 2027?

The federal annual maximum is $679 for a single individual without children, $890 for a married or common-law couple, plus $234 for each eligible child under 19. Income and family circumstances determine the actual entitlement; related provincial amounts may be added.

Is there another GST/HST top-up payment coming this winter?

The official one-time top-up was issued June 5, 2026. It was separate from the regular quarterly benefit. Do not budget for a second top-up unless CRA announces one.

Do I apply for CGEB each year?

In most cases, file your income tax return, even if you had no income. CRA automatically considers you for the benefit. New residents have a separate application route on CRA’s site.

Are the January and April 2027 payment dates confirmed here?

No. The CRA pages checked on November 24, 2026 listed the 2026 dates only. January and April are the usual quarterly months, but this page does not invent the 2027 calendar dates.

Does a raise reduce my next payment immediately?

The normal July recalculation uses the previous year’s tax return. The July 2026 to June 2027 period uses your 2025 adjusted family net income. Reassessments and family changes can also change an amount.
