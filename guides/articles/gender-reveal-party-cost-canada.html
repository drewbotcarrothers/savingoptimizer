Gender Reveal Parties: A Canadian Cost Guide

A small reveal can mean as much as a big one · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

A gender reveal party can be as simple as a cake with coloured filling and a few friends. Costs come from cake, decorations, food and reveal props. Skip smoke bombs, fireworks and other pyrotechnics, which are banned during fire bans and restricted by many municipal bylaws. A baby is expensive, so keep the party small, or combine it with a baby shower.

Key takeaways

  • A cake or cupcakes with coloured filling is a cheap, safe reveal.
  • Pyrotechnic reveals can break fire bans and municipal fireworks bylaws. Check before using any device.
  • A backyard gathering with potluck food keeps costs low.
  • Combining the reveal with a baby shower saves on a second party.
  • Example: a reveal party for 20 guests costs $120 at home, against $450 with a venue and caterer.

Safer and cheaper reveals

  • Cake or cupcakes with coloured filling.
  • A sealed card opened by a friend or the parents.
  • A box of coloured tissue or balloons, opened indoors.
  • Coloured confetti cannons are messy; pick biodegradable confetti and check venue rules.
  • Skip smoke devices and fireworks, especially during fire season.

Rules

Provinces and municipalities restrict open fires and fireworks, and fire bans apply during dry periods. Check your municipality's bylaw and any provincial fire restrictions before using any reveal device outdoors. Parks may need permits for gatherings.

Plan: an example

A couple hosts 20 guests in the backyard with a cake reveal and a potluck. They spend $120.

At home: $120, or $6 a guest. With a venue and caterer: $450. Save the difference for the baby: see our baby's first year cost guide and baby shower costs.

  • Municipal fireworks bylaws and provincial fire restrictions (for example, Ontario's forest fire restrictions and City of Toronto fireworks bylaw), checked 24 Nov 2026.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a gender reveal party cost?

It can be very cheap. In our example, a backyard reveal for 20 guests costs about $120; a venue with a caterer costs about $450.

What is a cheap way to do a gender reveal?

A cake or cupcakes with coloured filling, or a sealed card or box opened with friends.

Are smoke bombs and fireworks allowed for gender reveals?

They may be banned during fire bans and restricted by municipal bylaws. Check local rules, and choose a non-pyrotechnic reveal.

Can you combine a gender reveal with a baby shower?

Yes. Combining them saves on a second party.

Who pays for a gender reveal party?

Usually the parents-to-be, though family sometimes helps.
