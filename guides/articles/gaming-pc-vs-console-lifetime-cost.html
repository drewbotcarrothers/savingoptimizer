Gaming PC vs Console: The Lifetime Cost

Count the subscription, not just the box · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

A console costs less upfront, but online play needs a subscription. A PS5 Digital Edition is $749.99 and PS Plus Essential is $109.99 a year, about $1,300 over five years. A gaming PC costs more upfront but usually has no online fee and cheaper game sales. Over five years the gap is smaller than the sticker prices suggest.

Key takeaways

  • PS5 Digital $749.99; PS5 with disc $819.99; PS5 Pro $1,099.99.
  • PS Plus Essential: $109.99 a year or $14.99 a month.
  • Game Pass: Essential $13.99, Premium $17.99, Ultimate $25.99 a month (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • PC online play usually needs no subscription.
  • Game prices and sales often matter more than hardware.

Ways to pay less on either

  • Buy games on sale, not at launch.
  • Pick the lowest subscription that covers what you play.
  • Pay yearly instead of monthly if you'll keep it.
  • Buy used games for consoles with discs.
  • Upgrade one PC part instead of buying a new PC.

Five-year cost: an example

Example: PS5 Digital at $749.99 plus five years of PS Plus Essential at $109.99 is about $1,300. An example $1,500 gaming PC plus a $300 upgrade later is $1,800. Games come on top of both.
