Cheap Night Out: Bowling, Karaoke, Trivia

Low-cost nights out with friends · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Bowling, karaoke and pub trivia are some of the cheapest group nights out because the cost is shared or free. Bowling is priced per game or per lane hour, private karaoke rooms per room hour, and many pubs run free trivia and open-mic karaoke nights with a food or drink purchase. Look for weekday specials and set a food and drink budget, which is where the bill grows.

Key takeaways

  • Bowling: per game per person or per lane per hour, often plus shoe rental. Check the alley's price page.
  • Private karaoke rooms: priced per room per hour, so a bigger group pays less each.
  • Pub trivia and public karaoke are often free to enter; you pay for what you order.
  • Weekday and early-evening specials are common. Ask or check the venue's site.
  • Example: four friends spend $20 to $30 each, depending on the activity and what they order.

Ways to pay less

  • Go on a weekday or early in the evening.
  • Book a lane or room for a larger group to share the cost.
  • Ask about shoe rental and bring your own if you have them; some alleys don't allow it.
  • Eat before and order one shared plate.
  • Look for student, family or league nights.
  • Check if the karaoke room has a minimum food or drink spend.

Plan: an example group of four

These are example prices, not quotes. Bowling: 2 games at $8 each plus $4 shoes, $20 each. Private karaoke: a $60 hour split four ways, $15 each, plus one drink at $10. Pub trivia: free, plus one drink and a shared plate, about $20 each.

Frequently asked questions

How much does bowling cost in Canada?

It varies by alley. Many charge per game per person or per lane per hour, often with shoe rental extra. Check the alley's price page.

Is pub trivia free?

Often it is, or there's a small team entry fee. You pay for what you order.

How are karaoke rooms priced?

Private rooms are usually priced per room per hour, sometimes with a minimum spend. A bigger group lowers the cost per person.

When is bowling cheapest?

Weekdays and early evenings often have specials. Ask the alley.

What's the cheapest group night out?

Pub trivia or public karaoke, if you keep orders small, followed by shared bowling lanes or karaoke rooms.
