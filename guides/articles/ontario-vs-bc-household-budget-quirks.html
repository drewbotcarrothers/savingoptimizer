Ontario vs BC Household Budget Quirks: Provincial Costs That Change Savings Rates

Same income, different provincial costs · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Moving between Ontario and BC changes your budget even if your pay doesn't. Sales tax, home-buying tax, car insurance and hydro all work differently. Rebuild your budget from your first 2 or 3 months of real bills, and keep a 90-day cash buffer while you do.

Key takeaways

  • Sales tax: 13% HST in Ontario, 5% GST plus 7% PST in BC.
  • On a $700,000 home: $10,475 Ontario land transfer tax vs $12,000 BC property transfer tax.
  • Toronto buyers pay a second, municipal land transfer tax.
  • BC drivers must buy Basic Autoplan from ICBC; Ontario drivers shop private insurers.
  • 2027 rent caps: Ontario 1.9%, BC 2.2%.

Your first 90 days after a move

  • Keep 3 months of expenses in cash.
  • Get car insurance quotes before you move.
  • Budget for deposits on utilities.
  • Update your 50/30/20 split with real bills.
  • Change your address with CRA and your bank.

Why the savings rate shifts

Lower sales tax in BC helps a little on everyday spending, but housing usually decides it. Track 3 months of bills in the new province before setting a savings goal.

Transfer tax: an example

Example: on a $700,000 home, Ontario's tax is $275 + $1,950 + $2,250 + $6,000 = $10,475. BC's is $2,000 + $10,000 = $12,000. First-time buyer rebates can cut both.

Frequently asked questions

Is sales tax higher in Ontario or BC?

Ontario's HST is 13%; BC's GST plus PST is 12%.

How much is land transfer tax on a $700,000 home?

$10,475 in Ontario (more in Toronto) and $12,000 in BC, before rebates.

Can I choose my car insurer in BC?

Basic coverage must come from ICBC; optional coverage can be bought elsewhere.

What are the 2027 rent increase caps?

1.9% in Ontario and 2.2% in BC for most units.

How much cash should I keep after moving provinces?

About 3 months of expenses.
