The 5-Minute PC Optimum Offer-Loading Habit Canadians Skip

Load the offers before the list, not at the till · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 1 Oct 2026

PC Optimum offers in Canada update weekly on Thursdays, and they only earn points if they are loaded before you shop. A five-minute habit covers it: open the app's Your Offers (or Offers at PCOptimum.ca, or Load Offers in the weekly email), load everything, compare the offers with your list and the flyer, and scan your card or app at checkout. Points are worth $10 for every 10,000. The rule that keeps it a saving: only use offers on things you would buy anyway.

Key takeaways

  • Thursday: offers update for the week.
  • Load them in the app (Your Offers), at PCOptimum.ca (Offers), or from the weekly email (Load Offers).
  • Match offers against your list and the flyer; add an item only if you would buy it anyway.
  • Scan your card or the app at checkout, at Loblaw-family stores and Shoppers Drug Mart.
  • 10,000 points = $10, redeemed in $10 increments.

The five-minute routine

  1. After the Thursday refresh, open Your Offers in the PC Optimum app.
  2. Load all offers. Loading is free, and you only earn on what you buy.
  3. Scan the list for items already on your grocery list or in your price book.
  4. Save useful personalized offers for next week if the option is shown and you do not need the item this week.
  5. Check the flyer for the same items: an offer stacked on a sale price can be the best price.
  6. At checkout, scan your card or app before paying.

Avoid the traps

  • Spend-threshold offers (spend a set amount, get points) only help if you would reach the amount anyway.
  • Points on a higher-priced item can still cost more than a cheaper store brand without points. Compare the net price.
  • Offers expire; check the dates before you shop.
  • Redeem points in $10 increments when it suits you; Shoppers bonus redemption events can stretch points.

Where the habit pays

PC Optimum points are earned and redeemed across Loblaw's family of stores, including grocery banners such as Loblaws, No Frills and Real Canadian Superstore, along with Shoppers Drug Mart, and at participating partners such as Esso and Mobil stations. Offers can be store-specific, so read where each one applies before you plan a trip. Points from different stores land in the same account.

Step by step: matching offers to your list

  1. Write the week's grocery list from your meal plan and pantry first.
  2. Open Your Offers and load everything.
  3. Mark offers that match items already on your list.
  4. For each, check the shelf or flyer price and compare it with your price book; points do not rescue an expensive price.
  5. Note which store each offer applies to and plan the trip.
  6. After the trip, check the points posted to your account against the offers you used.

Working out the real price

Because 10,000 points are worth $10, each 1,000 points is worth $1. To compare a points offer with a cheaper option, subtract the points value from the price. These numbers are an example with made-up inputs: a $6 item with 1,000 bonus points works out to $5. If a store brand costs $4.50 without points, the store brand is still cheaper.

Redeeming points

Points can be redeemed in $10 increments. Some shoppers bank points for Shoppers Drug Mart bonus redemption events, when points can buy more than usual; others redeem at the grocery till to lower the weekly bill. Either works; the key is not to let a large balance sit unused for years. Check the app for the current redemption options.

If you shop for more than one household, such as an aging parent, keep separate accounts so offers match each person's habits.

Common mistakes

  • Loading offers after checking out; points only apply to loaded offers when you scan.
  • Forgetting to scan the card or app.
  • Buying unfamiliar items only for the points.
  • Chasing a spend threshold with items you did not need.
  • Using an offer at a store where it does not apply.

Frequently asked questions

When do PC Optimum offers update?

Weekly, on Thursdays.

How do I load PC Optimum offers?

In the PC Optimum app under Your Offers, at PCOptimum.ca under Offers, or by selecting Load Offers in the weekly offers email.

Do I need to load offers to get the points?

Load them before you shop so they apply when you scan your card or app at checkout.

How much are PC Optimum points worth?

10,000 points are worth $10, redeemed in $10 increments.

Should I buy items just for the points?

Only if you would buy them anyway at a price you are happy with. Compare the net price with a cheaper option without points.

Where can I earn PC Optimum points?

Across Loblaw's family of stores, including grocery banners and Shoppers Drug Mart, and at participating partners such as Esso and Mobil stations. Check each offer's terms for where it applies.

How do I work out the value of a points offer?

Each 1,000 points is worth $1, because 10,000 points are worth $10. Subtract that value from the price to compare with a cheaper option.

Can I redeem PC Optimum points in any amount?

Points are redeemed in $10 increments, starting at 10,000 points. Check the app for current redemption options.

Do PC Optimum offers work at every store?

Not always. Some offers are specific to a banner or to Shoppers Drug Mart. Read where each offer applies before planning your trip.
