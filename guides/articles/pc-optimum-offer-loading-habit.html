The 5-Minute PC Optimum Offer-Loading Habit Canadians Skip

Load the offers before the list, not at the till · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 1 Oct 2026

PC Optimum offers in Canada update weekly on Thursdays, and they only earn points if they are loaded before you shop. A five-minute habit covers it: open the app's Your Offers (or Offers at PCOptimum.ca, or Load Offers in the weekly email), load everything, compare the offers with your list and the flyer, and scan your card or app at checkout. Points are worth $10 for every 10,000. The rule that keeps it a saving: only use offers on things you would buy anyway.

Key takeaways

  • Thursday: offers update for the week.
  • Load them in the app (Your Offers), at PCOptimum.ca (Offers), or from the weekly email (Load Offers).
  • Match offers against your list and the flyer; add an item only if you would buy it anyway.
  • Scan your card or the app at checkout, at Loblaw-family stores and Shoppers Drug Mart.
  • 10,000 points = $10, redeemed in $10 increments.

The five-minute routine

  1. After the Thursday refresh, open Your Offers in the PC Optimum app.
  2. Load all offers. Loading is free, and you only earn on what you buy.
  3. Scan the list for items already on your grocery list or in your price book.
  4. Save useful personalized offers for next week if the option is shown and you do not need the item this week.
  5. Check the flyer for the same items: an offer stacked on a sale price can be the best price.
  6. At checkout, scan your card or app before paying.

Avoid the traps

  • Spend-threshold offers (spend a set amount, get points) only help if you would reach the amount anyway.
  • Points on a higher-priced item can still cost more than a cheaper store brand without points. Compare the net price.
  • Offers expire; check the dates before you shop.
  • Redeem points in $10 increments when it suits you; Shoppers bonus redemption events can stretch points.

Frequently asked questions

When do PC Optimum offers update?

Weekly, on Thursdays.

How do I load PC Optimum offers?

In the PC Optimum app under Your Offers, at PCOptimum.ca under Offers, or by selecting Load Offers in the weekly offers email.

Do I need to load offers to get the points?

Load them before you shop so they apply when you scan your card or app at checkout.

How much are PC Optimum points worth?

10,000 points are worth $10, redeemed in $10 increments.

Should I buy items just for the points?

Only if you would buy them anyway at a price you are happy with. Compare the net price with a cheaper option without points.
