Bridal Hair and Makeup: A Canadian Cost Guide

Know the price list before you book the chair · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Bridal hair and makeup in the Toronto area costs about $330 to $615 for the bride, often with a trial at around $400 on top. Bridesmaids and family usually pay $165 to $325 each. Check what's included, and add HST, travel, parking and early-start fees to compare quotes fairly.

Key takeaways

  • Bride hair and makeup: about $330 to $615 in the GTA (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Trials run about $400 for hair and makeup.
  • Bridal party: about $165 for makeup only to $325 for both.
  • Watch for travel, parking, early-start and HST.
  • Example: bride, trial and four others: $2,480, or $1,757 with makeup only for the party.

Do you need a trial?

A trial costs about as much as the day itself, but it lets you test the look and the artist. Skip it if you've worked with the artist before or you want a simple look.

Plan: an example

Example with BlushPretty prices: bride $495, trial $400 and four others at $325 ($1,300) is $2,195, or $2,480 with HST. If the four others book makeup only at $165 each ($660), the total is $1,555, or $1,757 with HST.

Ways to save

  • Ask bridesmaids to do their own hair, or pay for their own services.
  • Book a weekday or off-season date if the artist charges less.
  • Choose an artist close to your venue to avoid travel fees.
  • Skip airbrush and add-ons you don't need.

Frequently asked questions

How much does bridal hair and makeup cost in Toronto?

About $330 to $615 for the bride, based on GTA price lists checked on 24 Nov 2026.

How much is a bridal trial?

About $400 for hair and makeup at the artists checked.

Do bridesmaids pay for their own hair and makeup?

It varies. Agree on it early and tell them before they commit.

Is HST included in bridal hair and makeup prices?

Sometimes. Check each price list; some include HST and some add it.

Should I tip my hair and makeup artist?

Tipping is optional. If you tip, 15% to 20% is common for personal services.
