Party Rentals: A Canadian Cost Guide

Rent what you need, borrow the rest · About 7 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Party rental companies usually charge per item per event, plus delivery, setup and pickup fees, and a damage deposit. Tables and chairs are cheap per piece but add up for a big guest list; tents cost the most and may need a municipal permit and utility locates before staking. Get an all-in quote, borrow what you can, and rent only the pieces you need.

Key takeaways

  • Rentals are priced per item per event, plus delivery, setup, pickup and sometimes a damage deposit.
  • Ask for an all-in quote, including tax and delivery windows.
  • Larger tents may need a municipal permit, and staking needs utility locates. Check your city's rules.
  • Borrow tables and chairs from friends, a community centre or a faith hall where possible.
  • Example: a 40-guest backyard party costs $652 to rent everything before tax, or $300 when chairs and tables are borrowed.

Hidden costs

  • Delivery and pickup fees, often higher for evenings and weekends.
  • Setup and teardown labour.
  • Damage and loss charges; count items when they arrive.
  • Late return fees.
  • Tax on everything.

Rent, borrow or buy

Borrow if friends, a community centre or a faith hall can lend. Rent for large items you'll use once, like a tent. Buy only items you'll reuse often, such as a couple of folding tables. For venues with tables and chairs included, see our hall rental guide.

Plan: an example

A family plans a backyard party for 40 guests. Renting 40 chairs ($120), six tables ($72), six tablecloths ($60), a tent ($300) and delivery ($100) totals $652 before tax at the example prices. Choosing a smaller 10 x 20 ft tent at an example $200 and skipping the tablecloths brings it to $492, and borrowing chairs and tables leaves $300 for the tent and delivery.

Renting everything: $652. Smaller tent, no linens: $492. Borrowed chairs and tables: $300. For food planning, see catering vs potluck.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent tables and chairs?

It varies by company and city. Rentals are usually priced per item per event, plus delivery and pickup.

Do I need a permit for a party tent?

Larger tents may need a municipal permit, and staking needs utility locates. Check your city's rules before booking.

What fees should I ask about?

Delivery, pickup, setup, damage deposits, late returns and tax.

Is it cheaper to buy folding tables?

Only if you'll reuse them often. For one event, borrowing or renting is cheaper.

How far ahead should I book party rentals?

Book early for summer weekends and holidays, when demand is highest.
