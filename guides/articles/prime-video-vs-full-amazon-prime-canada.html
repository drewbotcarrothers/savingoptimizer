Prime Video vs Full Amazon Prime in Canada: Can You Keep One Without the Other?

No video-only plan, so time your monthly Prime instead · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

In Canada you can't buy Prime Video on its own. It comes with Amazon Prime, at $9.99 a month or $99 a year. If you only want Prime Video for a show or two, pay monthly in the months you watch and cancel in between.

Key takeaways

  • No standalone Prime Video plan in Canada (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Amazon Prime: $9.99 a month or $99 a year.
  • Ad-free Prime Video: $2.99 a month extra.
  • Cancelling Prime ends Prime Video; bought titles usually stay.
  • Annual Prime pays off only if you'd keep it about 10 months or more.

Use Prime Video as a rotation slot

  • Pick 1 or 2 streamers at a time.
  • Subscribe for a month when a show you want drops.
  • Binge, then cancel before renewal.
  • Rotate to the next service.
  • Check Memberships and Subscriptions after each change.

Monthly vs annual

Ten months of monthly Prime costs $99.90, about the same as the $99 annual plan. If you only watch 3 or 4 months a year and don't need fast shipping, monthly is cheaper.

Video-only year: an example

Example: 4 months of Prime at $9.99 is $39.96, vs $99 for the annual plan. That saves $59.04 a year.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get Prime Video without Prime in Canada?

No. Prime Video comes with Amazon Prime in Canada.

How much is Amazon Prime in Canada?

$9.99 a month or $99 a year when checked on 24 Nov 2026.

How much is ad-free Prime Video?

$2.99 a month on top of Prime.

Do I lose bought movies if I cancel Prime?

Purchased titles usually stay in your library.

Is monthly or annual Prime cheaper?

Monthly, if you would keep it fewer than about 10 months.
