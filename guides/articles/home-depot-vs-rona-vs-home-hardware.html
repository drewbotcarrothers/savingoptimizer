Home Depot vs RONA vs Home Hardware vs Canadian Tire: Which Saves More on Household Basics?

Same logo, different town, different till · About 14 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 28 Nov 2026

No single chain is cheapest for household basics in Canada, but in the holiday season the bigger money gap is the return clock. As of 28 November 2026, Home Depot gives most items 90 days with a receipt, yet holiday décor, TVs, furniture, generators, and battery or corded tools such as snowblowers get 30 days, and major appliances are non-returnable. RONA also cuts snowblowers and other outdoor power equipment to 30 days and wants holiday items back by the holiday. On price, Home Depot matches a lower advertised price, Kent beats a lower price by 10 percent, Home Hardware earns Scene+ at 50 points per full $50, and Canadian Tire’s base CT Money is 0.4 percent. This page compares those published policies. It does not rank a national winner, because a matched basket was not priced.

Who still price-matches is the household price-match guide. Canadian Tire's sale habits are the sale-cycle guide. What an LED does to the hydro bill is the bulb guide. Only the purchase price belongs here.

Key takeaways

  • Home Depot returns, as of 28 Nov 2026: 90 days with a receipt (365 days on a Home Depot credit card), but 30 days for holiday décor, TVs, furniture, generators, air conditioners, printers, computers, gas-powered equipment, and battery or corded tools, including snowblowers. Major appliances are non-returnable.
  • Example date math: a Black Friday purchase on 27 Nov 2026 runs out around 27 Dec 2026 on a 30-day clock, and around 25 Feb 2027 on a 90-day clock. RONA holiday items must go back on or before the holiday.
  • Home Depot's price guarantee is a match, not a beat, and it does not stack with a Home Depot coupon. Kent's page says it beats a lower price on an identical in-stock item by 10 percent.
  • Home Hardware Scene+ earns 50 points per full $50 before tax, so a $49 purchase earns nothing and $99 earns 50 points (50 cents). Canadian Tire base CT Money is 0.4 percent.
  • No bulb, filter, caulk, and mop basket was priced at all four, so this guide does not crown a cheapest chain.

Who's who in Canadian hardware retail in 2026 (Home Depot, RONA/RONA+, Home Hardware, Canadian Tire, Kent in Atlantic Canada)

Home Depot Canada's price-guarantee page speaks as The Home Depot and does not hand the offer to independent dealers. That is all this guide will say about its ownership.

The page was read 26 September 2026. RONA inc.'s release in Boucherville on 22 June 2023 says the network operates or services some 425 corporate and affiliated dealer stores under the Lowe's, RONA, Réno-Dépôt, and Dick's Lumber banners, and that Lowe's stores being converted will carry the RONA+ banner. Conversions were to start at the end of July 2023, beginning with ten Ontario stores the release names. Warranties offered by Lowe's would be honoured, and gift cards would still be accepted, in that transition note.

Home Hardware's homepage, as of 26 September 2026, calls itself Canada's largest dealer-owned and operated home improvement retailer. Canadian Tire's site says its stores are owned and operated by independent associate dealers, and the pricing policy says those dealers may sell for less. Kent's site, as of the same date, addresses Atlantic Canada: the homepage line is "We're Your Atlantic Canadian Team," and the FAQ says Kent gift cards can be redeemed at retail locations across Atlantic Canada. A Lowe's sign that is now RONA+ is that 2023 conversion, not a U.S. policy you can still quote.

What are the return windows at Home Depot, RONA, Canadian Tire and Kent?

Most items get 90 days with a receipt at all four chains, but the holiday-season purchases are the exceptions. Home Depot's return policy, read 28 November 2026, gives 30 days with a receipt for holiday décor, air conditioners, generators, furniture, televisions, printers, computers, and gas-powered equipment, and says items returned after 30 days or without a receipt may be sent out for repair as customer-owned merchandise. Battery and corded equipment, including snowblowers, is also 30 days. Major appliances are non-returnable: inspect at delivery, refuse the item before signing if it is damaged, and report damage within 48 hours.

RONA's returns page, read the same day, gives most unused items 90 days with the original receipt, major appliances a 30-day return or exchange request in original condition, and new outdoor power equipment such as snow blowers and generators 30 days if unused or unopened. Holiday items must be returned on or before the holiday, and holiday items bought after it are final sale. Canadian Tire's policy page takes unopened items in original packaging back within 90 days with a receipt. Home Depot also lists a 15 percent restocking fee on some special-order returns and cancellations.

Price-match policies compared, including Home Depot's 2026 change

Home Depot's price-guarantee page, read 28 November 2026, will match a current, lower, advertised price on an identical, new, in-stock item from a local Canadian retail store or website, or from a Canadian online-only retailer. The item has to be the same brand, model, and size. Proof at the till is a flyer, a printout, a website link, or a photo. For a homedepot.ca order, the page says to contact Customer Support before you buy. Rental tools are matched against local rental businesses.

The page does not offer an extra 10 percent off that matched price, and you choose between a price match or a Home Depot coupon or promo code, not both. Shipping, delivery, labour, assembly, and installation are not matched. Major appliances have a separate 30-day price adjustment if Home Depot's own price drops after you buy, not if a competitor's does. Example: a $90 lower price elsewhere means you pay $90 at Home Depot.

Kent's price-match page, read 28 November 2026, says Kent will beat a lower price by 10 percent on an identical item in stock at Kent and at a local retail competitor, in store or online, where the online price includes all delivery charges. It matches a lower advertised sale price, and a lower delivered price from a Canadian ".ca" online retailer that is not a local competitor. Clearance, close-outs, membership programs such as Costco, special orders, project quotes, installation, and bonus offers are among the exclusions, and a Kent associate verifies an online price on the competitor's site. Using the same $90 example, a 10 percent beat on $90 would be $81. Canadian Tire's published guarantee is a match inside 200 km, not a 10 percent beat; the day counts are on the sale-cycle and price-match guides. RONA's price-guarantee URL stopped at a security check on 26 September 2026. A retrieval that day said RONA will meet a competitor's lower price on an identical new in-stock item, and that local affiliated dealers may reserve whether they offer it.

Loyalty: Scene+ at Home Hardware, CT Money at Canadian Tire, and what Home Depot/RONA offer

The Scene+ help article for Home Hardware, read 28 November 2026, says you earn 50 Scene+ points for every $50 spent before taxes and delivery charges, in store or at homehardware.ca, at participating Home Hardware, Home Hardware Building Centre, and Home Building Centre stores. The minimum purchase to earn is $50 before tax and delivery, and Home Hardware's terms describe the earn as 50 points per $50 increment. At 1,000 points for $10 off, that is 1 percent, but only on full $50 steps.

Dealer-owned vs corporate stores: why prices vary town to town

Home Hardware's homepage states the dealer-owned model in one line. Canadian Tire's pricing policy says sale dates vary by region and dealers may sell for less, and need not match an online price before the regional sale starts. RONA's 22 June 2023 release describes both corporate and affiliated dealer stores, and the price-guarantee retrieval said affiliated independents may not apply the corporate match. A town with a dealer store is not required to mirror a corporate store's tile.

Home Depot's guarantee does not include that dealer reservation. That does not mean every Home Depot till shows the same number. It means the written offer this guide read is a company page, while the other three banners have put dealer discretion in writing. When you phone a price in, ask which store, and write the town next to the number. The Canadian Tire guide is the longer version of the dealer sentence for that banner.

When to pay a bit more for local service and returns

Pay the higher shelf when the cheaper one fails a test you can name. The return window is unusable.

The filter size is wrong. The caulk is a different product. Delivery wipes out the gap. Kent's FAQ says most Kent delivery orders are a flat $50 and are delivered within 50 km of the chosen store, with extra fees beyond that, and that a Kent associate will call before those extras. That $50 is a service price, not a product price. An appliance return after Kent delivery, on the return page, is within 90 days, in original condition, refunded less delivery fees, with a 25 percent restocking fee. A cheaper appliance that costs a quarter of its price to send back is not cheaper if you might return it.

A dealer store that will reorder a part and a corporate store that will not are different services. This guide does not price that difference, so it does not invent an hourly rate. If the gap on a mop is a dollar and the nearer store is the one you can return to, buy the mop. If the gap on a pallet of filters is large and both stores will take an unopened case back, buy the cheaper case and keep the receipt. The receipt rules are the return-policy guide.

Frequently asked questions

Which hardware store is cheapest for household basics in Canada?

No national winner. This guide does not compare a bulb, filter, caulk, and mop basket at Home Depot, RONA, Home Hardware, and Canadian Tire on the same date, so it does not print a basket total. The cheapest store is the one with the lowest out-the-door price on the identical product in your town, after any eco fee. Dealer-owned banners can differ by location even when the logo matches.

Does Home Depot still beat competitors' prices?

No. The Home Depot Canada price-guarantee page, read 28 Nov 2026, says it will match a current lower advertised price on an identical, new, in-stock item, and the match cannot be combined with a Home Depot coupon. It does not describe an extra 10 percent off. Kent's price-match page, read the same day, says Kent will beat a lower price by 10 percent and will match an advertised sale price.

What happened to Lowe's stores in Canada?

RONA inc., writing from Boucherville on 22 June 2023, said Lowe's stores being converted would carry the new RONA+ banner, with conversions starting at the end of July 2023. The first ten stores named were in Ontario. The same release said warranties offered by Lowe's would be honoured and gift cards would still be accepted. The network it described includes both corporate and affiliated dealer stores.

Why do Home Hardware prices vary by town?

The Home Hardware homepage, as of 26 Sep 2026, calls the chain Canada's largest dealer-owned and operated home improvement retailer. A dealer sets the shelf in that town. Canadian Tire's pricing policy says associate dealers may sell for less, which is the same kind of gap under a different logo.

Which hardware stores have loyalty programs?

Home Hardware: the Scene+ article read 28 Nov 2026 says 50 points per $50 before taxes and delivery, with a $50 minimum, and 1,000 points for $10 off, so a $49 purchase earns nothing and $99 earns 50 points. Canadian Tire: CT Money at a 0.4 percent base, plus bonus offers that have their own rules. This guide does not list a Home Depot or RONA points earn rate, so it does not name one. Kent's FAQ describes a Kent Card as store financing, which is not a points rate this page will rank.

What is Home Depot Canada's return policy for holiday décor and snowblowers?

Home Depot Canada's return policy, read 28 Nov 2026, gives most items 90 days with a receipt, but holiday décor, televisions, furniture, generators, air conditioners, printers, computers, and gas-powered equipment must come back within 30 days with a receipt. Battery and corded equipment, including snowblowers, is also 30 days. Major appliances are non-returnable, so inspect them at delivery. Example: a 27 Nov 2026 purchase on a 30-day clock closes around 27 Dec 2026.
