How to Ask Your Canadian Employer for Bike-to-Work Supports That Save Money

Ask for the things that make biking possible, then the money · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

The supports that save you the most money are the ones that make biking to work possible: secure bike parking, a shower and a locker, and some flexibility on start times and winter days. Ask for those first, then any cash. In Toronto, biking instead of hitting the $155.10 monthly TTC cap could save an example $130 a month.

Key takeaways

  • Ask first for secure bike parking, showers, lockers and flexible start times.
  • Add winter flexibility and safety workshops or group rides.
  • CRA: benefits and allowances are generally taxable unless an exception applies (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • TTC: $3.30 a trip, capped at 47 trips, or $155.10 a month.
  • Bring a one-page cost sheet to HR.

What to ask for

Bike-to-work supports and why they matter. As of 24 Nov 2026.
Support Why it saves money
Secure indoor bike parking Lowers theft risk, so you can ride a better bike
Showers and lockers Lets you ride in any weather and change at work
Flexible start times Avoids rush-hour traffic and dark rides
Winter flexibility Lets you switch to transit or home days
Safety workshop or group rides Helps new riders start

How to pitch it

  • Show how many coworkers would use it (a quick survey).
  • Compare the cost of a bike room to the cost of car parking stalls.
  • Mention health, retention and sustainability goals.
  • Suggest a three-month trial.

Your savings: an example

Example: you ride eight months of the year and spend an example $25 a month on upkeep instead of reaching the $155.10 TTC cap. That's about $130 a month in riding months. See a bike commute safety checklist and employer transit subsidies.

Sources

  • Canada Revenue Agency, T4130 Employers' Guide – Taxable Benefits and Allowances, checked 24 Nov 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What bike-to-work supports should I ask for?

Secure bike parking, showers and lockers, flexible start times and winter flexibility.

Is a bike allowance taxable in Canada?

CRA says allowances are generally taxable unless a specific exception applies. Ask payroll.

How do I pitch bike parking to HR?

Survey coworkers, compare its cost to car parking and suggest a short trial.

How much can biking to work save?

In Toronto, up to the $155.10 monthly TTC cap, minus your bike costs.

What about winter?

Ask for flexibility to switch to transit or work from home on bad days.
