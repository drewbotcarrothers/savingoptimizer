Festival Tickets: TIFF and Comedy Festivals

Rush lines, member presales and free shows · About 8 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

TIFF 2026 regular screenings started at $30, or $28 for members during the 21 to 27 August presale, with regular rush tickets at $32, premium screenings from $46 and select screenings from $15. TIFF also offered an Under-25 Free Pass. Comedy festivals like Just for Laughs Montréal mix ticketed shows with free outdoor ones. The cheapest plan is to pick a few screenings or shows, use free and youth options, and skip premium galas unless the event matters to you.

Key takeaways

  • TIFF 2026: regular from $30, member presale from $28, rush $32, premium from $46, select screenings from $15 (TIFF, checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • TIFF's Under-25 Free Pass went on sale 28 August 2026; public sales opened 31 August.
  • A TIFF membership pays off only if presale access and $2 savings cover its cost for you.
  • Just for Laughs Montréal 2026 ran 15 to 26 July with free outdoor shows.
  • Example: four regular TIFF screenings cost $120; swapping two for $15 screenings cuts it to $90.

Rush tickets

Rush tickets are released for sold-out screenings shortly before showtime, if seats become available. They're not guaranteed and cost $32 for regular screenings in 2026. Choose screenings with several showings, weekday daytime slots, or films with distribution already announced, which will likely come to theatres anyway.

Comedy festivals

Just for Laughs Montréal 2026 ran from 15 to 26 July with free outdoor shows alongside ticketed galas and club shows. Smaller comedy festivals across Canada run club shows at lower prices. For year-round comedy, see our cheap comedy tickets guide.

Plan: an example TIFF budget

You plan four regular screenings at $30 each. Option 2: two regular screenings and two $15 screenings.

Four regular: 4 × $30 = $120. Mixed: 2 × $30 + 2 × $15 = $90. Add transit and food, and remember TIFF's Festival Street is free. Compare paid festival passes with our early bird festival pass guide.

Frequently asked questions

How much are TIFF tickets?

For TIFF 2026, regular screenings started at $30, $28 for members in presale, rush was $32, premium screenings started at $46, and select screenings started at $15.

What is a TIFF rush ticket?

A ticket released for a sold-out screening shortly before showtime, if seats become available. In 2026, regular rush tickets were $32.

Is there a free TIFF pass for young people?

TIFF offered an Under-25 Free Pass in 2026, on sale from 28 August. Check TIFF's site for 2027 details.

Is Just for Laughs free?

Some of it. Just for Laughs Montréal 2026 had free outdoor shows alongside ticketed shows.

Is a TIFF membership worth it for the festival?

Only if the presale access and member savings are worth more to you than the membership cost. Compare before you join.
