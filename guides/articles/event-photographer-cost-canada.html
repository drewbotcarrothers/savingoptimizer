Event Photographers: A Canadian Cost Guide

Pay for the hours that matter · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Event photographers usually charge by the hour or by package, often with a two- or three-hour minimum, and the price includes editing time as well as shooting time. To spend less, book only the key hours, such as speeches and cake, consider a student or emerging photographer with a solid portfolio, and use a shared guest album for candid shots.

Key takeaways

  • Pricing is hourly or by package, often with a minimum booking.
  • The price covers editing and delivery, not just time at the event.
  • Book the key hours only: arrivals, speeches, cake and group photos.
  • Student and emerging photographers can cost less. Review full galleries, not just highlights.
  • Example: two hours from a student costs $300; four hours from an experienced photographer costs $1,000.

What's included

  • Shooting time at the event.
  • Editing and an online gallery.
  • A personal use licence for the photos.
  • Delivery by a set date; ask how long it takes.
  • Extras: prints, albums, rush delivery, travel, a second shooter.

Cheaper options

  • Book two hours around the key moments.
  • Hire a photography student or emerging photographer.
  • Ask a friend with a good camera, and give them a shot list.
  • Set up a shared album for guests' phone photos.
  • For weddings, see wedding photographer costs.

Contracts

Get in writing: hours, number of edited images or an estimate, delivery date, deposit and cancellation terms, overtime rates, and what you can do with the photos. Pay the deposit by a method that leaves a record.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an event photographer cost in Canada?

It depends on experience, hours and city. Many charge hourly with a minimum booking, or by package. Get quotes with editing and delivery included.

How many hours of event photography do I need?

Often two hours covers key moments like speeches, cake and group photos for a party.

Are student photographers good?

Many are. Review full galleries from similar events and get a contract.

Do I own the photos?

Usually the photographer keeps copyright and gives you a personal use licence. Check the contract.

How long does it take to get event photos?

It varies. Ask for a delivery date in the contract.
