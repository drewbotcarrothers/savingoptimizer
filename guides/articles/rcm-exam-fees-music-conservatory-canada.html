Music Exam and Conservatory Fees: RCM Costs and Credits

The exam fee is the small part; lessons are the big one · About 8 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Royal Conservatory of Music exams in Canada cost $75 to $322 for practical exams up to Level 9, and theory exams from Level 5 cost $149 to $187 or more. A Level 8 practical exam is $302 and the Level 8 theory exam is $187. The exam fees are small next to weekly lessons, which usually make up most of a year's music budget. In some provinces an RCM certificate can count toward high school credits, which adds value to the exam fee.

Key takeaways

  • RCM's Canadian practical exam fees, checked 24 Nov 2026: Preparatory $75, Level 1 $118, Level 5 $210, Level 8 $302, Level 9 $322.
  • Theory exams: Level 5 $149, Level 6 $159, Level 7 $170, Level 8 $187.
  • From Level 5, a certificate needs both the practical exam and the matching theory co-requisites.
  • Some provinces grant high school credit for RCM certificates. Ask your school which levels count.
  • Example: a Level 8 year costs about $3,089 with weekly lessons and books, or $489 in exam fees alone.

Exam fees

The RCM publishes separate fee pages for Canada and the US. The Canadian page lists practical exam fees that rise with the level: $75 for Preparatory, Preparatory A and B, $118 for Level 1, $144 for Level 2, $175 for Level 3, $186 for Level 4, $210 for Level 5, $226 for Level 6, $240 for Level 7, $302 for Level 8 and $322 for Level 9. Fees for Level 10 and the ARCT diplomas are higher; check the RCM fee page for the current amounts.

Theory exams

From Level 5, an RCM certificate requires the practical exam plus theory co-requisites. That roughly doubles the exam budget at the higher levels. Theory can be studied with the same teacher, in a group class or through online courses, and some students write theory exams in a different session from their practical exam to spread the cost. The exam session you choose does not change the fee, but late registration may carry an extra charge, so book early.

High school credit

Several provinces recognize RCM certificates for high school credit. In Ontario, external music credentials from the RCM can be counted toward the diploma when a student has the required practical and theory certificates; other provinces such as British Columbia and Alberta have their own rules. The exact levels and the paperwork vary, so ask the school's guidance office before the student books an exam they are counting on for a credit. A credit earned this way can free up a timetable slot.

Teachers

Lessons are the real cost. Private teachers in Canadian cities commonly charge by the half hour or hour, and longer lessons become necessary at higher levels. Ways to save:

  • Ask about a yearly or term rate, which is often lower than paying per lesson.
  • Try group theory classes instead of private theory lessons.
  • Check community music schools and university preparatory programs, which may offer bursaries.
  • Rent or buy a used instrument; for piano, a good used acoustic or digital piano costs less than a new one.
  • Book the exam only when the teacher thinks the student is ready. A failed attempt means paying again.

Budget: an example Level 8 year

An example Level 8 piano student takes 36 weekly 45-minute lessons at an example $70 each, writes the Level 8 practical and theory exams, and buys books.

Lessons: 36 × $70 = $2,520. Exams: $302 + $187 = $489. Books: an example $80. Total about $3,089. The exam fees are about 16% of the year. If a student drops to 30 lessons, the year falls by $420, more than the theory exam fee. If the student gets a high school credit for the certificate, the exam fees also buy a credit.

Frequently asked questions

How much is an RCM exam in Canada?

On 24 Nov 2026, RCM's Canadian fee page listed practical exams from $75 (Preparatory) to $322 (Level 9), including $210 for Level 5 and $302 for Level 8. Level 10 and diplomas cost more.

How much do RCM theory exams cost?

Level 5 Theory is $149, Level 6 Theory $159, Level 7 Theory $170 and Level 8 Theory $187. Higher-level harmony and history exams cost more.

Do I need theory exams for an RCM certificate?

Yes, from Level 5 a certificate needs the practical exam plus the matching theory co-requisites.

Can RCM exams count for high school credit?

In several provinces, including Ontario, RCM certificates can count toward the high school diploma. Ask the school's guidance office which levels and paperwork are needed.

What is the biggest cost of RCM music study?

Lessons. In the example on this page, 36 weekly lessons cost $2,520, compared with $489 for the Level 8 practical and theory exams.
