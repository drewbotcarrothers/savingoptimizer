Cheap CFL Tickets: A Canadian Cost Guide

Pick the right game, the right section and the right day · About 7 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

CFL tickets are cheapest for early-season, weeknight and non-rivalry games, in upper or end-zone sections. Family, group and flex packs lower the price per game, and season seats cost the least per game if you'll go often; the Toronto Argonauts took $50-per-seat deposits for 2027 season seats. Compare all-in prices with fees, and buy resale only on verified platforms.

Key takeaways

  • Early-season, weeknight and non-rivalry games usually cost less than Labour Day and playoff games.
  • End-zone and upper sections are the cheapest seats.
  • Family, group and flex packs cut the per-game price.
  • The Argos took a $50-per-seat deposit for 2027 season seats (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Example: a family of four pays $160 in end-zone seats against $360 at midfield.

When to buy

  • Buy early for big games: Labour Day, rivalry and playoff games.
  • For low-demand games, prices on verified resale may drop close to game day.
  • Watch for team promotions and family days.
  • Sign up for team newsletters for presales.

Packs and season seats

Flex packs give vouchers you can use across games. Season seats cost the least per game but tie you to a schedule. Deposits lock priority. Check what's included: playoff games, concession discounts, parking.

Frequently asked questions

When are CFL tickets cheapest?

Usually for early-season, weeknight and non-rivalry games, in end-zone or upper sections.

Are CFL season tickets worth it?

If you'll go to most home games, season seats usually cost the least per game. Flex packs suit occasional fans.

Is it safe to buy CFL tickets on resale?

Use the team's official resale partner or a verified platform, and avoid social media sellers.

Do CFL tickets include fees?

Compare the all-in price including fees and taxes before you buy.

How do I save on a CFL game day?

Choose cheaper sections, eat before, take transit and look for family packs.
