How B.C. drivers can still cut auto costs inside the ICBC system

Basic vs optional · odometer discounts · About 16 minutes · 20 Sep 2026

B.C. drivers hear “ICBC” and stop shopping. Basic Autoplan is public and mandatory. That is not the same sentence as “there is nothing to decide.” You still choose how the vehicle is rated, whether you buy optional Collision, Comprehensive, and extra liability, whether you qualify for distance discounts, and whether you are paying for add-ons you never use. Ontario broker-vs-direct diagrams do not apply here.

Educational only — not a quote and not a broker. Official tools live on icbc.com. For private-market provinces see broker vs direct.

Disclosure: Optional auto-coverage quote tools, where they exist for B.C., are an offer type. Saving Optimizer may earn a commission if we later add partner links. We are not an Autoplan broker or insurer. Basic ICBC coverage is a public product; we do not sell it. Confirm numbers with a licensed Autoplan broker and icbc.com.

Key takeaways

  • Basic Autoplan (mandatory) includes Enhanced Care accident benefits, $200,000 third-party liability, and Basic Vehicle Damage Coverage up to $200,000 when the other driver is responsible. It does not pay to fix your car when you are at fault — that is optional Collision.
  • Optional Autoplan: extended liability, Collision, Comprehensive (theft, vandalism, weather, glass), Specified Perils, packages such as RoadStar. This is the shop — coverage you need versus add-ons you do not.
  • Distance: 10% off Basic if you drive under 5,000 km/year; about 10–15% off eligible Optional if under 15,000 km on annual policies (ICBC expansion for renewals from 1 Jun 2025). Two odometer readings a year apart.
  • Rate class, listed drivers, and garage address are levers you control by telling the truth. Winter-tire insurance discounts are an Ontario-style product — do not assume ICBC matches FSRA’s mandatory offer.
  • Moving to or from B.C. resets everything. Do not port an Ontario bundle into Autoplan in your head.

What is public vs optional coverage in B.C.

ICBC Basic (icbc.com, Basic insurance and Enhanced Care) is what every plated vehicle needs. It is sold through Autoplan brokers, not “whichever direct writer emailed you.” Optional cover is extra, sold at the same broker visit, and is how a financed SUV stays legal with the lender. If you skip Collision on a $3,000 beater you can afford to replace, that can be rational — same logic FSRA uses in Ontario for cheap cars, different paperwork.

Plain-English map. Confirm the current Autoplan brochure before you sign. Not a policy wording.
Layer Pays for Your decision
Basic Autoplan Injury benefits; $200k TPL; not-at-fault vehicle damage (limits apply) Mandatory. Rate it honestly.
Extended TPL Higher liability where lawsuits still exist (out of province, some property) Ask a broker what limit matches your assets.
Collision Your car when you are at fault (minus deductible) Skip only if you can replace the car in cash.
Comprehensive Theft, vandalism, fire, weather, glass Street-parked in Vancouver ≠ a garage in Kamloops.

Basic TPL at $200,000 is a legal floor, not a lifestyle. Many households buy $1–2 million extended liability after a broker conversation about out-of-province driving and assets. That is a risk talk, not a slogan on this page.

Distance and vehicle factors you control

ICBC prices how the vehicle is used (rate class) and who drives it most. Pleasure versus commute, business use, and listed drivers are facts. Changing them in your head after a claim is how you have a bad year. Distance discounts (icbc.com/insurance/costs/distance-based-discount):

  • Under 5,000 km in 12 months: you may get 10% off Basic.
  • Under 15,000 km: you may get roughly 10–15% off eligible Optional (extended TPL, Collision, Comprehensive, Specified Perils) on annual policies. Motorcycles, collectors, and some classes are excluded because the rate class already assumes low use.
  • Proof is two odometer readings about a year apart (and readings on both vehicles if you transfer mid-term).

A hybrid office week in Burnaby can be a 5,000–15,000 km life. Take the photos. Do not guess 4,900 km if the dash says 18,000.

Optional coverage shopping without over-insuring

Walk into the broker appointment with a sheet:

  • Cash value of the car (what you would actually spend to replace it).
  • Deductible you can pay tomorrow without a credit card ($500 vs $1,000 is the usual conversation).
  • Where it sleeps (garage, street, underground with broken gate).
  • Glass and theft history in your neighbourhood — Comprehensive exists for a reason on the Drive.
  • RoadStar / roadside / loss-of-use: price them as line items. Your credit card may already tow 10 km. See CAA vs card roadside.

Lenders and lessors will force Collision and Comprehensive. That is a contract, not ICBC being greedy. If the car is owned outright and worth less than a couple of deductibles plus your patience, ask the broker what Basic-only looks like in dollars and in risk. Do not drop Collision on a financed vehicle because a podcast said so.

Claims and conviction impacts at a high level

Enhanced Care reduced a lot of at-fault injury lawsuits inside B.C. It did not make speeding tickets or at-fault crashes free. Convictions, suspensions, and claims still move what you pay and who will sell you Optional. If your licence is expired or a household driver is unlicensed, you have a bigger problem than a deductible. Tell the broker the truth on the renewal worksheet. This page will not estimate your surcharge — that is a licensed conversation.

Winter tires and safety discounts to ask about

B.C. requires adequate winter tires on many marked highways from October through April. That is a road law, not an Ontario-style “insurer must offer 2–5%” rule. Ask your Autoplan broker what, if anything, ICBC or an optional product still files for winter tires or anti-theft in your rate class this year. Do not paste FSRA’s mandatory winter-tire discount onto a B.C. bill. Buy the tires because the Coquihalla will not negotiate. Ontario owners: how that discount actually works.

When moving provinces changes everything

Arriving in B.C.: you need Autoplan when you plate here. Your Ontario or Alberta claims history still matters; the product map does not. Leaving B.C.: you will shop a private market (or SAAQ + private damage). Cancel and start dates, unused premium, and inspection rules are broker/registry work — start weeks before the moving truck, not at the border. A “I kept Ontario insurance for a month” story is how people drive uninsured.

Resources for official rate tools

  • icbc.com — Basic vs Optional, Enhanced Care, distance-based discount, Autoplan broker locator.
  • Your Autoplan broker — the human who rates the vehicle. Bring odometer photos and the coverage sheet.
  • ICBC Autoplan Insurance Guide (brochure PDF) — TPL $200,000, Collision vs Comprehensive definitions.
  • If someone offers “private B.C. basic,” stop. Basic is ICBC. Optional is where shopping language belongs, and even then it is still usually Autoplan optional sold at the same desk unless a specific non-ICBC optional market applies to your case — ask the broker; do not invent a second basic.

The B.C. save is boring: correct rate class, odometer discounts, optional cover that matches the car you have, no unused package. It is not an Ontario aggregator tab.

Frequently asked questions

Can B.C. drivers shop auto insurance like Ontarians?

Not for Basic Autoplan — that is ICBC and mandatory. You still choose optional Collision, Comprehensive, and extra liability, and you still control rate class, listed drivers, and distance-discount paperwork.

What optional coverage do I actually need?

If a lender requires Collision and Comprehensive, you need them. If you own a cheap car outright and can replace it in cash, ask a broker what Basic-only costs and risks. Extended liability is a conversation about assets and out-of-province driving, not a blog default.

How do ICBC distance discounts work?

Submit odometer readings a year apart. Under 5,000 km you may get 10% off Basic. Under 15,000 km you may get about 10–15% off eligible Optional on a 12-month policy. Some vehicle classes are excluded.

Does ICBC give a winter-tire insurance discount?

Do not assume Ontario’s mandatory 2–5% offer exists in B.C. Highway tire rules are a driving law. Ask your Autoplan broker what, if anything, applies to your optional policy this year.

Where do I get official ICBC numbers?

icbc.com and a licensed Autoplan broker. This page is education, not a premium. Rate tools and brochures change — confirm before you decline Collision.
