Scene+ Points for Movies: How to Get the Most From Every Point in Canada

At Cineplex, 1,000 points is $10 off; spend them where that rate holds · About 10 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 1 Oct 2026

To get the most from Scene+ points at the movies, redeem them at Cineplex, where 100 points take $1 off movie tickets, food or drinks, and earn them at the same time, since tickets and snacks earn 3 to 6 points per dollar. Points cannot pay the tax, a tip or alcohol, so the cleanest use is the pre-tax ticket price. Book on Tuesdays, when Cineplex runs discounted pricing and Scene+ members save an extra 10%, and watch for bonus-point promotions on premium screens. Movie redemptions are worth the same as groceries at Sobeys and travel through Scotiabank, and more than Scotiabank's Points for Credit at 150 points per dollar. Use the account at least once every 24 months, or the points are forfeited.

Key takeaways

  • Earn 3 to 6 Scene+ points per $1 on Cineplex tickets and snacks (Scene+).
  • Redeem 100 points for $1 off tickets, food or drinks.
  • Points cannot pay taxes, tips or alcohol.
  • Tuesdays: discounted pricing plus an extra 10% for Scene+ members (Cineplex).
  • Points for Credit is weaker: 150 points per $1 (Scotiabank).
  • Registered accounts close after 24 months of inactivity.

How you earn at Cineplex

The Scene+ help centre says you earn 3 to 6 points for every $1 spent on eligible movie tickets and concession purchases at Cineplex theatres, including VIP Cinemas and concession brands such as Outtakes, YoYo's, Pizza Pizza, Poptopia and Starbucks where available. You may earn more when you pay with a qualifying Scotiabank credit card. Points usually appear within 48 hours, though promotional points can take longer. At The Rec Room and Playdium, you earn 1 point per dollar on eligible purchases.

Cineplex also runs limited-time offers. As of 1 October 2026, its deals page lists 5 times the points on movie tickets, which it describes as 30 points per dollar on premium-experience tickets, and 200 bonus points when you buy Combo 1 or Combo 2. Offers change without notice, so check the deals page before you book.

How you redeem for movies

At Cineplex, you can redeem 100 points for $1 toward movie tickets, food or drinks, or 500 points for $5 off other eligible purchases. You can also use points to get Cineplex gift cards from the Rewards section of your Scene+ account. The help centre says redemptions cannot be applied to taxes, gratuities or alcohol. In practice, that means you can cover the ticket and the popcorn, but you will still pay the sales tax and any drinks from the bar. At The Rec Room, 500 points take $5 off food, drinks or game credits, and 1,000 points take $10 off other eligible purchases; at Playdium, 500 points take $5 off eligible purchases.

How movie value compares

A Scene+ point is worth about one cent at Cineplex. The same rate applies at Sobeys and other participating grocery stores, where every 1,000 points equal $10 off, and on travel charged to a linked Scotiabank card, at 100 points per dollar. The weakest option on these pages is Scotiabank's Points for Credit, at 150 points per dollar, which makes a point worth about two-thirds of a cent.

Because Cineplex, groceries and travel pay the same rate, the right choice is whichever you would spend cash on anyway. If you go to the movies regularly, redeeming there is fine. If you rarely go, groceries are just as good and more flexible.

Stacking: Tuesdays, promotions and gift cards

  • Tuesdays: Cineplex's Big Deal Tuesdays offer discounted pricing, and Scene+ members save an extra 10% on Tuesday ticket prices.
  • Bonus points: limited-time multipliers and combo bonuses raise what you earn on the same spend.
  • Pay with a qualifying Scotiabank card if you have one, for extra points.
  • Free refills: Cineplex lists one free refill per visit on a large popcorn or fountain drink, which makes the large size better value if you share.
  • Partner offers: Cineplex's deals page also lists third-party promotions, such as product-purchase offers for a free ticket, with their own dates and terms.

Don't let points expire

Scene+ says registered accounts close after 24 months of inactivity, and unregistered accounts after 6 months. Once an account is closed, you cannot earn or redeem on it, and the points are forfeited. Earning or redeeming regularly is the simplest way to keep an account active, and the Scene+ terms set out exactly what counts as activity, so check them if your balance has been idle for a while. Register your card online if you have not, since unregistered accounts close much sooner.

Example with made-up inputs

These numbers are an example with made-up inputs. A couple sees one movie a month. Each visit, they buy two Tuesday tickets at a made-up $10 each and a $15 popcorn and drink, a $35 pre-tax bill. At a made-up 5 points per dollar, they earn 175 points a visit, or 2,100 a year. Every fifth visit, they redeem 1,500 points to take $15 off the snacks. Over a year they redeem about 3,000 points for $30 off, using points earned at the movies plus some grocery points. If they had used Points for Credit for the same 3,000 points, they would have received $20.

Steps

  1. Register your Scene+ card and link it in the Cineplex app.
  2. Scan or sign in for every ticket and snack purchase.
  3. Go on Tuesdays for the lowest prices plus the Scene+ 10% extra.
  4. Redeem in 100-point steps toward tickets and food, not tax or alcohol.
  5. Check Cineplex's deals page for bonus-point offers before premium shows.
  6. Make at least one earn or redemption before 24 months pass.

Common mistakes

  • Redeeming for statement credit at 150 points per dollar when movies or groceries pay 100.
  • Expecting points to cover the tax or a drink from the bar.
  • Letting an unregistered card sit for months.
  • Paying full price on a weekend when Tuesday works.

Frequently asked questions

How many Scene+ points do I need for a movie?

At Cineplex, 100 points equal $1 off, so a ticket needs 100 points for each dollar of its pre-tax price. Points cannot cover the tax.

How many Scene+ points do I earn at Cineplex?

3 to 6 points per $1 on eligible movie tickets and concessions, and possibly more with a qualifying Scotiabank credit card.

Can Scene+ points pay for alcohol at Cineplex?

No. Scene+ says redemptions cannot be applied to taxes, gratuities or alcohol.

Is it better to use Scene+ points at Cineplex or for groceries?

They are worth the same: 1,000 points equal $10 at Cineplex and at Sobeys. Use them where you would spend cash anyway.

Do Scene+ points expire?

Registered accounts close after 24 months of inactivity and unregistered accounts after 6 months, and the points are forfeited.

Do Scene+ members get a Tuesday discount?

Cineplex says Scene+ members save an extra 10% on its discounted Tuesday ticket prices.
