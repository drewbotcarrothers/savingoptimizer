Soccer Tickets: MLS and CPL

CPL is the cheap seat for live pro soccer · About 7 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Canadian Premier League (CPL) matches are usually the cheapest way to see live pro soccer in Canada, and MLS clubs in Toronto, Montréal and Vancouver cost more. Forge FC in Hamilton, for example, sells season seats from about $29 a match and a 20-ticket Freedom pack you can spread across games. Supporter sections and flex packs lower the price further.

Key takeaways

  • CPL tickets usually cost less than MLS tickets.
  • Forge FC season seats start at about $29 a match, according to a fan guide; group rates run from $27 to $80 (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Flex packs, like Forge's 20-ticket Freedom pack, let you use tickets across games or all at once.
  • Supporter sections are often cheaper, and standing.
  • Example: two people going to 10 CPL matches spend $580 with season seats versus $800 buying singles.

Ways to pay less

  • Buy flex or season packs if you'll go often.
  • Choose supporter or end sections.
  • Look for family and group rates.
  • Check midweek and early-season matches.
  • Compare all-in prices with fees; see our ticket fees guide.

Plan: an example

Two people plan 10 CPL home matches. Season seats at about $29 a match: 2 × 10 × $29 = $580. Single tickets at an example $40: 2 × 10 × $40 = $800.

Season seats save $220 in this example. For other pro sports, see cheap CFL tickets.

Frequently asked questions

Are CPL tickets cheaper than MLS?

Usually yes. CPL clubs generally charge less than Toronto FC, CF Montréal and the Whitecaps.

How much are Forge FC tickets?

A fan guide lists season seats from about $29 a match and group rates from $27 to $80. Single-match prices are set game by game.

What is a flex pack?

A bundle of ticket vouchers you can use across matches. Forge FC's 20-ticket Freedom pack is one example.

Are supporter sections cheaper?

Often, yes, and usually standing with chanting fans.

How do I avoid ticket scams?

Buy from the club or its official ticketing partner.
