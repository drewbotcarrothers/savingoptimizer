Group Outings: Escape Rooms and Axe Throwing

Book the right group size and the right day · About 7 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Escape rooms and axe throwing are usually priced per person, so the cost depends on group size, day and length of session. BATL Grounds, for example, listed 90 minutes of axe and knife throwing at $39.99 a person for groups of 2 to 6 in its July 2026 group deck. Escape Manor's booking page notes discounted pricing on select days. Book off-peak, check the minimum group size, and decide on food and drinks before you go.

Key takeaways

  • Example price: BATL listed 90 minutes of axe and knife throwing at $39.99 per person for 2 to 6 guests (July 2026 deck, checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Escape rooms usually charge per person and may have a minimum group size or a private-room price.
  • Weekday and off-peak bookings are often cheaper. Escape Manor shows discounted pricing on select days.
  • Food, drinks, taxes and tips can add a lot. Agree a budget before you book.
  • Example: six friends spend about $45 each with tax, or $60 or more with drinks and food.

Group size

Escape rooms often have a minimum number of players and may charge a flat price for a private room. Too few players can cost more per person; too many can mean some people watch. Check the recommended number for the room. Axe throwing usually books by lane with a per-person price, so an extra friend adds roughly one more ticket.

Ways to pay less

  • Book a weekday or early slot.
  • Look for student, birthday or group packages, and check what's included.
  • Eat before, or share food. Drinks raise the bill fast.
  • Watch for gift cards or offers on group-deal sites, and read the fine print.
  • Split the cost with a payment app or have one person book and everyone pay back before the day.

Tipping and extras

Some venues add an automatic gratuity for larger groups, especially when food is served. Check the booking terms for tips, booking deposits and cancellation fees. Photo packages, merchandise and extra game time cost extra.

Plan: an example group of six

Six friends book 90 minutes of axe throwing at $39.99 each. With 13% HST in Ontario, that's about $45.19 each. They add an example $15 a person for a drink and a shared snack.

Activity only: 6 × $45.19 is about $271. With food and drinks: about $60.19 each, or about $361 total. A weekday discount or eating before can bring it down. For other cheap group nights, see our bowling, karaoke and trivia guide and cheap hobbies for adults.

Frequently asked questions

How much does axe throwing cost in Canada?

It varies by venue and session. BATL Grounds listed 90 minutes of axe and knife throwing at $39.99 per person for 2 to 6 guests in its July 2026 group deck.

How much do escape rooms cost per person?

Escape rooms set their own per-person prices, often with a minimum group size or a private-room price. Weekday or off-peak slots may be cheaper.

How many people do you need for an escape room?

It depends on the room. Check the venue's recommended and minimum number of players before booking.

Are there cheaper days for escape rooms?

Some venues, such as Escape Manor, show discounted pricing on select days. Weekday and early slots are often cheaper.

Do I need to tip at an axe throwing venue?

Check the booking terms. Some venues add an automatic gratuity for groups, especially with food service.
