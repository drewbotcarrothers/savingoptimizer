Is Cineplex CineClub Worth It in 2026? CineClub vs Paying with Scene+ in Canada

Count the movies you actually see, then do the math · About 10 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 1 Oct 2026

Cineplex CineClub is worth it in Canada if you see at least one movie a month at Cineplex and would otherwise pay the regular ticket price, as of 1 October 2026. Since 15 July 2026, CineClub costs $10.99 a month or $120.89 a year, and it includes one monthly ticket credit, extra tickets at $10.99, 20% off food and drinks and no online booking fees, according to Cineplex. If you go less than once a month, or only on discount days, paying per ticket and collecting Scene+ points is usually cheaper. The July changes also replaced the old 50 bonus points per redeemed credit with the standard Scene+ earn rate of 3 points per $1 on regular tickets and 6 points per $1 on premium tickets.

Key takeaways

  • Price from 15 July 2026: $10.99 a month or $120.89 a year (the annual price works out to one month free).
  • Includes a monthly ticket credit, extra Everyday Member-Priced Tickets at $10.99, 20% off food and drinks and no online booking fees.
  • Scene+ earn: 3 points per $1 on regular tickets and 6 per $1 on premium; the 50 points per credit bonus ended.
  • Break-even: roughly one full-price movie a month, especially if you buy online or buy snacks.
  • Existing annual and gifted members keep $9.99 member tickets until their next renewal.
  • Example with made-up inputs: three moviegoers, three different answers.

What changed on 15 July 2026

Cineplex raised the CineClub price for the first time since the program launched in 2021 at $9.99 a month. It also changed how members earn Scene+ points and clarified how ticket benefits apply to Event Cinema. Cineplex said there are no changes to the other benefits, including 20% off food and drinks and no online booking fees.

How CineClub works

Each month of membership comes with a ticket credit you can use for a regular showing. If you want a premium format, you can use a credit and pay the difference. You can buy additional tickets for yourself or friends at the Everyday Member-Priced Ticket rate. Members pay no online booking fee and get 20% off food and drinks at Cineplex concessions. Some movies, events and special presentations can carry restrictions, so check the details at checkout.

The break-even math

Regular ticket prices at Cineplex vary by city, theatre, format and time of day, so there is no single national price. To work out your own break-even, look up the regular adult price at the theatre you use most and compare it with what you would pay as a member.

  1. Find your usual regular ticket price before tax, plus the online booking fee if you buy online.
  2. Count how many movies you realistically see at Cineplex each month, using the last six months as a guide.
  3. Add what you usually spend on food and drinks per visit.
  4. Compare: without CineClub, tickets plus fees plus snacks; with CineClub, $10.99 a month plus extra member tickets plus snacks at 20% off.
  5. If the CineClub total is lower in a typical month, it is worth it for you.

Annual or monthly?

The annual membership at $120.89 equals 11 monthly payments, so you effectively get one month free. That is worth it only if you are sure you will keep going all year. Monthly membership lets you cancel in a slow month, or join only for a summer of blockbusters and cancel in the fall. Cineplex lets you cancel monthly membership, and you keep using it until the end of the paid period; check the current terms before joining.

Scene+ points: how much they are worth

As of 1 Oct 2026, Cineplex's Scene+ page says you can redeem 100 points for $1 toward movie tickets, food and drinks at Cineplex, with no minimum spend, which works out to 1 cent a point at Cineplex. At 3 points per $1 on a regular ticket, a $15 ticket earns 45 points, worth about 45 cents. Points are a small bonus; they should not decide whether CineClub is worth it. You can earn Scene+ points with or without CineClub, and you can link a Scene+ account to other partners to earn faster.

Cheaper ways to see movies

  • Discount days and early showings, where offered, can cost less than a regular ticket.
  • Redeeming Scene+ points for tickets.
  • Gift cards bought on sale during the holidays.
  • Waiting for the movie to reach a streaming service you already pay for. See rotating streaming services.
  • Community and repertory cinemas, which often have lower prices and memberships of their own.

If you already pay for several streaming services, CineClub is one more subscription. Review it with the others; the subscription audit guide shows how. And for live events, where fees work differently, see concert ticket fees and all-in pricing.

A decision checklist

  1. Count the Cineplex movies you saw in the last six months.
  2. Look up your local regular ticket price and booking fee.
  3. Decide whether snacks are part of your usual visit.
  4. Run the break-even with your own numbers.
  5. Start monthly; switch to annual only if you keep going every month.
  6. Set a reminder to review before each renewal.

Common mistakes

  • Joining for one big release and forgetting to cancel.
  • Paying annually, then going only a few times a year.
  • Counting Scene+ points as a major saving; Cineplex redeems them at 100 points for $1.
  • Forgetting that premium formats with a credit still cost extra.
  • Not checking restrictions on special presentations.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Cineplex CineClub cost in 2026?

From 15 July 2026, CineClub costs $10.99 a month or $120.89 a year, up from $9.99 and $109.89, according to Cineplex.

Is CineClub worth it?

Usually if you see at least one movie a month at Cineplex at the regular price, especially if you buy online or buy snacks. If you go less often or only on discount days, paying per ticket is often cheaper.

How many Scene+ points do CineClub members earn?

Since 15 July 2026, members earn the standard Scene+ rate: 3 points per $1 on regular movie tickets and 6 points per $1 on premium tickets. The old 50 points per redeemed credit ended.

What else does CineClub include?

A monthly ticket credit, extra tickets at the $10.99 Everyday Member-Priced Ticket rate, 20% off food and drinks and no online booking fees, as of 1 Oct 2026.
