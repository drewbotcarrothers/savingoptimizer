Is Checkout 51 Still Worth It for Canadian Grocery Receipts?

Worth a minute a week if you already buy the products on offer · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Checkout 51 is still worth it if you claim offers on things you'd buy anyway. It's free, works with receipts from almost any store, and pays cash once your balance reaches $20. The offers are for specific products and change every week, so the payoff depends on whether they match your list. Don't buy something just for the cash back.

Key takeaways

  • Free receipt-upload cash back on specific products (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Canadian offers run Thursday 12 a.m. to Wednesday 11:59 p.m.
  • Upload the receipt while the offer is live; offers can run out.
  • Cash out once your balance reaches $20.
  • Example: $0.50 to $1.50 a week is $26 to $78 a year.

How it works

  • Check the offers before you shop.
  • Buy the exact product listed, at any eligible store.
  • Upload a photo of the receipt while the offer is live.
  • Checkout 51 confirms the purchase and credits your account.
  • Request a cash out once you reach $20.

Is it worth the time?

Example yearly Checkout 51 earnings. As of 24 Nov 2026.

Tips

  • Scan the offers on Thursday, alongside the new flyers.
  • Stack it: a flyer sale, a price match, store points and a Checkout 51 offer can all apply to one item.
  • Upload right after you shop so you don't miss the deadline.
  • Skip offers on things you wouldn't normally buy.

Frequently asked questions

Is Checkout 51 still available in Canada?

Yes. It was operating in Canada when checked on 24 Nov 2026.

What is the minimum cash out on Checkout 51?

$20.

When do Checkout 51 offers change?

In Canada, offers go live Thursdays at 12 a.m. and expire the following Wednesday at 11:59 p.m.

Can I use any store's receipt?

Generally yes, as long as it shows the exact product and the store is eligible. Receipts must be in English or French.

How much can you make with Checkout 51?

It depends on the offers. In this example, $26 to $78 a year.
