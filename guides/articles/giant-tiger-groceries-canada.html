Should You Add Giant Tiger to Your Canadian Grocery Rotation?

Use the 1-cent ad match on the staples it carries, and skip the rest · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Giant Tiger is worth a spot in your rotation if one is close by, especially in smaller towns. Its Ad Match Guarantee sells an identical item for 1 cent less than a local competitor's flyer price, so you can buy name-brand staples, household basics and some groceries in one stop. It doesn't match private label or loose produce, and its fresh selection is smaller than a supermarket's.

Key takeaways

  • Ad Match Guarantee: 1 cent below a local competitor's advertised price on identical in-stock items (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Not matched: private label, loyalty prices, online offers, multi-buys, clearance and loose produce by weight.
  • Most paper flyers arrive on Wednesdays.
  • Rain checks are available on most advertised items that sell out.
  • Example: an $85 basket drops to $78 with matches.

How the ad match works

  • Show the competitor's current print or digital flyer at the till.
  • The item must be identical: same brand, size and variety.
  • Produce must match brand or farm, weight and country of origin; only bagged or packaged produce counts.
  • The item must be in stock at that store, and quantities can be limited.
  • Online orders: contact customer service with your order number and the competitor's link.

When it fits

Giant Tiger fits best as a second store for name-brand pantry items, snacks, cleaning supplies and basics, especially where the nearest discount supermarket is a drive away. Buy fresh meat and produce where the selection is better.

Frequently asked questions

Does Giant Tiger price match?

Yes. Its Ad Match Guarantee beats a local competitor's current advertised price on an identical item by 1 cent.

Does Giant Tiger match produce?

Only packaged or bagged produce that matches on brand or farm, weight and origin. Loose produce by weight isn't matched.

Does Giant Tiger match store brands?

No. Private label products are excluded.

When does the Giant Tiger flyer come out?

Most paper flyers are distributed on Wednesdays, and the digital flyer is on its website.

Does Giant Tiger give rain checks?

Yes, on most advertised items, unless a similar item is available at a comparable price. Not on clearance or limited-quantity items.
