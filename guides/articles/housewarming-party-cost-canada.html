Housewarming Parties: A Canadian Cost Guide

Celebrate the new place without a moving-sized bill · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

A housewarming party can be cheap: an afternoon open house with snacks, a few drinks and borrowed glasses costs far less than a dinner party. Moving is already expensive, so wait until you're settled, invite guests to drop in over a few hours, and say if it's potluck or BYOB. Guests usually bring a small gift, such as a plant, food or a drink.

Key takeaways

  • An open house with snacks costs less than a sit-down meal and spreads guests over several hours.
  • Potluck and BYOB are common for housewarmings. Say so on the invitation.
  • Borrow chairs, glasses and coolers from friends instead of buying or renting.
  • Guests often bring a small gift, about $15 to $30, or a practical item.
  • Example: an open house for 25 guests costs $150, or $6 a head.

Gifts for guests

  • A plant, flowers or herbs.
  • Food: baked goods, local coffee, a bottle of wine.
  • Practical: a tool, a doorstop, kitchen towels.
  • A gift card for a hardware or home store.
  • No gift is needed if the invitation says so.

Timing

Wait until boxes are unpacked and furniture is in place, often a month or two after the move. Moving costs, closing costs and new furniture add up, so don't overspend on the party. If you bought, our hosting guide has more ideas for hosting on a budget.

Plan: an example

A couple hosts a three-hour open house for 25 guests with snacks, a few drinks and borrowed glasses. They spend $150.

Open house: $150 ÷ 25 = $6 a head. Sit-down dinner: $550 ÷ 25 = $22 a head. The open house saves $400.

Sources

  • Party costs vary by city and store; amounts in this guide are examples, checked 24 Nov 2026.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a housewarming party cost?

It depends on the format. In our example, an open house for 25 guests costs about $150; a sit-down dinner costs about $550.

How much should I spend on a housewarming gift?

A small gift of about $15 to $30 is common, such as a plant, food or a drink. Follow the host's wishes.

When should you have a housewarming party?

Once you've unpacked and settled, often a month or two after moving.

Is it OK to ask guests to bring food or drinks?

Yes. Potluck and BYOB are common. Say so on the invitation.

Do you have to bring a gift to a housewarming?

It's customary but not required. If the invitation says no gifts, follow it.
