How to find smart thermostat rebates from Canadian utilities (before you buy)

Eligibility, then the model list, then the receipt · About 14 minutes · 20 Sep 2026

Households buy a $250 thermostat on a Saturday, then discover on Monday that the $100 utility code needed a pre-approved model and a receipt uploaded within 60 days. The rebate was never a till coupon. Buy-order is the whole game: confirm the live list, get the code or the application window, then spend.

This is Canadian utility rebate sequencing — not a U.S. “up to $850” brochure. Whether the device is even worth buying sits in the savings worksheet. Ontario’s demand-response card is a different pot of money — Peak Perks.

Disclosure: Smart thermostats are an offer type (Home Renovation Savings lists ecobee, Nest, Sensi, Mysa, and others). Saving Optimizer may earn a commission if we later add partner links. We do not currently claim thermostat-brand, Enbridge, or IESO partnerships. Amounts and eligible models change — re-check the live program page before you buy.

Key takeaways

  • Buy-order: eligibility → model list → instant code or post-purchase window → install and Wi-Fi → then any demand-response enrolment. Reverse that and you donate the rebate.
  • Ontario Home Renovation Savings (Enbridge + Save on Energy, used 20 Sep 2026): $100 instant code or post-purchase rebate on a qualified smart thermostat (EAP kit path can show $125). Purchase / activate by 31 Dec 2026 on the terms we read. One per household. Cornwall Electric excluded.
  • Gas-heat Enbridge customers and electric-heat / electric-cool IESO-grid customers can both qualify under that stream — read the current “how to qualify” bullets, not a 2024 blog.
  • Peak Perks $75 + $20 is demand response, not a till discount. You can often stack a purchase rebate and a DR credit if both terms allow it — confirm in writing; do not stack stories in your head.
  • No rebate? Still run the 5–10% schedule worksheet. A $40 programmable that you use beats a $250 smart stat you leave on Hold.

Why buy-order matters: many rebates need pre-approval or specific models

Utility rebates are not manufacturer mail-in coupons. They are program rules: a SKU list, a fuel type, a dwelling type, a date window, and proof. Instant-code paths (Home Renovation Savings “Get code”) expire and only work at named retailers. Post-purchase paths want a receipt within a stated number of days (60 days on the Ontario terms we used). Buy an ineligible colourway or a line-voltage model on a 24 V list and the portal will say no.

Photograph the serial, keep the invoice with tax and model number, and do not throw out the box until the credit posts.

Save on Energy and provincial efficiency portals

Start pages, then confirm the child page the week you shop:

  • Ontario: saveonenergy.ca and homerenovationsavings.ca — smart thermostat as a single upgrade (no EnerGuide assessment required on that stream). Bundled HRSP insulation / heat-pump offers are a different application.
  • B.C.: CleanBC Better Homes and BC Hydro / FortisBC offer directories. Thermostat money, if any, is often bundled with a heat pump or a Peak Saver device path — not assumed.
  • Québec: Hydro-Québec Energy Wise and any current LogisVert / provincial list. Hilo hardware is a service subscription, not a $100 till code.
  • Alberta and the rest: municipal and retailer pilots appear and vanish. Use the NRCan home-program directory as a map, then open the utility URL.

Federal Greener Homes is closed to new applicants. Do not subtract a dead grant from a 2026 quote.

HRSP eligibility on the pages we used: Enbridge Gas customer with an active account heating primarily with a gas furnace or boiler, or connected to the Ontario electricity grid and heating or cooling electrically; single / semi / row / town / mobile on a permanent foundation; have not already taken a smart-thermostat rebate from Enbridge, Save on Energy, or HRSP. Instant and post-purchase streams cannot be combined. Offer end date on the terms PDF: 31 Dec 2026.

Stacking manufacturer discounts carefully

A Best Buy or Home Depot sale plus an HRSP instant code is the intended stack when the portal says those retailers. A manufacturer mail-in plus a utility post-purchase rebate can be allowed or forbidden — the utility terms win. Do not apply twice (instant code and post-purchase) for the same household. Do not use a code on an ineligible SKU and expect a courtesy override.

Line-voltage baseboard stats (Mysa and peers) appear on some HRSP lists and not on Peak Perks. Buy the voltage you have — the line-voltage guide — then check the rebate list, not the other way around.

Proof-of-purchase and installation photo requirements

Expect some mix of: dated retail receipt, model and serial, account number, installation address, and proof the device is online. Instant-code paths still want you to install and connect; a stat in a closet is how programs claw back or refuse a later DR bonus. Keep PDFs. If the credit is an Enbridge bill credit vs an e-transfer for electric customers, the portal will say which. Phishing exists — start from the official hostname, not an email “confirm your rebate” link you did not expect.

If no rebate: still run the savings worksheet

Hydro-Québec: electronic vs dial can cut about 10% of annual heating cost. A well-set programmable already has most of a smart schedule. Smart extras (remote setback, heat-pump lockouts, DR) have to earn the extra $100–$200. Worksheet: last year’s heating dollars × 5–10% only if you will gain a real schedule, plus any live rebate, divided into net device cost. Seal drafts first — air sealing.

If the portal is closed or your dwelling type is a high-rise suite the program excludes, skip the hunt and buy (or do not buy) on the worksheet alone.

Frequently asked questions

Should I buy the thermostat first and apply later?

Only if the program is explicitly post-purchase and your model is on the live list. Instant-code paths need the code at checkout. Many portals want the receipt within 60 days.

How much is the Ontario utility rebate right now?

Home Renovation Savings advertised $100 (instant code or post-purchase) on terms used 20 Sep 2026, with a 31 Dec 2026 purchase/activate window. EAP kit participants may see $125. Re-check before you shop.

Can I stack the $100 rebate with Peak Perks?

They are different pots: one is a purchase incentive, one is demand response. Stacking is possible only if both terms allow it. You cannot do Peak Perks and Hydro One myEnergy Rewards together.

I heat with electric baseboards. Do I qualify?

Maybe for a line-voltage model on the HRSP list if you are on the IESO grid. Peak Perks still wants central AC. Confirm the SKU before you buy.

What if my city has no rebate?

Run the schedule worksheet anyway. A programmable you use can beat a smart stat on Hold. Hydro-Québec’s ~10% is electronic vs dial, not a Wi-Fi logo.
