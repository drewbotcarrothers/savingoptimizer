Video Game Deals in Canada (2026): Black Friday, Digital Sales and When to Wait

A wish list, a price history and a date · About 11 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 1 Oct 2026

The best time to buy video games in Canada depends on what you want: physical games and accessories are often cheapest around Black Friday, which is 27 November 2026, while digital PC games see their deepest discounts in platform sales such as the Steam Winter Sale, running 17 December 2026 to 4 January 2027. Console hardware is the exception in 2026. Sony raised PlayStation 5 prices in Canada on 2 April 2026, to $819.99 for the standard model and $749.99 for the Digital Edition, and Nintendo raised the Switch 2 to $679.99 on 1 September 2026. Keep a wish list, watch the price history, and buy when the price drops below the level you set, not when the banner says sale.

Key takeaways

  • Black Friday is 27 November 2026. Physical games, controllers and accessories are often discounted that week.
  • Steam: Autumn Sale 1 to 8 October 2026; Winter Sale 17 December 2026 to 4 January 2027.
  • Console prices in Canada rose in 2026: PS5 $819.99, PS5 Digital $749.99, PS5 Pro $1,099.99 from 2 April; Switch 2 $679.99 from 1 September.
  • Subscriptions as of 25 Sep 2026: Xbox Game Pass from $13.99 to $25.99 a month; Nintendo Switch Online $29.99 a year individual or $49.99 family.
  • Physical games can be resold or traded in; digital games cannot, but they go on sale more often.
  • Example with made-up inputs: a five-game holiday list costs $174 instead of $385 by splitting purchases between Black Friday and a winter digital sale.

Console prices in 2026: why waiting may not help

Console prices usually fall later in a generation. In 2026 they went the other way. Sony raised PlayStation prices in Canada on 2 April 2026, citing global economic pressures, and Nintendo revised the Switch 2's Canadian price on 1 September 2026.

Holiday deals on consoles in 2026 are more likely to come as bundles with a game or as a retailer gift card with purchase than as a lower list price. Digital versus physical

Digital games are convenient and go on sale often, sometimes at deep discounts long after launch. You cannot resell them, lend them or trade them in, and they are tied to your account. Physical games can be resold, traded in or passed to a sibling, which lowers their real cost, and retailers often discount them during Black Friday and Boxing Day. Some newer consoles, such as the PS5 Digital Edition, cannot play discs at all.

Refunds: know the rules before you buy

As of 1 Oct 2026, Valve's Steam Refunds page says you can request a refund for purchases made within two weeks if the title has been played for less than two hours. Sony's PlayStation Store cancellation policy for Canada says you can cancel a game or add-on purchase within 14 days for a refund, provided you have not started downloading or streaming it, unless the content is faulty. Nintendo's support FAQ on digital game purchases through Nintendo.com says all sales are final, except that a pre-order can be cancelled any time before payment is processed and the game is pre-loaded, which happens up to 7 days before release. Read the policy for the store you use before buying a game you are unsure about, especially during a sale when the purchase is impulsive.

Subscriptions: when they replace buying

Game subscriptions give access to a rotating library. If you play many different games for a few hours each, a subscription can cost less than buying them. If you play one or two big games a year, buying them is usually cheaper. The site's subscription guides list prices as of 25 Sep 2026: Xbox Game Pass Essential $13.99, PC Game Pass $16.99, Premium $17.99 and Ultimate $25.99 a month; Nintendo Switch Online $29.99 a year individual or $49.99 for a family of up to eight accounts, and the Expansion Pack $69.99 or $109.99.

A subscription is easy to keep paying after you stop using it. Turn off auto-renew when you subscribe for a specific game, then decide again at the end of the month. The subscription audit guide explains how.

How to tell a real deal

  1. Make a wish list in each store you use. Platforms notify you when a wish-listed game goes on sale.
  2. Use a price-history site to see the lowest price the game has reached. A sale that matches the usual price is not a deal.
  3. Set a target price for each game. Buy at or below it.
  4. Compare the physical price at retailers with the digital price, and count resale value for the physical copy.
  5. Check the edition. A deluxe edition at a discount can still cost more than the standard edition at full price.

Gift cards and stacking

Retailers sometimes sell PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo and Steam gift cards at a discount or with a bonus during holiday sales. Buying a discounted gift card and using it during a platform sale stacks the two savings. Gift cards for digital stores are generally tied to the region of the account, so buy Canadian cards for a Canadian account. The guide to stacking tech savings covers gift cards, cash back and points.

Common mistakes

  • Buying a console bundle with games you do not want.
  • Treating every sale banner as a deal without checking the price history.
  • Buying a deluxe edition because the discount looks larger.
  • Forgetting that digital games cannot be resold or traded in.
  • Keeping a game subscription after the game you joined for is finished.
  • Buying a non-Canadian digital gift card for a Canadian account.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to buy video games in Canada?

Physical games and accessories are often cheapest around Black Friday, 27 November 2026, and Boxing Day. PC games see deep discounts in Steam sales, including the Winter Sale from 17 December 2026 to 4 January 2027. Use a wish list and price history to decide.

How much is a PS5 in Canada in 2026?

Since 2 April 2026, Sony's Canadian list prices are $819.99 for the PS5, $749.99 for the PS5 Digital Edition, $1,099.99 for the PS5 Pro and $319.99 for the PlayStation Portal.

How much is the Nintendo Switch 2 in Canada?

Nintendo revised the Switch 2's Canadian price from $629.99 to $679.99 on 1 September 2026. The original Switch's price did not change.

Can I get a refund on a digital game in Canada?

It depends on the store. As of 1 Oct 2026, Steam allows a refund request within two weeks of purchase if you played less than two hours. PlayStation Store allows cancellation within 14 days if you have not started downloading or streaming, unless the content is faulty. Nintendo says digital purchases through Nintendo.com are final, except that a pre-order can be cancelled before payment is processed, up to 7 days before release.

Is digital or physical cheaper?

Digital games go on sale more often, but physical games can be resold or traded in, which lowers their real cost. Compare the sale price with the resale value of a physical copy.
