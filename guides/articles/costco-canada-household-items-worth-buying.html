What to Buy at Costco Canada for the House (and What's Cheaper Elsewhere): Paper, Laundry, Cleaning, Batteries

Unit price in the household aisle, not the food court · About 15 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 2 Nov 2026

A Costco membership does not make every jug in the household aisle a bargain. It makes a large pack available. The pack is a bargain when its unit price beats the other tag you would have paid, and when the last sheet gets used. This page stays on paper, laundry, cleaning, and the bags and foil that showed up on one dated list. Food, gas, and the membership fee have their own guides.

The prices are from the Costco East cleaning-supply post of 7 Oct 2026 (Ontario and Atlantic warehouses, Costco flyer 28 Sep to 25 Oct 2026), plus the household items on the Costco flyer of 26 Oct to 22 Nov 2026 as posted by the same fan blog on 26 Oct 2026. It is a fan transcription of warehouse tags, not a Costco press release. Treat every figure as that date and that region. Where the same item was on the fan blog's 10 Jun 2026 cleaning post, the June tag is shown so you can see what moved. If your warehouse's tag has moved again, the method still works and the cents do not.

Key takeaways

  • Skip the Kirkland dish liquid. On the 7 Oct 2026 list it was $10.99 for 2.66 L, or $4.13 per litre. Palmolive Advanced beside it was $9.99 for 4.27 L, or $2.34 per litre, and the flyer to 8 Nov 2026 takes it to $7.99, or $1.87 per litre.
  • Kirkland liquid laundry, 146 loads at $19.99, is 13.69 cents per advertised load. That beats Tide Original at 20.54 cents, but Springtime Ultra (250 loads, $18.99) is 7.60 cents and 6.00 cents on the flyer to 8 Nov 2026. Advertised loads are the maker's claim; your dose decides.
  • Kirkland still wins on paper, pacs and freezer bags: tissue 21.04 cents per 100 sheets, paper towel $1.35 per 100 sheets against Bounty Plus at $2.38, pacs 17.76 cents against Tide Pods at 24.99 cents, freezer bags 11.45 cents against Ziploc at 15.99 cents.
  • Bags and paper towel cost more than on the June list: Kirkland paper towel $24.99 to $25.99, large freezer bags $19.99 to $21.99, kitchen drawstring bags $25.99 to $28.99. Tissue, liquid laundry, pacs and dish liquid kept their June tags.
  • Costco's warehouse price-adjustment rule is 30 days from purchase, item in stock, inside the promotional dates. A Palmolive jug bought at $9.99 within 30 days is the case the rule describes while the $2.00 flyer saving runs.

Costco household aisle winners: Kirkland paper, pacs, freezer bags (and where Kirkland loses)

Paper still wins on the 7 Oct 2026 list. Kirkland bathroom tissue, 30 rolls of 380 sheets at $23.99, is 21.04 cents per 100 sheets, the same tag as on the June list.

Cashmere Premium 2-ply, 40 rolls of 250 sheets at $21.49 with a $5.50 instant saving the list said expired 25 Oct 2026, is 21.49 cents. Kirkland paper towel, 12 rolls of 160 sheets, went from $24.99 in June to $25.99, which is $1.35 per 100 sheets. Bounty Plus, 12 rolls of 91 sheets at $25.99 with a $6.50 saving that ended 25 Oct 2026, is $2.38. Sponge Towels Premium, 12 rolls of 106 sheets, is $21.99 on the flyer to 8 Nov 2026 ($6.00 off), or $1.73. Ply for Kirkland was not on the list line. The sheet-count guide walks through the formula.

Laundry is where the store-brand story breaks. The Kirkland liquid jug, 146 washloads at $19.99, is 13.69 cents per advertised load. Tide Original, also 146 loads, is $29.99 with no saving on this list, or 20.54 cents, so Kirkland saves $10.00 a jug against Tide. But two name brands on the same list are lower per advertised load: Purex Cold Water, 250 loads at $22.99, is 9.20 cents, and Springtime Ultra, 250 loads at $18.99, is 7.60 cents, or 6.00 cents at $14.99 with the $4.00 flyer saving to 8 Nov 2026. Kirkland pacs, 152 at $26.99, are 17.76 cents each, still under Tide Pods at 24.99 cents for the 152-count box ($37.99). An advertised load is the maker's dose on the maker's jug. If you pour more of the cheaper liquid, you can erase the gap. That is the detergent guide.

Freezer and trash bags are a unit price of one bag, because the list gave counts. Kirkland large freezer bags, 6 packs of 32 at $21.99 (June $19.99), are 11.45 cents a bag; Ziploc large freezer bags, 3 packs of 50 at $23.99 (June $21.99), are 15.99 cents. Kirkland large garbage bags, 100 at $15.99 (June $14.99), are 15.99 cents a bag. Kirkland drawstring kitchen bags, 200 at $28.99 (June $25.99), are 14.50 cents. No other brand's garbage bag was on the list, so "winner" for garbage bags means "here is the unit," not "cheaper than Walmart."

Batteries and filters are in the heading because shoppers look for them in this aisle. They are not on the cleaning list cited in this guide. There is no cents-per-battery and no filter price. Furnace filters in particular vary by size. A cheap filter of the wrong size is not a saving. When you have the card in your hand, divide by the count, and for a filter divide by the months the maker says it lasts, which this page does not invent.

Items often cheaper elsewhere (or beside Kirkland): dish liquid, some detergents, small sizes

A name brand can win inside Costco, which is the surprise. Palmolive Advanced, 4.27 L at $9.99, is $2.34 per litre against Kirkland's 2.66 L jug at $10.99, or $4.13. Kirkland costs about 77% more per litre on the regular tags, and more than twice as much while Palmolive is $7.99 on the flyer to 8 Nov 2026. If the household is standing in the warehouse, the Palmolive jug is the dish-soap buy on that list.

Example, using only the list prices above: 4.27 L of dish liquid at Kirkland's $4.13 per litre is $17.64. The Palmolive jug on the flyer is $7.99. That is $9.65 for the same number of litres. Laundry works the same way: 250 advertised loads at Kirkland's 13.69 cents is $34.23, against $14.99 for the Springtime jug on the flyer, a gap of $19.24 on advertised loads. Those two swaps are $28.89 in this example. It assumes you use the same amount of each product.

Small can win too. PC dishwashing liquid on the No Frills flyer for 24 to 30 Sep 2026, Darren's No Frills, 372 Pacific Ave, Toronto, was $1.00 for 638 or 739 mL; at 638 mL that is $1.57 per litre. That flyer has ended, so treat it as the kind of line to look for, not this week's price. The small bottle wins per litre until the extra trip costs more than the gap.

Using the monthly coupon book and Costco Canada price-adjustment window (30 days)

Each book is one window. The 7 Oct 2026 post lists a flyer from 28 Sep to 25 Oct 2026; the next flyer runs 26 Oct to 22 Nov 2026.

Instant savings expire on the date printed beside them, not "sometime this season." On the current flyer, Palmolive's $2.00, Springtime's $4.00 and Sponge Towels' $6.00 end 8 Nov 2026; Tide Ultra Coldwater's $6.00 and Gain Cold Water's $3.80 end 22 Nov 2026. When you shop from a coupon book, write the end date next to the price before you divide. This page does not claim Costco has promised that every month will look like these books. It claims you should read the book you were handed.

If the price falls after you pay, the warehouse rule on Costco's help page (published 10 Sep 2025, still the current version on 2 Nov 2026) is 30 days from the purchase, item in stock, demonstration merchandise excluded, and the request has to fall within the valid promotional dates. Ask at the membership counter. If you bought online, the help page points you to a separate Costco.ca orders policy, which this guide does not restate line by line. A household jug you already opened is a poor candidate for a mood-driven return. A purchase still inside 30 days, with the saving still running, is what the rule describes. Ask. Do not assume the rule covers a price you saw at a different warehouse or after the saving ended.

Kirkland vs name brand for household staples: what testers and members report

This section does not quote a test, because no named, dated laboratory or consumer-panel report on these Kirkland household goods was published as of 2 Nov 2026. A fan blog's comment that someone prefers a brand is a preference.

It is not a sheet count and it is not a cleaning score. The dated evidence in hand is the price list. Kirkland tissue and paper towel cost less per 100 sheets than the name brands on that list. Kirkland liquid costs less per advertised load than Tide Original and more than Springtime or Purex. Kirkland dish liquid costs more per litre than Palmolive. That is the report.

If you need a performance test, run a small one. Buy the name-brand jug once and the Kirkland jug once. Use them on the same soils, the same washer, the same dose. Keep the one you will finish. A household that "knows" Tide is better and has not compared a load is paying 20.54 cents for the knowledge. The store-brand guide separates products to switch now from products to test first. Laundry and toilet paper are in the test-first group there for a reason: feel and residue are personal, and the unit price is not.

Storage and use-up math: when bulk is a false saving for small households

Kirkland tissue is 30 rolls. At an illustrative one roll a week, the pack lasts 30 weeks.

At two rolls a week, 15 weeks. Kirkland paper towel is 12 rolls of 160 sheets, 1,920 sheets. At an illustrative two sheets a day, that is 730 sheets a year and the pack lasts more than two years. Those rates are a worksheet, not a national usage study. A couple in a condo who cannot store a two-year paper-towel pack should buy the smaller unit even when the cents are worse, or split the pack. The couples framework is the membership version of that sentence.

False saving, priced. Bounty Plus at $2.38 per 100 sheets versus Kirkland at $1.35 is a gap of about $1.03 per 100 sheets, or $19.71 across the 1,920 sheets in one Kirkland pack. That calculated gap assumes you use both products at the same sheet rate and that you finish the Kirkland pack. If a third of the pack gets damp in a garage and goes in the bin, you did not save $19.71. The bulk guide is about food and the rule is the same: waste is a price increase.

Cross-link: membership break-even (Food) and Executive reward math

Household unit prices do not answer whether you should hold a card. Costco's membership conditions, dated 1 Aug 2026, say the Gold Star fee is $65 and the Executive fee is $130, plus applicable taxes, per 12-month period, each with a household card.

The membership guide is where the $65 is set against a discounter habit. The Executive versus Gold Star guide is where the reward is calculated. This page does not re-derive either one. A cheap paper towel does not, by itself, pay a fee. A fee you already paid does not make a bad unit price good. Split the cart if the warehouse wins on paper and loses on the flyer proteins.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kirkland dish soap cheaper than Palmolive at Costco?

No, not on the Costco East list of 7 Oct 2026. Kirkland dish liquid was $10.99 for 2.66 L, or $4.13 per litre. Palmolive Advanced was $9.99 for 4.27 L, or $2.34 per litre, and $7.99 on the flyer to 8 Nov 2026, or $1.87 per litre. Kirkland costs about 77% more per litre on the regular tags.

Is Kirkland toilet paper cheaper than store brands elsewhere?

On the Costco East list of 7 Oct 2026, Kirkland bathroom tissue was 21.04 cents per 100 sheets and Cashmere, with an instant saving that ended 25 Oct 2026, was 21.49 cents. No Name, Great Value, and Royale prices are not listed here, so this page does not say Kirkland beats every store brand in Canada. It beats the tissue packs on that list.

Are Costco batteries a good deal?

No battery SKU is on the cleaning list cited in this guide, so there is no cents-per-battery figure here. Divide the pack price by the count on the card, then compare with the card you would buy at another banner. A large count you will not use before the date on the pack is not a deal.

How does Costco's price adjustment work for household items?

Costco's warehouse help page, published 10 Sep 2025 and still the current version on 2 Nov 2026, says in-warehouse purchases can be adjusted within 30 days from the date of purchase if the item is in stock, demonstration merchandise excluded, and the request is within the valid promotional dates. Ask at the membership counter. Online orders use a different Costco.ca page. A saving that has ended is outside the promotional dates.

Is bulk worth it for a small household?

Only when the unit price wins and you will finish the pack. Kirkland tissue on that list is 30 rolls. At an illustrative one roll a week, that is 30 weeks. If the closet cannot hold it, the 21.04 cents is not the full cost. The couples guide and the bulk-waste guide cover membership and food. This page stops at the household aisle.

Does this post cover whether a membership is worth it?

No. Gold Star was $65 and Executive was $130, plus tax, on the membership conditions dated 1 Aug 2026. Whether that fee comes back is the Food guides linked below. This page does not re-do the break-even.
