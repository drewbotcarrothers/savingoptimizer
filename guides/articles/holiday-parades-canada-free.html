Holiday Parades: A Canadian Cost Guide

Free to watch, cheap to enjoy · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Holiday parades are free to watch in almost every Canadian city and town. Toronto's 122nd Original Santa Claus Parade ran on Sunday, 22 November 2026, starting at Christie Pits at 12:30 p.m. on a new route down Yonge Street. Smaller towns run Santa Claus and night light parades from mid-November to mid-December. Take transit, dress warmly, and bring a thermos and snacks.

Key takeaways

  • Toronto's 2026 Santa Claus Parade was 22 November, starting at 12:30 p.m. at Christie Pits (Toronto Police Service, checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Most towns run Santa Claus or light parades from mid-November to mid-December. Check your municipality's event page.
  • Watching is free. Many parades collect food bank donations.
  • Transit avoids road closures and parking costs.
  • Example: a family's parade outing costs $10 with a thermos and transit, against $50 with parking and vendor snacks.

Finding parades

  • Your municipality's events page.
  • Local news and community groups in early November.
  • Business improvement areas, which often run light parades.
  • Library and recreation calendars.
  • Toronto's parade details are on thesantaclausparade.com.

Getting there

Parades close roads for hours. Toronto Police published closures for the 2026 parade, and the TTC listed subway stations along the route. Taking transit is usually faster and cheaper than parking. Arrive early for a front spot.

Staying warm and cheap

  • Bring a thermos of hot chocolate and snacks.
  • Pack blankets, folding chairs and hand warmers.
  • Dress in layers and waterproof boots.
  • Skip vendor glow toys or set a small budget.
  • Bring a non-perishable food donation if the parade collects them.

Plan: an example

These are example amounts, not quotes. A family of four. Option 1: transit ($10 with a family or day pass) and a thermos from home. Option 2: parking ($25) and vendor hot drinks and snacks ($25).

Frequently asked questions

Are holiday parades free?

Yes, watching is free. Some parades collect food bank donations.

When was the Toronto Santa Claus Parade in 2026?

Sunday, 22 November 2026, starting at 12:30 p.m. at Christie Pits.

How do I find a Santa Claus parade near me?

Check your municipality's events page, local news and community groups in early November.

How early should I arrive?

Arrive early, often an hour or more for big-city parades, to get a front spot.

What should I bring to a holiday parade?

Warm layers, blankets, chairs, a thermos, snacks and a donation if the parade collects them.
