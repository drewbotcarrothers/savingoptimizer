Cheap Moving Supplies in Canada: Reuse, Borrow, and Buy Only What Survives Resale

Free boxes for the heavy stuff, new boxes only where it matters · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Most moving supplies can be free. Collect boxes from liquor and grocery stores, Buy Nothing groups and friends who just moved. Buy new only where it really helps, like wardrobe boxes for hanging clothes. Pack with towels and linens instead of bubble wrap, rent blankets and a dolly, and pass your boxes on when you're done.

Key takeaways

  • Free boxes: liquor stores, grocery stores, offices and Buy Nothing groups.
  • New medium boxes at Canadian U-Haul dealers: $3.50 to $3.99 each (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Space Saver wardrobe box: $27.95 at a U-Haul dealer in Barrie.
  • Pack with towels, linens and clothes instead of bubble wrap.
  • Resell or give away boxes after the move.

Moving blankets and dolly Rent or borrow Used once
Tape Buy No good free substitute

Labelling that saves rework

  • Number every box and list the contents.
  • Write the room on two sides.
  • Use one colour of tape per room.
  • Mark 'open first' boxes clearly.
  • Take photos of electronics wiring before unplugging.

A 2-bedroom supply budget: an example

Example: 40 new medium boxes at about $3.75 is $150. Free boxes plus 2 wardrobe boxes ($55.90) and tape (about $30) bring it to about $86.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I get free moving boxes in Canada?

Liquor and grocery stores, offices, Buy Nothing groups and Marketplace.

How much are moving boxes?

Canadian U-Haul dealers listed medium boxes at $3.50 to $3.99 when checked on 24 Nov 2026.

Are wardrobe boxes worth it?

For hanging clothes you don't want to fold, one or two can be.

Should I buy moving blankets?

Usually rent or borrow them.

What do I do with boxes after?

Resell them or give them away.
