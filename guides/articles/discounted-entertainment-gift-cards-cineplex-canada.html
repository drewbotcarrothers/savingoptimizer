Discounted Entertainment Gift Cards in Canada: Cineplex, Restaurants and Experiences

A discount only counts if you spend the whole card on something you'd buy anyway · About 10 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 1 Oct 2026

A discounted entertainment gift card saves money only if you would have spent that amount at that business anyway, and only by the gap between the face value and the price you paid. If a pack of cards with $100 of value costs $80, the discount is 20%, but only if every dollar gets spent on things you planned to buy. Packs of Cineplex, restaurant and experience cards are often sold below face value at warehouse clubs, grocery and drug stores, and through workplace perks programs. In Ontario, most retail gift cards cannot expire and cannot carry activation or dormancy fees, and Cineplex says its own cards never expire. Buy cards from the retailer or a store you trust, check them for tampering, and never use gift cards to pay someone who contacts you.

Key takeaways

  • Effective discount = (face value − price paid) ÷ face value. It only counts if you spend the whole card on planned purchases.
  • Packs are often sold at warehouse clubs, grocery and drug stores, and through workplace perks programs.
  • Cineplex says its gift cards never expire. Ontario bans expiry and most fees on retail gift cards.
  • Paying with a gift card at Cineplex can still earn Scene+ points if you scan your card; check the program's terms.
  • The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre: check cards for tampering, avoid resale and auction sites, and never pay anyone with gift cards under pressure.
  • Example with made-up inputs: a $100 pack for $80 saves $20 only if the family uses all of it.

Where discounted cards are sold

Discounted entertainment gift cards usually come in packs, for example several cards that add up to a set face value for less than that amount. Common places to find them include warehouse clubs, grocery stores and drug stores around the holidays, and employee perks platforms. Prices and availability change, so read the shelf tag or listing: it should show both the face value and the price.

Bonus cards are not the same thing

A common holiday offer is a free bonus card when you buy a gift card, such as a $10 bonus with a $50 card. The bonus card is a promotional card, and promotional cards are often allowed to expire under provincial rules. Write the date on it and plan to use it before then.

The discount math

Work out the effective discount, then ask whether you will use every dollar. A card pack that sits in a drawer, or leads you to spend more than you planned, is not a saving.

If you go to the movies twice a year, a large pack of movie cards is too much. If your family eats at one restaurant chain every month, a pack for that chain makes sense.

Ontario's gift card expiry and fee rules

Ontario's consumer page says most gift cards sold in the province cannot expire, and sellers cannot charge activation, service or dormancy fees on them. Fees to customize a card or replace a lost or stolen one are allowed. Some cards are not covered, including loyalty and club cards and prepaid credit cards, and cards for one specific service and charity cards can expire. Shopping mall cards can carry an activation fee of up to $1.50 and, after 15 months, a monthly dormancy fee of up to $2.50. Other provinces have their own rules; see gift card rules by province.

Cineplex's own gift card page says its gift cards never expire. That matters for an entertainment card that might be used slowly over a year or two.

Stacking with Scene+

Discounted cards can stack with loyalty points. At Cineplex, Scene+ members earn points on tickets and food. Cineplex says that from 13 May 2026, the earn rate is 3 points per $1 on regular tickets and on food and drinks, and 6 points per $1 on premium tickets, and 100 points redeem for $1 off tickets or food and drinks. Scan your Scene+ card when you pay with a gift card and check the program terms to confirm points apply to your purchase.

Stacking adds up, but the gift card discount is the biggest piece. For regular moviegoers, compare with a membership in CineClub versus Scene+.

Gift card scams

Gift cards are a favourite tool of fraudsters because they are hard to trace. The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre's holiday advice says to check gift cards in stores for tampering, by comparing them with others in stock and running a finger over the barcode, to avoid resale and auction sites when buying online, and to remember that legitimate businesses and organizations will not ask for gift cards as payment, especially under pressure.

  • Buy from the business or a store you trust.
  • Check the packaging and barcode for tampering before you pay.
  • Keep the receipt until the card is used.
  • Register the card online if the business allows it.
  • Never read card numbers to someone who phones, texts or emails asking for payment.
  • Report suspected fraud to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre.

Example with made-up inputs: one family's year

These numbers are an example with made-up inputs. They are not real prices. A family buys a pack of movie gift cards with a face value of $100 for $80, a 20% discount. Over the year they go to the movies four times, spending $90 on tickets and snacks, and give one $10 card as a stocking stuffer. The whole $100 is used on things they planned, so the saving is a real $20. They also earn Scene+ points worth a few dollars. A second pack, for a restaurant they visit once a year, would have sat unused, so they skipped it.

Plan

  1. List the entertainment and restaurant businesses you use every month or two.
  2. Estimate a year's spending at each.
  3. Buy a discounted pack only for a business where planned spending is at least the face value.
  4. Work out the effective discount from the tag.
  5. Check cards for tampering and keep receipts.
  6. Use cards with expiry dates, such as bonus cards, first.

Frequently asked questions

Are discounted Cineplex gift cards worth it?

They save the gap between face value and the price you pay, but only if you spend every dollar on movies you would have seen anyway. Work out the effective discount and compare it with your planned spending for the year.

Do Cineplex gift cards expire?

No. Cineplex says its gift cards never expire.

Can I earn Scene+ points when I pay with a gift card?

Cineplex's Scene+ page lists earn rates on tickets and food and drinks; scan your Scene+ card when you pay and check the program terms to confirm points apply.

How do I avoid gift card scams?

The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre advises checking cards for tampering, avoiding resale and auction sites, and remembering that legitimate businesses and organizations will not ask for gift cards as payment.

Can a free bonus gift card expire?

Often, yes. Bonus cards are promotional, and provincial rules usually allow promotional cards to expire. Check the date on the card.
