Provincial Child Benefits Paid With the CCB

File your taxes and you're usually signed up automatically · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Most provinces and territories pay a child benefit on top of the Canada child benefit, and you don't apply separately. The CRA uses your CCB application and both parents' tax returns. For July 2026 to June 2027, Ontario pays up to $146.66 a month per child, BC up to $145.83 for the first child, and Alberta $1,529 a year for the first child plus a working-income amount.

Key takeaways

  • Ontario child benefit: up to $146.66 a month per child (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • BC family benefit: up to $145.83 a month for the first child.
  • Alberta: $1,529 a year for the first child, plus a working-income component.
  • No separate application; the CRA uses your CCB information.
  • Both parents must file taxes every year.

How to make sure you get them

  • Apply for the CCB when your child is born, usually through the birth registration.
  • Both parents or partners file taxes every year, even with no income.
  • Update your address and marital status with the CRA.
  • Check My Account for payment amounts.
  • Alberta's benefit is paid separately, four times a year.

Other provinces and territories

Several other provinces and territories also pay benefits with the CCB; the CRA's T4114 pamphlet lists each program and its amounts. Quebec's family allowance is paid by Retraite Québec, not the CRA.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to apply for the Ontario child benefit?

No. The CRA uses your Canada child benefit information.

How much is the Ontario child benefit?

Up to $146.66 a month per child for July 2026 to June 2027.

How much is the BC family benefit?

Up to $145.83 a month for the first child, $91.66 for the second and $75 for each additional child.

When is the Alberta child and family benefit paid?

In August, November, February and May, separately from the CCB.

Why did my provincial benefit stop?

Often because a parent didn't file a tax return. File both returns every year.
