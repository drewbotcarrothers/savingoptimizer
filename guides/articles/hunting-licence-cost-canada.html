Hunting Licence Costs: A Canadian Cost Guide

Licences are the small part; plan the whole first year · About 8 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Hunting licences are usually a small part of the cost of hunting in Canada. In Ontario, residents pay $8.57 for an Outdoors Card, $22.76 for a one-year small game licence and $43.86 for a deer licence, before HST, under 2026 fees. New hunters also need the Ontario Hunter Education Program, a firearms licence to use firearms, and gear. Other provinces set their own fees and rules.

Key takeaways

  • Ontario 2026 resident fees before HST: Outdoors Card $8.57, small game $22.76 a year, deer $43.86 (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Moose: $15 tag application, $35.29 licence, and a tag of $200 (bull), $150 (cow/calf) or $30 (calf) if you're drawn.
  • New hunters must complete the Ontario Hunter Education Program and pass the exam.
  • To use firearms, you also need a firearms licence through the RCMP Canadian Firearms Program.
  • Migratory birds like ducks and geese need a federal migratory game bird permit as well.

First-time hunters

  • Take the Ontario Hunter Education Program course and exam; instructors set course fees.
  • Get a firearms licence (PAL) through the RCMP Canadian Firearms Program if you'll hunt with a firearm; this needs a safety course and a fee.
  • Get your Outdoors Card and licences.
  • Learn the regulations for your Wildlife Management Unit and season.
  • Bow hunting has its own requirements; check the regulations summary.

Other costs

Gear, blaze orange clothing, travel, accommodation and meat processing usually cost more than licences. Join a club or hunt with experienced hunters to borrow gear at first. If you fish too, see our Ontario fishing licence guide.

Plan: an example first year

A new Ontario hunter wants small game and deer. Licences: Outdoors Card $8.57 + small game $22.76 + deer $43.86 = $75.19 before HST, or about $84.96 with 13% HST. Courses, the firearms licence and gear are extra.

Choosing a three-year small game licence at $68.28 instead of three one-year licences at $22.76 costs the same, $68.28, but saves buying each year.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a hunting licence in Ontario?

For residents in 2026, before HST: Outdoors Card $8.57, one-year small game licence $22.76, deer licence $43.86, and other licences and tags by species.

How much does a moose tag cost in Ontario?

The application is $15, the moose licence is $35.29, and a bull tag is $200, a cow/calf tag $150 or a calf tag $30 if you're drawn, before HST.

Do I need a course to hunt in Ontario?

Yes. New hunters must complete the Ontario Hunter Education Program and pass the exam.

Do I need a firearms licence to hunt?

To hunt with a firearm, you need a firearms licence through the RCMP Canadian Firearms Program.

Are hunting licence fees the same across Canada?

No. Each province sets its own licences, fees and rules.
