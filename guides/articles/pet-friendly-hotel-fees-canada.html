Pet-Friendly Hotel Fees in Canada

The pet fee can cost more than the upgrade · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Pet fees at Canadian hotels range from nothing to $50 a night or more, and rules vary by hotel, even within one chain. Fairmont Hotel Vancouver caps its fee at $100 a stay, while a $40-a-night fee adds $160 to a 4-night stay. Kimpton Saint George in Toronto charges no pet fee at all. Always check the hotel's own page or call.

Key takeaways

  • Fairmont Hotel Vancouver: $50 a night, $100 maximum per stay.
  • Fairmont Banff Springs: $50 per room per night.
  • Best Western Premier Chateau Granville: $40 a night plus $75 refundable deposit.
  • Kimpton Saint George, Toronto: no pet fee or deposit (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Service and guide dogs are exempt from pet fees.

Tips

  • Check the hotel's own pet page, not just the chain's.
  • Ask about weight and number limits.
  • Ask if there's a maximum per stay.
  • Compare with a pet-friendly rental's cleaning fee.
  • Don't leave pets alone in the room unless allowed.

Frequently asked questions

How much do Canadian hotels charge for pets?

From nothing to $50 a night or more; it varies by hotel.

Which hotels don't charge a pet fee?

Kimpton Saint George in Toronto charged no pet fee when checked on 24 Nov 2026.

Do service dogs pay pet fees?

No. Service and guide dogs are exempt.

Is there a maximum pet fee?

Some hotels cap it; Fairmont Hotel Vancouver caps it at $100 a stay.

Can I leave my dog alone in a hotel room?

Many hotels don't allow it; check the policy.
