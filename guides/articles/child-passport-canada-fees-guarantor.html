Child Passports in Canada: Fees, Guarantors and Timing

Apply early, and the other parent can be the guarantor · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

A Canadian child passport costs $58.50 and lasts five years. Apply at least a couple of months before you travel. For a first passport you need a guarantor, and the other parent can be it. Processing takes 10 business days at a passport office or 20 by mail. If your child is travelling without both parents, carry a consent letter.

Key takeaways

  • Child passport (under 16): $58.50, valid 5 years (from 31 March 2026, checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • The other parent can sign as guarantor.
  • You need 2 references who aren't family.
  • Service standard: 10 business days at a passport office, 20 by mail.
  • Urgent pickup costs $125.75 extra.

Fees and timing

Child passport fees and service standards. Checked 24 Nov 2026. As of 24 Nov 2026.
Item Detail
Child passport, in Canada $58.50 (5 years)
Child passport, outside Canada $102.50
Passport office, in person 10 business days
Mail or regular Service Canada Centre 20 business days
Urgent pickup $125.75 extra

Guarantor and references

  • Guarantor: 18 or older, a Canadian citizen with a valid 5- or 10-year passport (or expired under a year).
  • They must have known you 2 years and know your child.
  • The parent applying can't be the guarantor, but the other parent can.
  • References: 2 people who aren't family and have known you 2 years.
  • Guarantors can't charge for signing.

Travel consent letters

If your child travels without both parents, or with only one, carry a consent letter signed by the parent who isn't travelling. Border officers may ask for it. Child passports can't be renewed online, so plan for a mailed or in-person application.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a child passport in Canada?

$58.50 in Canada from 31 March 2026, valid for five years.

Can a parent be the guarantor for a child passport?

The applying parent can't, but the other parent can if they meet the requirements.

How long does a child passport take?

10 business days at a passport office, or 20 by mail or at a regular Service Canada Centre.

Do I need a consent letter?

It's recommended when a child travels without both parents.

Can I renew a child passport online?

No. Online renewal is only for adult passports.
