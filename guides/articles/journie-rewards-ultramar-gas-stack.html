How Journie Rewards at Ultramar Fits a Canadian Gas Loyalty Stack

Free to join; the CIBC link is worth more than the points · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

If you already fill up at Ultramar, Chevron or Pioneer, Journie Rewards is free and worth adding. You earn 1 point per litre and get a reward every 300 points. For someone buying 150 litres a month, the rewards are worth about $30 a year. Linking a CIBC card adds 3 cents a litre, about $54 more. If you don't have a participating station nearby, skip it.

Key takeaways

  • 1 point per litre, 2 points per $1 in-store (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Every 300 points: 7 cents a litre off one fill, $5 in-store, or 300 Aeroplan points.
  • Linked CIBC card: 3 cents a litre off, up to 100 litres a fill.
  • Rewards expire after 60 days; points after a year with no activity.
  • Ultramar stations with Circle K stores don't take part.

How to stack it

  • Scan Journie at the pump for points.
  • Pay with a linked CIBC card for 3 cents a litre.
  • Or pay with a gas or grocery credit card that earns more than the CIBC discount.
  • Pick the $5 in-store reward if your usual fill is under about 70 litres.
  • Use rewards within 60 days.

A year: an example

Example: 150 litres a month is 1,800 litres a year, or six 300-point rewards from fuel alone. Taking the $5 in-store reward each time is $30. A linked CIBC card saves 3 cents on 1,800 litres, or $54. Together that's $84 a year.

Frequently asked questions

What is Journie Rewards?

The free loyalty program at participating Ultramar, Chevron, Pioneer, Fas Gas and On the Run stations.

How much are Journie points worth?

Every 300 points you can take 7 cents a litre off one fill, $5 in-store, or 300 Aeroplan points.

Do all Ultramar stations take Journie?

No. Ultramar stations with Circle K stores don't take part. Check the station locator.

How does the CIBC Journie discount work?

Link an eligible CIBC card and pay with it to save 3 cents a litre, up to 100 litres a fill.

Do Journie points expire?

Rewards expire after 60 days, and points are forfeited after a year with no activity.
