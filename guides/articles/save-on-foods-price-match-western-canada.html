Save-On-Foods Price Match: How Western Canadian Shoppers Can Use It

Bring the competitor's flyer and match the identical item · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Save-On-Foods will match a lower flyer price from a local competitor on an identical grocery item. Show the competitor's current flyer, or a screenshot of it, at the till or customer service. It doesn't apply to online orders. With several competitors nearby, it can save a lot on name brands: in one Metro Vancouver check this week, 38 items were advertised for less elsewhere.

Key takeaways

  • We Match: identical grocery items, competitor flyer prices, local trade area (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Not available for online purchases.
  • Bring the flyer or a screenshot of it.
  • 38 items were advertised for less at Metro Vancouver competitors on 24 Nov 2026.
  • Example: three matches save $13.48.

How to use it

  • Check competitor flyers in a flyer app before you shop.
  • Match the same brand and size.
  • Make sure the competitor's flyer is still valid that day.
  • Show it at the till or customer service.
  • Ask your store which competitors it accepts.

Frequently asked questions

Does Save-On-Foods price match?

Yes. Its We Match policy matches identical grocery items in a local competitor's current flyer.

Does Save-On-Foods price match online orders?

No. We Match isn't available for online purchases.

What do I need to show?

The competitor's current flyer, or a screenshot of it.

Which competitors does Save-On-Foods match?

Competitors in the store's trade area. Ask your store for its list.

Can I match a website price?

Not a website price. It has to be an advertised flyer price.
