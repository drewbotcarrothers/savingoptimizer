Adult Halloween Parties: A Canadian Cost Guide

Spooky on a budget, for grown-ups · About 7 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

An adult Halloween party costs most in food, drinks and costumes. Hosting at home with a potluck and bring-your-own drinks keeps the bill low; a ticketed bar or club party adds cover, drink prices and a ride home. Reuse decorations, buy next year's on clearance the day after Halloween, and thrift or make costumes. Halloween 2027 falls on a Sunday, so most parties will be the Saturday before.

Key takeaways

  • Halloween 2027 is a Sunday, so most adult parties will be on Saturday 30 October.
  • A potluck and BYOB party cuts the host's biggest costs, food and alcohol.
  • Buy decorations on clearance after Halloween and store them for next year.
  • Thrifted or DIY costumes usually cost much less than new packaged ones.
  • Example: a host spends $180 for 20 guests, $9 a head, compared with $60 or more a person at a ticketed party.

Costumes

  • Build from what you own; one item, like a hat or wig, changes an outfit.
  • Thrift stores stock costume pieces in October.
  • Swap costumes with friends.
  • Buy accessories on clearance after Halloween for next year.
  • Group costumes can share the cost of props.

Going out

Ticketed bar and club parties add cover charges, drink prices and late-night rides. Buy tickets early if prices rise closer to the date, eat first and set a drink budget. Plan a sober ride, transit or a rideshare in advance; impaired driving penalties in Canada are severe. For family Halloween costs, see our Halloween budget guide.

Plan: an example

A host invites 20 adults to a potluck and BYOB party. They spend $180 in total, as in the table above.

Hosting: $180 ÷ 20 = $9 a head. A ticketed bar party at an example $25 cover, two drinks at $12 and a $15 ride share costs about $64 each. The home party saves each guest about $55.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an adult Halloween party cost to host?

It depends on who brings food and drinks. In our example, a potluck and BYOB party for 20 guests costs about $180, or $9 a head; providing everything costs about $620.

When is Halloween in 2027?

Halloween 2027 falls on Sunday, 31 October, so many adult parties will be on Saturday, 30 October.

How can I save on a Halloween costume?

Build from clothes you own, thrift, swap with friends, and buy accessories on clearance after Halloween.

Is it rude to ask guests to bring drinks?

No. BYOB is common for adult parties. Say so clearly on the invitation.

How do I get home safely from a Halloween party?

Plan a sober ride, transit or a rideshare before you go out.
