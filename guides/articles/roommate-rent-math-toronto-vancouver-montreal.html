Roommate math for Toronto, Vancouver, and Montréal: cutting rent without losing your mind

Split the 2-bed, then write the rules · About 17 minutes · 20 Sep 2026

Urban workers tweak groceries while rent still eats the budget. The lever that actually moves a Toronto, Vancouver, or Montréal paycheque is often a roommate — if the split is honest and the house rules are written. Personal Finance Canada and askTO threads keep rediscovering the same math: a $3,200 solo one-bed leaves no savings rate; a lawful share of a two-bed can.

Date-stamped asking rents, not vibes. Statistics Canada’s Quarterly Rent Statistics for Q2 2026 (Daily, 9 Sep 2026, program run with CMHC): average asking two-bedroom Vancouver $3,030, Toronto $2,650, Montréal $1,820. Paid two-beds (sitting tenants) were $2,470 / $2,160 / $1,360. A hunt on Kijiji or PadMapper faces asking. CPI rent for August 2026 was +2.8% nationally (Daily, 14 Sep 2026) — still not your listing.

Key takeaways

  • Q2 2026 asking 2-bed: Van $3,030 · TO $2,650 · Mtl $1,820. Two-way equal split: $1,515 / $1,325 / $910 before utilities.
  • Split by room size or equal; income splits need a written gift plan.
  • Add hydro, internet, and a grocery house-fund on the same sheet as rent.
  • All names on the lease vs one primary is a risk choice, not a vibe.
  • Write exit and sublet rules before someone’s job is in Calgary.

Budget pies: what rent share does to savings rates in expensive cities

Labelled after-tax $4,400/month (a round “urban worker” sketch, not your T4). A $2,400 solo one-bed is 55% of take-home before the Metropass or Compass. A $1,325 Toronto half of a $2,650 two-bed is 30%. The $1,075 gap is the RRSP/FHSA/emergency-fund conversation people try to have with oat milk. Vancouver’s $1,515 half of $3,030 is still 34% of that sketch — better than a $2,800 solo, not a cheap city. Montréal’s $910 half of $1,820 is the outlier that still needs TAL-aware lease habits.

Fair split methods: by room size, income, or equal

  1. Equal. Fast. Works when bedrooms are comparable and no one is on a den.
  2. By room. Measure or use the listing square footage. A 20% larger room pays 20% more of rent. Closets and ensuite bathrooms count.
  3. By income. A higher earner can choose to pay more. Write it as a voluntary split with a review date, or it becomes a grudge when raises differ.

The person on the sofa bed is not a one-third roommate. Parking spots and lockers follow the person who uses them.

Written house rules that prevent expensive blowups

  • Rent due date, e-transfer address, and what happens on day 2 of silence.
  • Guests / partners: nights per month before they contribute or leave.
  • Quiet hours, dishes, and the thermostat — especially in a baseboard unit.
  • Who talks to the landlord. One voice prevents “I thought you filed.”
  • Cleaning rotation that fits the actual rooms, not a printed chore wheel from 2014.

A one-page PDF in the same folder as the lease is enough. Politeness that cannot survive a written date will not survive February.

Lease structure: all on lease vs one primary tenant risks

All adults on the lease: the landlord can generally pursue each of you for the rent. You usually have the statutory tenant protections of that province (Ontario LTB, B.C. RTB, Québec TAL). One primary plus informal roommates: the primary holds the arrears and the hearing; the others may be guests or occupants with thinner rights if things blow up. Subletting and assignment have their own forms and consent rules — Ontario and B.C. are not the same as a Montréal lease assignment. This page will not litigate your house. A tenant clinic will.

Move-in cash is also split math. Ontario last-month, B.C. half-month caps, and Québec’s no-deposit rule live on the first-and-last page. Do not invent a “damage deposit” to make a roommate feel serious.

Exit clauses and sublet plans

Write: notice period to the group (often 60 days, aligned with the lease), who finds the replacement, whose name stays on the lease, and how the last month / security is handed off. If someone might take a job in another city, agree now whether the remaining people can afford the place at N−1. Illegal extra-occupant stacking to “make the number work” is how people lose units and relationships in the same month. Hunt replacements with the same scam filters you used the first time.

Frequently asked questions

What are typical two-bedroom asking rents in those cities?

Statistics Canada’s Quarterly Rent Statistics for Q2 2026 (Daily, 9 Sep 2026): average asking rent for a two-bedroom was $3,030 in Vancouver, $2,650 in Toronto, and $1,820 in Montréal. Paid rents for sitting tenants were lower ($2,470 / $2,160 / $1,360). A roommate hunt usually faces asking, not paid.

Is an equal split always fair?

Equal is simplest when rooms are similar. A closed bedroom with a door is not a sofa. Square-footage or “room + closet” splits reduce resentment. Income-based splits are a gift plan — write them down so they do not become a silent subsidy.

Should every roommate be on the lease?

All-on-lease means the landlord can chase each of you. One primary tenant who sublets or “has a roommate” can leave the others with fewer statutory protections and the primary with all the arrears. Provincial rules differ (Ontario RTA, B.C. RTA, Québec Civil Code / TAL). This is not a substitute for a clinic.

Does each roommate need tenant insurance?

Often yes — contents and liability are personal. Do not assume one $1 million policy covers three names. See the tenant-insurance comparison page and match any lease certificate clause.

Is this legal advice?

No. Educational money math. Use LTB / RTB / TAL resources or a tenant clinic for your facts.
