Québec renters: why most security deposits are illegal—and what to do instead

Article 1904 as cash-flow savings · About 17 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 20 Sep 2026

Newcomers land in Montréal with an Ontario “first and last” spreadsheet and hand over a damage deposit a lessor generally may not exact. Civil Code of Québec article 1904 is the savings statute: no extra instalment beyond one month’s rent, no rent in advance beyond the first payment period, no amount other than the rent as a deposit, no postdated cheques. TAL’s English “paying the rent” page says the same thing in public language. July 1 culture is not article 1904.

This is a move-in cash guide. It is not legal advice and not a reason to stay in an unsafe unit to win an argument.

Key takeaways

  • Typical lawful up-front cash: the first period’s rent. Not last-month. Not a damage jar.
  • Key money, extra months, and required “voluntary” deposits are the refuse list. Postdated cheques are also barred.
  • Refuse in writing with a citation. Compete with a complete application, not a $1,500 e-transfer.
  • If you already paid: write once, then TAL. Clinics exist because the forms are fiddly.
  • Same $2,200 rent: Québec ~$2,200 · Ontario ~$4,400 · B.C. ~$3,300. 20 Sep 2026 labelled sketch.

What landlords can generally demand up front in Québec

The first payment period’s rent — and not more than one month if that period is longer — plus a properly completed lease. Identification and references are ordinary. A lessor who wants first, last, and damage is importing Ontario slang. Use the three-province planner if you are moving from Toronto or Vancouver so you do not over-budget or overpay.

Damage deposits, key money, and post-dated cheque issues

Article 1904 groups the extras: amounts other than rent as a deposit, extra instalments, postdated instruments. TAL repeats that a lessor may not require a security or key deposit as a condition. A clause in the lease that says otherwise is the kind of clause the Tribunal treats as invalid. Éducaloi (8 May 2026) is useful public-language backup: requiring a deposit is illegal; a truly voluntary offer is a different, fact-specific story — do not let a printed “voluntary” form do the exacting.

Application fees sit in the same “extra amount” problem category. See the cross-Canada fee map.

How do you refuse an illegal deposit without losing the unit?

Hello [name], I can send a complete application and the first month’s rent as the Civil Code and TAL allow. I cannot send a damage deposit, last-month deposit, or postdated cheques (article 1904). Happy to provide references today and sign the mandatory lease form.

You are asking to rent lawfully. Some lessors will move on. That is cheaper than parking $1,500 you must later recover. A same-day package and a French cover paragraph help you compete without the cheque.

If you already paid: recovery overview via TAL framing

Keep the listing, texts, and e-transfer. Ask in writing for the excess back with a date.

If they ignore you, TAL can order repayment of amounts a lessor was not allowed to exact. This overview is not a filing kit: confirm current forms, fees, and time limits on tal.gouv.qc.ca. Community legal clinics exist because landlords sometimes ignore email. Safety first — do not stay in a lockout to win $800.

Lease form basics newcomers miss

Québec residential leases are supposed to use the mandatory Tribunal form, not a one-page Facebook template. Fill the rent, inclusions, and occupants.

Watch for clauses that reintroduce a deposit, a penalty, or a waiver of TAL rights. Assignment and sublet rules changed in 2024 — do not sign a homemade “no assignment ever” paragraph without reading the assignment vs sublet guide. French and English versions exist; use the one you will actually understand, and keep a copy.

Frequently asked questions

Can a Montréal landlord ask for a damage deposit?

Generally no. Civil Code article 1904 says a lessor may not exact any amount other than the rent as a deposit, may not take more than the first payment period up front, and may not demand postdated cheques. TAL’s paying-the-rent page repeats that shape. A printed “voluntary” form that is required to get the keys is still exacting.

What can they ask for on signing?

Typically the first period’s rent (not more than one month if the period is longer), a completed mandatory lease form, and ordinary identification / application documents — not extra months and not key money.

I already paid a $1,500 deposit. Now what?

Keep the listing and the e-transfer. Ask in writing for it back, citing article 1904 / TAL. If that fails, the Tribunal administratif du logement is the usual path. This is not a filing kit or a promise about your facts.

How does move-in cash compare to Ontario and B.C.?

On a labelled $2,200 rent: Québec typically about $2,200 (first month); Ontario about $4,400 (first + last-month); B.C. about $3,300 (first + $1,100 security). 20 Sep 2026 sketches. Not your lease.

Is this legal advice?

No. Use tal.gouv.qc.ca, Éducaloi, a community clinic, or a licensed lawyer for a dispute.
