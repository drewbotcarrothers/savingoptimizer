How to compare PC Optimum, Petro-Points, and Scene+ at the pump in Canada (2026)

Esso, Petro-Canada, Shell · litres not gallons · About 17 minutes · 16 Sep 2026

Canadians still pick a gas brand the way they picked a hockey team: childhood, the corner, a faded Air Miles card. Meanwhile Esso pays PC Optimum, Petro-Canada runs Petro-Points Platinum (launched 1 Jan 2026), and Shell finished leaving Air Miles for Scene+ in spring 2026. The cents-per-litre winner moved. Habit did not.

This is a pump-first stack for 2026 Canada — not a U.S. warehouse-club essay, and not a grocery-loyalty duplicate of PC vs Scene+ vs Moi. Grocery points still matter if you already live in one ecosystem. The worksheet is 40 litres, because that is a common tank slice and Esso’s 10¢/L burn caps at 40 L.

Disclosure: Gas-rewards credit cards and co-branded loyalty cards are offer types. Saving Optimizer may earn a commission if we later add partner links. No card or fuel-brand partnerships are claimed. Compare applications on issuer sites and pay the statement in full. Pump prices and bonus offers change weekly.

Key takeaways

  • Base earn is similar: about 1¢ per litre at Esso (PC), Petro-Canada, and Shell (Scene+) before cards.
  • The stack is the story: PC Mastercard at Esso (30–70 pts/L), Petro Platinum + Triangle/RBC, Shell Go+ + Scotia/Tangerine pump cents.
  • Grocery-first households should not switch banners for 1¢/L. Gas-first (trades, long commute) should check Platinum and card links.
  • Load offers weekly. Scene+ has run litre bonuses (example in press: 750 points on 30 L ≈ $7.50) that dwarf base earn.
  • Street price still wins. Loyalty is a tie-breaker, not a religion.

PC Optimum at Esso is the same currency you use at No Frills and Shoppers. Scene+ at Shell is the same currency as Sobeys / FreshCo. Petro-Points are station-native but plug into Canadian Tire’s Triangle world. That is why “best gas program” is really “which household already has a till.”

Base points-per-litre vs stacked credit-card discounts

Valuation we use unless a bonus says otherwise:

  • PC Optimum: 10,000 = $10 → 0.1¢ per point. 10 pts/L = 1¢/L. Esso FAQ: PC Mastercard at least 30 pts/L; Insiders World Elite can reach 50 pts/L base card earn and 70 pts/L with a 150 L monthly volume bonus, plus extra 10 pts/L on some premium grades. 30 pts/L ≈ 3¢/L if you value points at grocery.
  • Petro-Points: 1,000 = $1 → 0.1¢ per point. 10 pts/L = 1¢/L; Platinum 15 pts/L = 1.5¢/L. Linked RBC cards: about 3¢/L instant plus Avion bonuses (issuer/Petro terms).
  • Scene+: 1,000 = $10 → 1¢ per point. 1 pt/L = 1¢/L. Shell Go+ can add 10% bonus on regular and 2× on V-Power. Linked Scotiabank: public FAQ cites 3¢/L instant on any grade and extra on V-Power with Scene+ cards (up to about 7¢/L in their worked stack). Tangerine has its own 3¢/L style offer when linked.

Interest is not a fuel discount. A 20.99% card for 3¢/L is a bad trade. The grocery-card rule still applies: pay in full or skip the accelerator.

Petro-Points Platinum and CT Money conversion basics

Platinum is Petro-Canada’s frequent-fueller tier from 1 January 2026. Threshold in company materials: 1,000 litres per quarter (they illustrate ~45 L twice a week). Status is earned in-quarter, then you re-qualify. Rewards cited in Petro/creditcardGenius explainers: 50% more points, member offers, up to ~$500/year of program value if you also use partner links — treat $500 as a ceiling marketing number, not your household forecast.

Triangle Rewards link: up to 3¢/L in CT Money on qualifying fuel (existing), plus $25 CT Money each quarter you are Platinum (max $100/year). Conversion of Petro-Points to CT Money is typically 1,000 points → $1, in 1,000-point steps, with an annual cap in program rules (commonly cited at $1,000 CT Money). Confirm in the Petro app before you dump a six-figure balance.

If you do not buy 1,000 L/quarter, Platinum will never turn on. Do not drive extra loops to “unlock status.” That is buying litres to earn a 0.5¢ raise.

Shell Go+ and Scene+ stack after Air Miles exit

Milesopedia: Shell + Scene+ effective 3 Mar 2026 in Alberta and 26 May 2026 elsewhere. Earn 1 Scene+ / L and 1 / $ in-store; redeem 1,000 = $10 at participating Shell. Link Scene+ inside Shell Go+ for the bonus-point layer. Link an eligible Scotia or Tangerine card for instant cents, which are not the same as Scene+ points — they come off the pump price.

If your groceries are already Scene+ (Sobeys empire), Shell keeps one currency. If your groceries are Loblaw, Shell Scene+ is a second app. Two apps are fine. Two detours are not.

If Esso is 4¢/L higher on the street, the card stack has to work before you leave the cheaper Petro. If you would redeem PC at a Shoppers bonus event, Esso earn is worth more than 0.1¢ — but only on points you were going to burn anyway (see maximize PC Optimum).

  1. Write three street prices for the same grade.
  2. Subtract known instant cents (Scotia/Tangerine/RBC/CT).
  3. Subtract base points at the valuations above.
  4. Add one loaded offer if it applies to this fill, not a hypothetical.
  5. Add driving time only if the winner is not on the commute.

Offers to load weekly so you do not miss bonus burns

Sunday, with groceries: open PC Optimum (Esso tiles), Petro-Points/app, Scene+ and Shell Go+. Load, do not browse. Bonus litres (Scene+ 750 pts on 30 L style events) are the only time base math looks silly — $7.50 on 30 L is 25¢/L, which beats almost any card. If the offer needs a brand you will not pass, skip it.

Redemption hygiene: Esso 4,000 pts for 10¢/L × 40 L = $4, same 0.1¢ as grocery. A car wash at 10,000 pts only wins if you would have paid $15–$20 cash. Scene+ $10 burns at the pump are clean. Petro points as fuel cents versus CT Money depends on whether Canadian Tire is already a stop.

Journie / Ultramar and independent stations exist; they are a later-wave topic. For now, three empires plus Costco is enough system.

Frequently asked questions

Which Canadian gas loyalty program is best in 2026?

There is no universal winner. Base earn is roughly 1¢/L at Esso (PC Optimum 10 pts/L at 10,000 = $10), Petro-Points (10 pts/L at 1,000 = $1), and Shell Scene+ (1 pt/L at 1,000 = $10). Credit cards, Petro-Points Platinum, Shell Go+, and grocery-first habits usually decide more than the base rate.

When did Shell switch from Air Miles to Scene+?

Shell joined Scene+ on 3 March 2026 in Alberta and 26 May 2026 in the rest of Canada (Milesopedia). Air Miles at Shell is not the 2026 stack.

How do I qualify for Petro-Points Platinum?

Petro-Canada’s frequent-fueller tier (from 1 Jan 2026) needs about 1,000 litres per calendar quarter — roughly two fills a week. Platinum earns 50% more points (15/L instead of 10) and, if Triangle Rewards is linked, $25 CT Money per qualifying quarter (up to $100/year) plus existing CT Money per litre offers.

Do PC Optimum points earn more with a PC Mastercard at Esso?

Yes, on Esso’s public FAQ: at least 30 points per litre with a PC Mastercard, up to 70/L on Insiders World Elite with volume bonuses, plus extra on some premium grades. That is a card decision — never carry a balance for 3¢/L.

Should I drive extra kilometres to a ‘better’ brand?

Usually no. A 4 km detour at 8 L/100 km is not free. Compare station street prices first, then loyalty. Costco gas is a separate membership math problem.
