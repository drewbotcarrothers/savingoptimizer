How to Compare PC Optimum, Petro-Points, and Scene+ at the Pump in Canada (2026)

Esso, Petro-Canada, Shell — not a U.S. gas club · About 16 minutes · 16 Sep 2026

Canadian drivers pick a gas brand the way they pick a coffee shop: the one on the way home. Meanwhile the loyalty stack under that pump has moved. Esso and Mobil still pay PC Optimum. Petro-Canada added a Platinum frequent-fueller tier on 1 January 2026. Shell’s Air Miles era is over; participating stations now earn and burn Scene+, with Shell Go+ instant cents if you link the right Scotia or Tangerine card.

This is a 2026 Canada comparison of those three pump ecosystems — not Costco membership math (different article later) and not U.S. fuel-club advice. Grocery loyalty still lives with Food & Groceries; here the question is cents per litre after the posted price. If you already shop No Frills, start with how to maximize PC Optimum so Esso is a burn option, not a second hobby.

Disclosure: Gas rewards credit cards and loyalty co-branded cards (PC Financial Mastercards, RBC cards linked to Petro-Points, Scene+ Scotia/Tangerine cards) are offer types. Saving Optimizer may earn a commission if we later add partner links. We do not currently claim card-issuer partnerships. Compare applications on the issuer’s site, and never carry a balance for 3¢/L.

Key takeaways

  • Base earn at all three majors is about 1¢ per litre once you convert points at typical grocery/fuel redemption.
  • The real spread is stacked cards and instant pump discounts — roughly 3–7¢/L when you already hold the plastic.
  • Petro-Points Platinum is a volume product (~1,000 L/quarter). Most hybrid commuters will not hit it.
  • Grocery-first households should fill where they already earn, unless the posted pump is meaningfully cheaper elsewhere.
  • Load weekly offers. A 10¢/L bonus week beats a 1¢ base earn you drove 12 minutes to collect.

Base points-per-litre vs stacked credit-card discounts

Convert everything to cents per litre on a 40 L fill. At 10 PC points per litre and 0.1¢ per point, base Esso earn is 1¢/L = $0.40 on 40 L. Same story at Petro-Points 10 pts/L if 1,000 points = $1. Shell’s 1 Scene+ point per litre at 1¢ per point is also 1¢/L. The programs are tied until you add plastic.

  • PC Mastercard at Esso: public Esso FAQ — at least 30 points/L (~3¢/L). Insiders World Elite: 50 points/L base card earn, plus 20 more in a month where you buy 150 L, and extra 10 points/L on some premium grades (up to ~70 points/L in the marketing line, ~7¢/L). Confirm esso.ca before you treat 7¢ as a promise.
  • RBC linked to Petro-Points: public explainers describe 3¢/L instant plus 20% more points (12 pts/L). Instant cents are better than points you might forget to burn.
  • Shell Go+ + eligible Scene+ Scotia/Tangerine: Shell’s partner FAQ describes 3¢/L instant on all grades when linked, and up to 7¢/L on Shell V-Power with a Scene+-earning Scotia card (3¢ instant + 4¢ V-Power). Regular grade is the 3¢ product for most tanks.

Petro-Points Platinum and CT Money conversion basics

Platinum is Petro-Canada’s answer to “we drive a lot.” CreditcardGenius and other 2026 explainers: 1,000 litres every three months (roughly two fills a week) unlocks 50% more points on fuel, washes, and in-store, plus member-only offers. That is 15 points/L instead of 10 while status is active. Miss the next quarter’s 1,000 L and you drop back.

Triangle Rewards / Canadian Tire overlay: Platinum quarters can add CT Money (publicly described as $25 CT Money per qualifying quarter, up to $100/year) and extra cents per litre when you pay a certain way. Conversion rates move — read the current Petro-Points and Triangle pages before you reroute a tank to collect $25 in store credit.

Households that ended in March 2026: you can no longer pool points across family cards the old way. If two adults share a car, put both fills on the account that is chasing Platinum, or stop chasing Platinum.

A 1,000 L quarter is 333 L/month. Ratehub’s 2026 TCO sketch uses ~132 L/month for a RAV4 at 20,000 km/year. Platinum is for high-km households and work trucks, not for a two-day office week.

Shell Go+ and Scene+ stack after Air Miles exit

If your mental model is still “Shell = Air Miles,” update it. Scene+ at Shell earns 1 point/L and 1 point per dollar on eligible convenience and car wash, redeemable 1,000 = $10. That is a clean 1¢/L base — the same as the other two majors — with a grocery and movie burn that Air Miles never matched as neatly for Empire shoppers.

Go+ is the account you link a payment card to. Instant 3¢/L is the everyday lever. V-Power extras are only a win if you were buying premium anyway. Do not upsize octane to chase 4¢.

Scene+ grocery earn is still offers-first at Sobeys and FreshCo. Filling at Shell does not replace the Sunday offer-load in the grocery system. It just means the same points wallet can shave a tank.

Which program wins for grocery-first vs gas-first households

  • Loblaw / No Frills / Superstore household: Esso/Mobil. You already want PC points for groceries and Shoppers bonus-redemption weeks. A no-fee PC Mastercard is the usual accelerator — see the grocery card guides — not a second fuel-only card.
  • Empire / FreshCo / Sobeys household: Shell + Scene+ if a Shell is on the route. Petro-Canada does not feed Scene+.
  • Gas-first, high-km, already at Petro-Canada: chase Platinum only if 1,000 L/quarter is realistic. Link RBC for the 3¢ instant whether or not you hit Platinum.
  • No loyalty grocery home: follow the lowest posted litre within a few minutes of your route. Loyalty is a tie-breaker, not a religion.

A 40-litre fill worksheet you can reuse

Print this or copy it into a note. Use it on the last Sunday of the month.

  1. Write the posted ¢/L at the three nearest majors (and Costco if you have a membership and a queue tolerance).
  2. Add your known stack: PC card ¢/L, RBC 3¢, Shell Go+ 3¢, loaded 10¢/L offer, Fuel Savings card, etc.
  3. Effective price = posted − instant ¢ − (points ¢ you will actually redeem this quarter).
  4. Multiply the gap by 40. If the “winning” station is 10 minutes out of the way, price that detour at your time and extra kilometres. A $1.60 loyalty win that burns $2 of fuel to reach is a loss.
  5. Decide once. Do not re-shop every fill unless a loaded offer is large (10¢/L or a bonus week).

Worked example (labelled): posted Esso $1.499, Petro $1.479, Shell $1.489. You have a PC Mastercard (~3¢/L in points) and no RBC or Go+ link. Effective: Esso ~$1.469, Petro $1.479, Shell $1.489. Esso wins by 1.0–2.0¢/L even though the sign was highest — if you value PC points at 0.1¢ and will redeem them. If your PC balance is a graveyard, Petro’s posted $1.479 is the honest winner.

Offers to load weekly so you do not miss bonus burns

Park a two-minute pump ritual next to the grocery offer-load:

  • PC Optimum: Esso personalized offers and 4,000-point 10¢/L burns on the first 40 L.
  • Petro-Points: app bonuses and Fuel Savings Reward Cards (public stacks with RBC can reach double-digit ¢/L on a planned fill — only if you already stored the points).
  • Scene+ / Shell Go+: check for 2× point weekends and linked-card extras before a long highway trip.

The detour test: if the extra drive uses more than about 1 L of fuel, you have spent the typical weekly bonus. Fill on the commute you already take. Loyalty is a layer on a route, not a reason to create a route.

Gas is a smaller envelope than insurance and parking in the real cost of owning a car. Win the litre, then go home. Do not build a personality around 1¢.

Frequently asked questions

Which Canadian gas loyalty program is best in 2026?

The one that matches the station you already pass and the grocery banner you already shop. Base earn is about 1¢ per litre at Esso (PC Optimum), Petro-Canada (Petro-Points), and Shell (Scene+). Co-branded cards and instant pump discounts move that to roughly 3–7¢/L. A 5¢/L gap on 40 L is $2. Do not drive across town for it if the posted pump price is already 8¢ higher.

How many PC Optimum points do I earn per litre at Esso?

Esso’s public FAQ (reviewed Sep 2026): 10 points per whole litre for members, plus 10 points per dollar on eligible convenience and car-wash purchases. A PC Mastercard typically brings the fuel earn to at least 30 points per litre; PC Insiders World Elite can reach 50–70 points per litre with volume and premium-grade bonuses. Default redemption is 10,000 points = $10, so 10 points/L is about 1¢/L.

What is Petro-Points Platinum?

A frequent-fueller tier Petro-Canada launched 1 January 2026. Public explainers describe a 1,000 litres per quarter threshold for 50% more points (15 points/L instead of 10) plus member offers. Linking Triangle Rewards / CT Money and an RBC card can add instant cents-per-litre. Confirm current thresholds in the Petro-Points app — household pooling ended 15 March 2026.

Did Shell replace Air Miles with Scene+?

Yes. Shell Canada’s public loyalty FAQ now describes Scene+ earn at participating stations: 1 Scene+ point per litre and 1 point per dollar on eligible convenience and car wash, with 1,000 points = $10 off. Shell Go+ can add instant cents off when you link an eligible Scotiabank or Tangerine card. Air Miles is not the 2026 Shell stack.

Should I open a gas credit card just for points?

Only if you already pay the card in full and already buy that brand of fuel. Interest wipes out 7¢/L before the statement posts. Treat PC Financial, RBC Petro, and Scene+ Scotia cards as offer types — compare applications on the issuer’s site. Saving Optimizer is not a card issuer.
