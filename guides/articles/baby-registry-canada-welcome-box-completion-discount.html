Baby Registries in Canada: Welcome Boxes, Completion Discounts and Returns

Register for perks, buy the rest at the discount · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

A baby registry can pay you back. Amazon.ca gives a completion discount of 10%, or 15% with Prime, on items still on your list, plus a welcome gift for Prime members. Walmart Canada offers a welcome box too. Register early, add what you really need, and buy what's left at the discount before the baby arrives.

Key takeaways

  • Amazon.ca completion discount: 10%, or 15% with Prime (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Amazon.ca welcome gift: Prime, 20 items and $25 bought from the registry.
  • Walmart Canada welcome box: 20 unique items, $25 spent, registry open 7 days.
  • Registries help avoid duplicates and make returns easier.
  • Example: $600 left on the list at 15% off saves $90.

Registry perks

Amazon.ca Completion discount 10% (15% with Prime) Eligible items left on the registry

Walmart Canada Welcome box 20 unique items, $25 spent, open 7 days

Duplicates, returns and privacy

  • Mark gifts you already got so people don't buy duplicates.
  • Check each store's return window for registry gifts.
  • Use a delivery address you're comfortable sharing.
  • Keep the list public only to people you invite, if you can.

Savings: an example

Example: $600 of items are still unbought a few weeks before your due date. With a 15% Prime completion discount, you save $90. Add the welcome gift, and the registry pays for itself.

Frequently asked questions

Does Amazon.ca have a baby registry discount?

Yes. The completion discount is 10%, or 15% for Prime members.

How do I get the Amazon.ca baby registry welcome gift?

Be a paid Prime member, add 20 items and buy at least $25 from your registry.

Does Walmart Canada have a baby registry?

Yes, since May 2026, with a welcome box for qualifying registries.

Can I return registry gifts?

Usually, within the store's return window. Check before your shower.

Is a baby registry worth it?

Yes, if you use the perks and buy what's left at the discount.
