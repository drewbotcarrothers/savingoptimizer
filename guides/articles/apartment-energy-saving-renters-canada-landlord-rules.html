Apartment energy saves for Canadian renters (without breaking lease rules)

You do not own the boiler · About 16 minutes · 20 September 2026

Renters feel powerless on January hydro because they do not own the envelope. That is half true. You cannot replace windows. You can stop paying to heat the hallway through a missing door sweep, and you can stop treating a heat-included thermostat like a personal sauna. This is a lease-boundary list — not an OEB plan chooser and not permission to caulk a heritage sash.

If the listing’s real problem is “hydro extra + electric baseboard,” compare units on the heat-included vs hydro worksheet before you buy gadgets. Rate plans, Peak Perks, and heat pumps live under Utilities.

Disclosure: Energy-efficient gadgets (LEDs, power strips, draft stoppers) are an offer type. Saving Optimizer may earn a commission if we later add partner links. We do not currently claim retailer, thermostat-brand, or utility partnerships. This is not legal or energy-contract advice.

Key takeaways

  • Reversible first: LEDs, strips, door snakes, curtains. Written yes for anything that sticks, drills, or replaces controls.
  • Heat-included: etiquette and repair requests. Hydro-extra: timing and setpoint actually hit your bill.
  • Broken seals are maintenance. Photograph and email. A towel under the door is not a work order.
  • Abatement is a tribunal path after documented repair requests — high-level only.
  • Decode the meter story (bulk, submeter, house split) before you celebrate a $40 “hydro” line.

Draft-proofing and curtain tactics that are usually allowed

Air that leaves under the door is air you paid to heat if you pay the fuel. Cheap, reversible tactics:

  • Fabric or foam door snakes on the suite door and on the balcony door. Do not nail a sweep into a metal frame you do not own without asking.
  • Interior storm film that the package says removes cleanly. Test a corner in October, not on move-out day.
  • Heavy curtains or a quilt on a tension rod — especially on single-pane north glass. Close them at dusk; open them on sunny days.
  • Outlet gaskets behind furniture if the wall feels like a breeze. Skip anything that needs an electrician.

If the window itself is failed (condensation between panes, daylight around the sash), that is a repair email, not a $12 film project.

Thermostat etiquette in heat-included buildings

When heat is in the rent, cranking 24°C and opening the bedroom window is a neighbour tax. Superintendents notice. Some leases already set a maximum. You still want the unit habitable — that is maintenance, not a coupon.

When heat is extra and electric, a 1–2°C nighttime setback on your baseboard or thermostat is real money. When heat is extra and gas in a house, the same idea applies, but the furnace is often shared — see roommate splits. Do not rewire a boiler because a podcast said 17°C.

Appliance and laundry timing habits that cut hydro

If you are on Ontario time-of-use or ultra-low overnight, laundry and the dishwasher belong in the cheap window — the Utilities hub explains the clocks. If you are on a flat or tiered bill (BC Hydro, Hydro-Québec Rate D, many condos), timing does less and volume does more: full loads, cold wash, air-dry when the lease and mould risk allow.

Fridge coils, a dryer lint trap, and not running a space heater beside an open window are boring and they work. A second fridge in a locker is a winter hobby, not a saving.

LED and power-strip upgrades with ROI

Labelled sketch, not a rebate form: swapping ten incandescent or tired compact-fluorescent bulbs for LEDs is often a single-season payback at Canadian residential kWh prices, especially if lights stay on in a home-office. Keep the old building bulbs in a labelled bag if they want them back.

A power strip that actually switches off the TV / console / soundbar pile cuts vampire load. A strip you never click is décor. Smart plugs help if you will use a schedule; they are an offer type, not a requirement.

Documenting issues (broken seals, drafty windows) for repairs

  1. Photo in daylight and at night (you can see the street through the sash).
  2. Note dates, outdoor temperature, and which rooms are unusable.
  3. Email the landlord or property manager. Keep the thread. Text-only is how “I told them” dies.
  4. Offer access windows. A refused entry can stall a repair file.
  5. If nothing happens, provincial maintenance and vital-service rules are the next read — LTB, B.C. tenancy, TAL — not a Facebook threat.

Condo renters: the corporation may own the window. Your landlord still has to chase the board. Write both names.

When to ask for a rent abatement vs DIY fixes (high-level)

A door snake is a $20 comfort patch. An abatement is a money claim because the landlord failed to maintain. They are not substitutes.

  • DIY / reversible: drafts you can block without altering the building; lighting; laundry habits.
  • Repair request: heat that does not work, mould from a failed envelope, windows that do not close.
  • Tribunal / board (later): documented requests, no repair, and a statute that talks about maintenance or vital services. High-level only. Keep paying rent unless an order or a specific provincial rule says otherwise.

Personal Finance Canada threads are useful for “ask for bills before you sign” and terrible as a filing kit. Pair this page with submetered hydro if the tower bills you by the kWh.

Frequently asked questions

What energy upgrades can Canadian renters do without asking?

Reversible, non-damaging items are usually safest: LED bulbs in your own lamps, power strips, door snakes, removable draft film that does not wreck frames, and heavier curtains on existing rods. Anything that caulks, drills, replaces a thermostat, or adds a permanent film needs a written yes. Your lease wins.

Does turning the heat down save money if heat is included?

It saves the building, not your rent, unless the suite is submetered. In heat-included buildings the neighbour-tax is blasting a window open in January. If you pay hydro and heat is electric, setpoint and laundry timing hit your bill. Decode the lease first.

Who fixes a drafty rental window?

Broken seals, missing weatherstripping, and dead baseboards are usually the landlord’s maintenance job. Photograph, date, and email a repair request. A door snake is a comfort patch, not a substitute for a written request. Tribunals are a later step.

Can I ask for a rent abatement because the unit is cold?

High-level only: provinces have maintenance and vital-service rules. You document, you request repair, you keep paying rent unless a statute or order says otherwise. Withholding rent because a Facebook group said so is how people lose deposits. LTB / RTB / TAL pages beat a script.

Is this legal or energy-contract advice?

No. It is a renter tactic list. Rate plans, heat pumps, and rebates live in the Utilities guides. Gadgets are an offer type if we later add links. We do not claim thermostat or utility partnerships.
