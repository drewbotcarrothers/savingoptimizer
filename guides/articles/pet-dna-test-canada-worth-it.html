Pet DNA Tests in Canada: Worth the Cost?

Fun, sometimes useful, never a diagnosis · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

A dog DNA test costs about $120 to $165 in Canada. It's a fun way to learn your mixed-breed dog's background, and the health panel can flag genetic risks worth mentioning to your vet. It can't diagnose anything. If your pet has a symptom, spend the money on a vet visit instead.

Key takeaways

  • Wisdom Panel Essential: $164.99 at Amazon.ca (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Embark Breed + Health: US$159, US$119 on sale, billed in US dollars.
  • A genetic risk marker isn't a diagnosis.
  • Talk to your vet before acting on health results.
  • Buy on sale, around Black Friday or Prime Day.

When it's worth it

  • You're curious about breed mix and can afford it.
  • You plan to breed and need genetic screening.
  • Your vet wants a specific genetic result.
  • Not worth it to explain a symptom; see a vet.
  • Not worth it if the result won't change anything you do.

Test vs vet visit: an example

Example: if your dog is limping, a $165 DNA test won't tell you why. That money is better put toward the exam.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a dog DNA test in Canada?

About $120 to $165; Wisdom Panel Essential was $164.99 at Amazon.ca.

Is Embark available in Canada?

Embark ships internationally and prices in US dollars.

Can a DNA test diagnose my pet?

No. A genetic marker shows risk, not disease.

Is a pet DNA test worth it?

If you're curious or breeding. Not to explain a symptom.

When are pet DNA tests on sale?

Often around Black Friday and other big sales.
