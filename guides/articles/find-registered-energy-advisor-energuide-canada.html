How to Find a Registered Energy Advisor for an EnerGuide Evaluation in Canada

Book through the right service organization so your rebate counts · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Registered Energy Advisors (REAs) work through service organizations licensed by Natural Resources Canada (NRCan). Start with your province's or utility's rebate program, because many rebates only count if you book through their approved list. Then compare prices and wait times.

Key takeaways

  • REAs are booked through NRCan-licensed service organizations, not individually.
  • Check your rebate program's approved list before booking.
  • Example prices: $540 initial and $406 follow-up at Reep Green Solutions (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Ontario HRSP pays $600 toward the assessments after two or more upgrades.
  • HRSP's follow-up must be scheduled by 30 Nov 2026 and done by 31 Dec 2026.

Steps to book

  • Check your provincial or utility program for an approved list.
  • Search NRCan's list of licensed service organizations for your area.
  • Ask for the price of the initial and follow-up visits.
  • Ask about wait times and travel fees.
  • Confirm the advisor will file the rebate paperwork.

What happens at the visit

The advisor checks your home from basement to attic and runs a blower-door test. The initial visit takes about 2 hours and you get an EnerGuide rating and a report. The follow-up after upgrades takes about 1 hour.

Net cost: an example

Example: an initial visit at $540 plus a follow-up at $406 is $946. With Ontario's $600 HRSP rebate, the net is $346, but only if you finish two qualifying upgrades in time.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find a Registered Energy Advisor?

How much does an EnerGuide evaluation cost?

Reep Green Solutions charges $540 for the initial visit and $406 for a follow-up (checked 24 Nov 2026).

Is there a rebate in Ontario?

The Home Renovation Savings Program pays $600 after two or more recommended upgrades.

What is the HRSP deadline?
