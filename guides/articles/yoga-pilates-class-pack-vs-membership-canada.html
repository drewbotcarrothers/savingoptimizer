Yoga and Pilates: Class Pack vs Membership vs Drop-In in Canada

Divide the membership price by the per-class price to find how many classes a month you need · About 9 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 1 Oct 2026

Divide the membership price by the price of one class to see how many classes a month you need for the membership to win. At City of Toronto recreation centres, a single drop-in fitness class, which includes yoga and Pilates, is $10.64 for an adult, and a FitnessTO All Access membership is $49.44 a month or $280.33 a year. The monthly membership pays off at about five classes a month, and the yearly one at about two and a half. Private studios charge more, so do the same math with their prices, and check how long a class pack lasts before it expires.

Key takeaways

  • Break-even = membership price ÷ price per class.
  • City of Toronto: $10.64 per drop-in class; All Access $49.44 a month or $280.33 a year.
  • All Access monthly wins at about 4.6 classes a month; yearly at about 2.2.
  • Toronto's free centres charge nothing for drop-in group fitness classes.
  • Studio packs often expire; buy only what you will use in time.
  • Example with made-up inputs: eight classes a month for $49.44 instead of $176.

City of Toronto prices

The City of Toronto says its group fitness programs include aquatic fitness, yoga, Pilates, cardio and more, and lists these adult prices before tax. Youth and older adults aged 60 and over pay about half.

The city also says there are no refunds for memberships or multi-visit passes, that space in drop-in programs is not guaranteed, and that drop-in participants in registered programs are admitted after registered participants if space is available. A surcharge applies to memberships for non-residents.

The 12-month plan costs $280.33, the same as about 26 single classes. If you go at least once a week all year, it is far cheaper than paying per class. The catch is commitment: with no refunds, a year you do not use is money lost. If you are unsure, start with one month and switch to the yearly plan once the habit sticks.

Older adults and youth

The City of Toronto charges youth (13 to 18) and older adults (60 and over) about half the adult price. For them, a single drop-in class is $5.32 and All Access is $24.72 a month, $41.94 for three months or $140.18 a year, before tax. At those prices, the monthly membership still breaks even at about 4.6 classes a month, because both prices are halved, but the dollar amounts at stake are smaller, which makes trying the yearly plan less risky.

Studio packs and memberships

Private yoga and Pilates studios set their own prices and terms, and reformer Pilates usually costs more than mat classes because of the equipment. Studio offers come in three shapes: drop-in, class packs and memberships, often with an introductory deal for new students. Convert each to a per-class price for the number of classes you will really take.

Adding a home practice

If classes are the expensive part, consider mixing them with practice at home. One class a week keeps your form in check, and free videos or a library book cover the other days. That can move you from needing a membership to needing only a pack, or from a studio to the city schedule. A mat and a strap are usually the only gear you need for home yoga.

Steps

  1. Count how many classes you actually took last month, or will realistically take.
  2. Find the per-class price for drop-in, packs and memberships.
  3. Divide each membership price by the per-class price to get the break-even.
  4. Check pack expiry and late-cancel rules.
  5. Try the city schedule or a free centre before buying a studio membership.
  6. Start monthly; switch to yearly once the habit sticks.

Frequently asked questions

Is a yoga membership worth it?

If you take enough classes. Divide the membership price by the per-class price. At City of Toronto prices, the $49.44 monthly All Access pays off at about 4.6 classes a month.

How much is a drop-in yoga class in Toronto?

A City of Toronto single drop-in fitness class, which includes yoga and Pilates, is $10.64 for an adult before tax, as of 1 Oct 2026.

Does FitnessTO include yoga and Pilates classes?

FitnessTO All Access includes drop-in fitness and aquatic fitness classes at City-run locations, and the city lists yoga and Pilates among its group fitness programs.

Can I get a refund on a FitnessTO membership?

No. The City of Toronto says there are no refunds for memberships or multi-visit passes.

Why does reformer Pilates cost more?

It uses equipment, which usually means smaller classes and higher prices than mat classes. Price it per class like any other option.

What should I check before buying a class pack?

The expiry date, which class types are included, late-cancel and no-show rules, and the renewal price after any introductory offer.
