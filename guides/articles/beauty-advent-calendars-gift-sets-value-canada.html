Beauty Advent Calendars and Holiday Gift Sets in Canada: Doing the Value Maths

Value maths for gift sets · About 12 minutes · By Andrew Carrothers · Updated 29 Sep 2026

A holiday set that says it is worth several hundred dollars is a representation about price. It is not a list of what your household will use. This page shows the arithmetic: size in the set, full-size size, full-size price, a pro-rated figure, and a yes or no on whether you would use it. The dollars in the worksheet are an example with made-up inputs. No Shoppers, Sephora, or other calendar price was copied on 29 Sep 2026. This is not a ranking of brands, and it is not legal advice.

Key takeaways

  • The Competition Bureau’s ordinary-price guidelines, dated 16 Oct 2009 and still current when checked 29 Sep 2026, use a volume test or a time test for a represented ordinary price. The Bureau says those guidelines are under review after changes to the Competition Act.
  • Pro-rated value is the full-size price times the sample size divided by the full-size size. Real value to you is that figure only for items you will use. The rest is zero.
  • Black Friday falls on 27 Nov 2026 and Boxing Day on 26 Dec 2026. Those are calendar dates. This guide does not cite a retailer markdown calendar, so it does not say a set will be reduced on either day.
  • Shoppers Drug Mart’s returns page, updated 24 Apr 2026, gives 30 days with a receipt and makes clearance and final-sale items final. It does not mention advent calendars. Check Sephora’s own return policy; no Sephora day count is stated here.

How 'value' claims are calculated

Subsections 74.01(2) and 74.01(3) of the Competition Act, as set out in the Bureau’s ordinary-price guidelines, deal with a representation to the public about the price at which a product is ordinarily supplied. One test is whether a substantial volume was sold at that price or higher within a reasonable period. The other is whether the product was offered in good faith at that price or higher for a substantial period. The guidelines are dated 16 Oct 2009. The page checked 29 Sep 2026 says the Bureau is reviewing them after amendments to the Act, so this article does not treat the 2009 penalty figures as a current maximum. It is not legal advice.

A gift-set line that says the contents are worth a stated sum is the kind of representation to read against those tests, and against the literal impression plus the general impression, which the guidelines say both count. Write down the claimed number. Then rebuild it from full-size prices you can see that week. If you cannot reproduce the claim from prices on offer, the claim is not your worksheet. A cash-back portal or a discounted gift card changes what you pay for the set. It does not change the claimed figure. The gift-card guide is the stacking method. It is not a reason to buy samples you will not open.

Sample-size vs full-size cost per ml

Pro-rated value of one item is the full-size price multiplied by the size in the set, divided by the full-size size. Use millilitres or grams, and do not mix them. Example with made-up inputs. A moisturiser at 15 millilitres in the set, against a 50 millilitre full size priced at 80 dollars, is 80 times 15 divided by 50, which is 24 dollars. A serum at 5 millilitres against 30 millilitres at 90 dollars is 15 dollars. A fragrance at 2 millilitres against 30 millilitres at 150 dollars is 10 dollars. A lip colour at 2 millilitres against 6 millilitres at 36 dollars is 12 dollars. None of those prices was on a shelf.

The seller’s claim in the same example is 400 dollars. The four full-size prices sum to 356 dollars, which is already short of 400. That gap is the point of the worksheet: the claim and the sum you can rebuild are allowed to disagree, and when they do you keep the sum you can rebuild. Cost per millilitre of a sample is not the cost per millilitre of the full size. A tiny bottle can look generous on a door and still be a poor substitute for the bottle you would have bought.

Timing: early release vs Black Friday and Boxing Day markdowns

Record three dates: the day the set appears, the week of Black Friday on 27 Nov 2026, and the week of Boxing Day on 26 Dec 2026. Those two holidays are calendar dates only. The method is to check the same seller again, not to assume a sale.

If the same seller drops the price inside a published price-adjustment window, that is the price-adjustment guide, not a second calendar. If a different seller is cheaper in late November, you do not have an adjustment. You have a new purchase, with its own return rule. The Black Friday and Boxing Day guide is written for appliances. Use its habit of comparing two dated prices. Do not copy an appliance discount into a beauty set.

Gift sets you'd use vs impulse buys

Mark each row yes only if you would buy that item on its own before the full-size bottle’s expiry, in a shade or scent you already know you will finish. A yes on a product you have never tried is a guess. Count it as no, or buy the smallest full size instead of the calendar. Real value to you is the pro-rated figure on the yes rows and zero on the no rows. In the made-up example the yes rows are the moisturiser, the serum, and the lip colour: 24 plus 15 plus 12, which is 51 dollars. The fragrance is a no, so its 10 dollar pro-rated figure does not enter the total.

Compare 51 dollars with the price you would actually pay for the set, not with the 400 dollar claim. If the set costs more than 51 dollars in this example, the claim can be large and the set can still fail. Your numbers replace both. A set you are giving away is a different question: the yes column is what the recipient will use, which you often do not know. When you do not know, the real value is the price of a product you have watched them finish, not the sum of doors.

Splitting sets into multiple gifts

Splitting a set into several gifts can turn one unused calendar into two presents someone will open. It can also end the return. Do it only after you have read whether the seller treats the set as one unit. Shoppers’ returns page does not mention gift sets as their own class. It does say clearance and products marked final sale are final, and that a general refund or exchange needs the original receipt within 30 days. If you break the box before you know that rule, you may be past the only window you had.

Keep the outer box and the receipt until that window ends. Put each item you give away in its own labelled container so the shade name stays with it. Do not decant a sample into an unlabelled jar. The person receiving it cannot see the size or the expiry, and you cannot put the set back together if the return is still open.

Returns and exchanges on gift sets

Ask four questions before you pay, and write the answers on the receipt. Does the seller take the set back if it is unopened? Is the set marked final sale? Can one missing or damaged item be exchanged without returning the whole set? Does opening one door end the return? Shoppers Drug Mart’s page, updated 24 Apr 2026 and checked 29 Sep 2026, answers the general case: receipt, 30 days, clearance and final sale are final, and a named house brand can come back within 30 days if you are not satisfied with the quality, some exclusions applying. It does not answer the advent-calendar questions. No Sephora day count is stated here; check Sephora’s own return policy.

The wider map of what Canadian stores publish is the return-policy guide. A provincial rule is not a change-of-mind right you can assume. If the sticker says final sale, believe the sticker. The Shoppers beauty guide is the points version of the same season. Points on a set you will not use are not a discount.

Frequently asked questions

Are beauty advent calendars worth the money?

Only if the pro-rated value of the items you will use is at least the price you pay. In the worked example, which uses made-up inputs, that figure is 51 dollars against a claimed 400 dollars. A claim you cannot rebuild from full-size prices is not your budget. This guide does not cite a real calendar price.

How is the 'value' of a gift set calculated?

Pro-rate each item: full-size price times the size in the set, divided by the full-size size. Add that figure only for items you will use, and count the rest as zero. A seller's worth-a-stated-sum line is a representation to read against the Competition Bureau's ordinary-price tests. The Bureau's guidelines are dated 16 Oct 2009 and the page says they are under review.

When do gift sets go on sale?

This guide does not cite a Shoppers or Sephora markdown calendar, so it does not name a sale week as a fact. Black Friday is 27 Nov 2026 and Boxing Day is 26 Dec 2026 as calendar dates only. Check the same seller again in those weeks, and use a price-adjustment window only when that seller publishes one.

Can I return a beauty advent calendar?

Only if that seller's page says so, and not when the item is marked final sale. Shoppers' returns page, updated 24 Apr 2026, allows a general refund or exchange with a receipt within 30 days, and it makes clearance and final-sale items final. It does not mention advent calendars. No Sephora day count is stated here.

Is it cheaper to buy the items separately?

Compare the price of the set with the pro-rated value of the rows you marked yes, not with the claimed total. If the separate bottles you would actually finish cost less than the set, buy those bottles. The made-up example puts that usable figure at 51 dollars, which is not a shelf result.
