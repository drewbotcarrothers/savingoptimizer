Auditing Tech Add-On Subscriptions: Protection Plans, Ink and Cloud

Small monthly add-ons add up quietly · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Tech add-ons are easy to forget: a protection plan, a cloud storage tier, an ink subscription. AppleCare+ for iPhone starts at $10.49 a month, iCloud+ at $1.29 and HP Instant Ink at $1.89. Check them once a year. Cancel plans on old devices, share cloud storage with family, and pick an ink plan that matches what you print.

Key takeaways

  • AppleCare+ for iPhone: from $10.49 a month or $104.99 a year (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • iCloud+: $1.29 (50 GB) to $79.99 (12 TB) a month, shareable with five family members.
  • HP Instant Ink: plans from $1.89 a month.
  • Check Apple and Google subscription settings, carrier bills and card statements.
  • Cancel plans for devices you no longer use.

How to audit and cancel

  • List every add-on from Apple, Google, carrier and card statements.
  • Cancel protection on devices you sold or replaced.
  • Downgrade storage after you clear old files.
  • Share one family storage plan.
  • Set a yearly reminder to review.
