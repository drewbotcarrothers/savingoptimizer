Toothpaste: Store Brand vs Name Brand and What Matters (Fluoride)

Any fluoride toothpaste does the main job · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

The thing that matters most in toothpaste is fluoride. A basic fluoride paste, whether it's a store brand or Colgate Cavity Protection at about $1.95 per 100 ml, protects against cavities just as well as a $5-per-100-ml whitening paste. Pay more only for a specific need, like sensitivity, and use the right amount for kids.

Key takeaways

  • The Canadian Dental Association recommends fluoride toothpaste (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Look for fluoride on the label, or the CDA seal.
  • Under 3: a rice-grain smear. Ages 3 to 6: a pea-sized amount.
  • Walmart: Colgate Cavity Protection about $1.95 per 100 ml; Crest 3D White about $5.09.
  • Example year: $9.75 basic vs $25.45 whitening.

What matters

  • Fluoride: the key cavity fighter.
  • Sensitivity paste: worth it if cold or sweet foods hurt.
  • Whitening: removes surface stains only; doesn't change cavity protection.
  • Store brands: fine if they contain fluoride.
  • Amount: a pea-size is enough for adults too.

A year of toothpaste: an example

Frequently asked questions

Is store-brand toothpaste as good?

If it contains fluoride, it does the same main job of preventing cavities.

What does the CDA seal mean?

It marks products the Canadian Dental Association has found meet its standards.

How much toothpaste should kids use?

A rice-grain smear under age 3 and a pea-sized amount from 3 to 6, says the CDA.

Is whitening toothpaste worth it?

It removes surface stains only. It doesn't protect better against cavities.

How much is toothpaste in Canada?

Walmart listed Colgate Cavity Protection 60 ml at $1.17 when checked on 24 Nov 2026.
