Run Ethernet (or MoCA) Before Buying More Wi-Fi Gear: Canadian Home Playbook

Wire the TV and desk first, then fix Wi-Fi for everything else · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Most slow Wi-Fi rooms need one wire, not more gear. Run Ethernet to your TV, console or desk. If you can't run cable, use the coax outlets you already have with MoCA adapters. Then, if you still need mesh, connect the nodes by wire.

Key takeaways

  • 100 ft Cat6 cable: $19.14 to $22.21 at Amazon.ca (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • goCoax MoCA 2.5 adapter: $109.99 each; you need two.
  • TP-Link Deco mesh 3-packs: $139.94 to $179.97.
  • Wire devices that don't move first.
  • Wired backhaul makes mesh far faster.

MoCA setup tips

  • Check both rooms have coax outlets on the same line.
  • Put one adapter by the router and one at the device.
  • Add a PoE filter at the entry point if your provider needs it.
  • Avoid old splitters that block MoCA frequencies.
  • Ask your provider; some modems already support MoCA.

For renters

Flat Ethernet cable tucks under baseboards and door edges with clips. It comes out cleanly when you move.

Cable vs mesh: an example

Example: wiring a home office with one 100 ft cable costs $22.21. A Wi-Fi 6 mesh 3-pack costs $179.97. The cable is faster for that room and costs about $158 less.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ethernet better than mesh Wi-Fi?

For devices that stay put, yes: it is faster and more stable.

How much does a 100 ft Ethernet cable cost?

$19.14 to $22.21 at Amazon.ca when checked on 24 Nov 2026.

What is MoCA?

Adapters that send internet over your home's coax cable.

How much do MoCA adapters cost?

A goCoax MoCA 2.5 adapter was $109.99 each; you need two.

Can renters run Ethernet?

Yes, with flat cable and clips that come off cleanly.
