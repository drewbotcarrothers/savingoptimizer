Mother's Day and Father's Day: A Canadian Cost Guide

Make the day about time, not the receipt · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Mother's Day and Father's Day costs come mostly from restaurant meals and gifts. Mother's Day 2027 is Sunday, 9 May, and Father's Day 2027 is Sunday, 20 June. Set a budget, split it with siblings, and consider a home brunch or a meal on another day, when restaurants don't run holiday set menus. Experiences and homemade gifts often mean more than another item.

Key takeaways

  • Mother's Day 2027: Sunday, 9 May. Father's Day 2027: Sunday, 20 June.
  • Holiday brunch set menus can cost more than regular menus. Book early or celebrate on another day.
  • A home brunch for six can cost a fraction of a restaurant brunch.
  • Split gifts with siblings so each person pays less and the gift is better.
  • Example: a family of six spends $90 at home instead of $300 at a restaurant.

Meals

Restaurants are busiest on these Sundays, and some offer only set menus. Ask for the menu and price when you book, and whether gratuity is added for groups. Alternatives: brunch at home, a picnic, a barbecue, or a restaurant meal the day before or after.

Gifts

  • Experience gifts: a class, a show or a day trip together. See experience gifts.
  • Homemade: a photo book, a cooked meal, a handwritten letter.
  • Time: a day of help with a project or garden.
  • Split a larger gift with siblings.
  • Set a per-person limit and stick to it.

Plan: an example family budget

These are example amounts, not quotes. Six adults celebrate. Option 1: a restaurant brunch at an example $42 a person plus tip, about $300. Option 2: a home brunch with groceries at about $60 and a $30 shared flower bouquet, $90 total.

Restaurant: about $300, or $50 each. Home: $90, or $15 each. Add a shared gift of $90 split six ways, $15 each, and the home option is still far cheaper. For other gift budgets, see adult gift budgets.

Frequently asked questions

When is Mother's Day in 2027?

Sunday, 9 May 2027, the second Sunday in May.

When is Father's Day in 2027?

Sunday, 20 June 2027, the third Sunday in June.

How much should I spend on a Mother's Day gift?

There's no set amount. Set a budget you can afford, and consider splitting a gift with siblings.

Are restaurants more expensive on Mother's Day?

Some offer only set holiday menus, which can cost more. Ask for the menu and price when you book.

What are cheap Father's Day ideas?

A home barbecue, an outing together, a homemade gift or a day of help with a project.
