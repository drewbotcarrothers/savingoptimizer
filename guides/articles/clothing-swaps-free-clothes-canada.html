Clothing Swaps and Buy Nothing Groups in Canada: Free Clothes Without the Awkwardness

Clear rules make free clothes easy · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

A clothing swap is the cheapest way to refresh your wardrobe: you bring clothes you don't wear and take home ones you will, for free. Swaps run at churches, libraries and community centres, and Buy Nothing groups pass clothes between neighbours. Clear ground rules take the awkwardness out, and a charity lined up for leftovers keeps it tidy.

Key takeaways

  • Swaps usually ask for clean, gently used clothes with no rips or stains (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Set rules on what's accepted and how many items to bring and take.
  • Arrange a charity for leftovers before the event.
  • Buy Nothing groups give items free to neighbours.
  • Example: a free swap replaces $150 of shopping.

Ground rules that work

Clothing swap ground rules. As of 24 Nov 2026.
Rule Why it helps
Clean, gently used items only Nobody has to sort through stained clothes
Bring up to an example 10 items Keeps it fair and manageable
Sort by type and size Makes browsing fast
Take what you'll wear Avoids a new pile at home
Leftovers go to a named charity No one takes bags home

Hosting one

  • Book a free room at a library, church or community centre, or use a living room.
  • Borrow racks, mirrors and tables.
  • Promote it through a Buy Nothing group or local social media.
  • Ask a local charity in advance whether it will take leftovers.

Savings: an example

Example: you bring 10 items you don't wear and take home a jacket, two sweaters and jeans you'd otherwise buy for about $150. The swap cost nothing but an example $10 for snacks.

Frequently asked questions

How does a clothing swap work?

People bring clean clothes they don't wear and take home clothes they will, for free.

What can I bring to a clothing swap?

Usually clean, gently used clothing with no rips or stains. Check the event's rules.

What happens to leftover clothes?

Good swaps arrange a charity in advance to take them.

How do I host a clothing swap?

Find a free space, set ground rules, promote it locally and line up a charity for leftovers.

What is a Buy Nothing group?

A local group where neighbours give items away for free.
