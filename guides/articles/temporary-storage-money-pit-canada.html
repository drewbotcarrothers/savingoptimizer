How Canadians Turn Temporary Storage Into a Money Pit—and How to Stop

Give every storage unit an end date · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Storage feels temporary until the bills add up. A 5x10 unit in Toronto had a median standard rate of $170 a month in October 2026, about $2,040 a year. Most people keep units far longer than planned. Set an end date when you sign, visit monthly to decide what goes, and compare the total with what your things are actually worth.

Key takeaways

  • Toronto 5x10 median: $170 a month; 10x10: $316 (FindStorage.ca, October 2026).
  • Move-in deals usually end after a month or two.
  • Set an end date and calendar alerts when you sign.
  • Hold monthly inventory days to sell, donate or keep.
  • Count admin fees, insurance, gas and time.

Ways out

  • Sell furniture and big items from the unit.
  • Donate what won't sell in two weeks.
  • Scan papers and photos, then recycle or keep the originals small.
  • Borrow a friend's basement or garage.
  • Downsize to a smaller unit while you sort.

The true cost: an example

Example: a 5x10 at $170 for 18 months costs $3,060, before gas and time. If the contents would cost $1,500 to replace, it's cheaper to sell now and buy later.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a storage unit in Toronto?

FindStorage.ca put the median 5x10 at $170 a month in October 2026.

Why does storage cost more than I expected?

Move-in deals end, and units are kept longer than planned.

How do I stop paying for storage?

Set an end date, sort monthly, and sell or donate.

Is storage worth it?

Only if the contents are worth more than the total cost.

What fees should I ask about?

Admin fees, locks, insurance and notice to move out.
