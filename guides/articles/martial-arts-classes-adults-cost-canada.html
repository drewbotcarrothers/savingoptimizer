Martial Arts Classes for Adults: A Canadian Cost Guide

Try classes first, then read the contract · About 7 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Adult martial arts classes usually cost a monthly fee or a contract, plus registration, belt or grading test fees, a uniform and sparring gear. Take a trial class first and compare with city recreation programs, which are often cheaper. In Ontario, martial arts schools are personal development services under the Consumer Protection Act: prepaid contracts of $50 or more come with a 10-day cooling-off period and can't run longer than one year.

Key takeaways

  • Ask for the total cost: monthly fee, registration, grading fees, uniform and gear.
  • Take a free or low-cost trial class before signing.
  • Ontario: prepaid contracts of $50 or more have a 10-day cooling-off period, and contracts must end after one year.
  • City recreation and community centre programs are often cheaper than private schools.
  • Example: a year at a private school costs $1,860; a city program costs $600.

Your rights in Ontario

Ontario's Consumer Protection Act treats martial arts schools, gyms and similar services as personal development services. If you prepay $50 or more, you can cancel within 10 days of receiving the written contract. Contracts must end after one year, initiation fees can't exceed twice the annual fee, and the school must offer monthly instalments. Not-for-profits and municipal facilities aren't covered. Our Ontario gym cancellation rights guide has the details. Other provinces have their own rules.

Contract checklist

  • Total cost, including grading and gear.
  • Length of contract and renewal terms.
  • How to cancel, in writing.
  • Freeze options for injury or travel.
  • What happens if the school closes or moves.

Plan: an example

Frequently asked questions

How much do adult martial arts classes cost?

It varies by school and city. Ask for the total including monthly fees, registration, grading tests, uniform and gear.

Can I cancel a martial arts contract in Ontario?

If you prepaid $50 or more, you can cancel within 10 days of receiving the written contract. Contracts must also end after one year.

Are belt tests extra?

Often, yes. Ask about grading fees before you join.

Are city martial arts programs cheaper?

Often. City and community centre programs usually cost less than private schools.

Should I sign a long contract?

Take a trial first, and in Ontario remember contracts can't run longer than one year.
