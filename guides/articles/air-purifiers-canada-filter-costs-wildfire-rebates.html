Air Purifiers in Canada: Filter Costs, Wildfire Season, and Utility Rebates

Budget for filters, not just the purifier · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

A portable HEPA air purifier is most useful in one room you keep closed during wildfire smoke, usually a bedroom. Filters are the hidden cost: over 3 years they can cost more than the purifier. Pick an ENERGY STAR model sized for the room, and check for a utility rebate.

Key takeaways

  • Levoit Core 300: $128.99 at Amazon.ca (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Replacement filter: $39.79 at Amazon.ca.
  • SaveEnergyNB: $75 off ENERGY STAR room air purifiers until 31 Mar 2027.
  • Match the CADR on the box to your room size.
  • Over 3 years, filters can cost more than the unit.

Wildfire-season setup

  • Pick one room to keep as a clean-air room.
  • Close windows and doors.
  • Run the purifier on high when smoke is heavy.
  • Keep a spare filter before smoke season.
  • A box fan with a furnace filter is a short-term backup only.

Rebates

SaveEnergyNB in New Brunswick takes $75 off ENERGY STAR room air purifiers in store until 31 Mar 2027. Other utilities run seasonal rebates, so check yours before buying.

Electricity cost: an example

Example: running at 30 W around the clock uses about 263 kWh a year. At Ontario's 11.2¢ Tiered price, that is about $29 a year.
