Buying LED Bulbs in Canada: Cost per Bulb, Multipacks, and Colour Temperature

Compare cost per bulb, then pick the right colour · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Compare LED bulbs by cost per bulb, then by brightness (lumens) and colour (kelvin). Store-brand multipacks are often the cheapest. Pay more only for dimmable, high-CRI or smart bulbs where you need them.

Key takeaways

  • Great Value 60W-equivalent 4-pack: $8.98 at Walmart, $2.25 a bulb (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Philips UltraDefinition 3-pack: $10.78 at Walmart, $16.48 at Home Depot Canada.
  • A 60W equivalent gives about 800 lumens.
  • 2700K is warm white; 5000K is daylight.
  • Buy dimmable bulbs only for dimmer switches.

Pick the right colour

  • 2700K warm white: bedrooms and living rooms.
  • 3000K soft white: kitchens and bathrooms.
  • 4000K to 5000K: garages, basements and workspaces.
  • Selectable bulbs if you are unsure.
  • Match bulbs within the same room.

Smart bulbs or basic LEDs?

Smart bulbs cost several times more. They pay off only where you will use schedules or away-mode lighting. For most fixtures, a basic LED plus a timer or smart switch is cheaper.

Yearly savings: an example

Example: swapping a 60W incandescent for an 8.5W LED used 3 hours a day saves about 56 kWh a year. At Ontario's 11.2¢ Tiered price, that is about $6.32 a year per bulb.
