Wedding Websites: A Canadian Cost Guide

Get the RSVPs and the details online without paying a cent · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

You don't need to pay for a wedding website. Zola, The Knot and Joy all offer free sites with RSVPs, schedules and registry links. The main paid extra is a custom domain, which in this example costs about $20 a year. Add a password so only guests see your details.

Key takeaways

  • Zola, The Knot and Joy wedding websites are free (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • A custom domain is the main optional paid add-on.
  • Joy charges for optional text and WhatsApp messages and premium invitation designs.
  • Use a password, and keep your home address off the page.
  • Example: $0, or about $20 a year with your own domain.

What to put on it

  • Date, times and venue addresses.
  • Hotel block and travel tips.
  • Dress code and parking.
  • RSVP with meal choices.
  • Registry or cash fund link.

Privacy

Turn on password protection, don't list your home address, and avoid posting the date publicly if your home will be empty that day. Remove the site, or make it private, after the wedding.

Do you need a custom domain?

No. The free web address works fine on invitations, and a QR code makes it easy. If you want a short address, an example $20 a year is a small cost, but remember to let it lapse or cancel auto-renew after the wedding.

Frequently asked questions

Are wedding websites free?

Yes. Zola, The Knot and Joy all offer free wedding websites.

What costs extra on a wedding website?

Mainly a custom domain. Joy also charges for optional text and WhatsApp messages and premium designs.

Do I need a custom domain for my wedding website?

No. The free address works, and a QR code makes it easy to share.

Should my wedding website be private?

Use a password, and keep your home address and other personal details off it.

When should we launch our wedding website?

Around the time you send save-the-dates.
