Costco Canada membership: is it worth it in 2026? Gold Star break-even math

Gold Star fee vs a discounter split-shop · About 9 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 20 Oct 2026

A Costco Canada membership is worth it in 2026 if your unit-price savings, after waste and extra driving, beat the $65 Gold Star fee plus tax. For households that cook at home and finish bulk, that bar is low. For small households without storage or a car, it often is not. Executive ($130) is a separate question: its extra $65 pays back only after about $3,250 of eligible purchases a year.

Key takeaways

  • Gold Star is $65 a year plus applicable tax and includes 2 cards, one for someone in your household (Costco.ca, checked 20 Oct 2026). That is about $1.25 a week.
  • Costco usually wins on bulk pantry, paper goods, and family packs you finish. Discounters often win on small produce runs.
  • Waste and kilometres are the hidden fees. Count them in a 90-day receipt audit.
  • Executive’s extra $65 needs about $3,250 of eligible purchases at 2%. Gas, food court, and pharmacy purchases do not count.
  • Costco’s satisfaction guarantee says it will refund the membership if you are dissatisfied, so a trial is low-risk if you actually check the numbers.

How much does a Costco Canada membership cost in 2026?

Costco.ca lists Gold Star at $65 and Executive at $130 for 12 months, plus applicable sales tax. Both include 2 membership cards: one for you and one for someone in your household at the same address. Upgrading from Gold Star to Executive costs an extra $65 a year, prorated for the months left.

How much do you need to save to break even?

You need a little more than the fee plus tax in documented unit-price wins against your usual discounter, after you subtract food you throw out and the extra trips. $65 a year is about $1.25 a week.

Count only items you would have bought anyway, and compare the unit price with what you would pay at No Frills, FreshCo, Food Basics, or Walmart. A 3 kg tub of yogurt you composted is not a win.

What does Costco usually win (and lose) against a discounter?

Costco is not cheaper on everything. It tends to be cheaper on a list of items you can store and finish, which is why many households split-shop: the warehouse for bulk, a discounter for the rest.

Compare the price you will actually pay. The CRA says basic groceries are zero-rated (0% GST/HST) in every province and territory, while carbonated drinks, candy, and snack foods are taxable. A bulk case of pop carries sales tax; a bag of oats does not.

How do travel, storage, and waste change the math?

A 40-minute round trip once a month is a different cost than a 40-minute trip every Saturday. Fuel, parking, and time all count. If your apartment fridge is small, 2 kg of greens is compost with a membership card attached.

Write a not-list on the freezer door: no giant berries until the last bag is gone, no second ketchup, no produce you will not prep on Sunday. That is the same discipline as food-waste systems.

Is Costco worth it for a single, a couple, or a family of four?

  • Single: Gold Star is a maybe. The second card is for someone in your household at the same address, so you may be able to share it with a partner or a parent you live with. Buy pantry, coffee, and frozen protein. Skip bulk produce.
  • Couple: Often yes if you cook most nights and live near a warehouse. A monthly warehouse trip plus a weekly discounter run is a common pattern.
  • Family of four: Often yes for pantry, school snacks you will actually pack, paper, and meat, with a snack cap so the haul does not eat the win. Details in family grocery budgeting.

Do gas, optical, and other add-ons make it worth it?

Costco gas is for members only and is offered at select warehouses. If a station is on your route, log your fills against your usual station for a month before you count it as membership value. Gas does not earn the Executive 2% Reward (Costco’s terms exclude purchases at its gas stations), so treat it as Gold Star value. More in does Costco gas make the membership worth it.

Optical, hearing, pharmacy, and tires can cover the fee in one visit, or not, if your benefits plan already covers a local shop. Get a quote. The food court is lunch, not a rebate. Check which payment methods your warehouse accepts before your first trip; see cards by banner.

How do you run a 90-day trial audit?

  1. Join Gold Star only. Do not upgrade to Executive on day one.
  2. Keep every receipt. Mark each line: would I have bought this at No Frills, FreshCo, or Food Basics, and at what unit price? Be honest about “would have.”
  3. Log thrown-out Costco food separately. Subtract it.
  4. Log extra kilometres versus your usual store.
  5. At day 90: (unit-price wins) − (waste) − (travel cost) − (a quarter of the $65 fee). If the number is not clearly positive, ask about the satisfaction guarantee or stop going.
  6. Only then open the Executive vs Gold Star math.

Is Executive worth it in year one?

Usually not. The upgrade costs an extra $65 a year, and the 2% Reward covers that only after about $3,250 of eligible purchases a year ($65 ÷ 2%), or about $271 a month. The reward is capped at $1,250 per 12 months. Costco’s terms exclude purchases at its gas stations, food courts, and pharmacies, plus tobacco, services, and taxes.

Executive also adds a $10 monthly credit on one Costco Same-Day or Costco via Instacart order with a $150 basket minimum. It only helps if you already order there. Start with Gold Star, check a few months of receipts, then decide.

When should you skip membership entirely?

  • No car, no nearby warehouse, and no one in your household to share the second card.
  • You eat mostly fresh produce in small quantities and dislike leftovers.
  • The nearest warehouse is a weekend outing that always includes unplanned non-food buys.
  • You mainly want the food court. That is entertainment; budget it as entertainment.
  • Northern and remote prices and selection are a different market, so do not copy a big-city bulk strategy.

Costco is a tool for households that already cook and already finish bulk. It is not cheaper than a discounter on every item. Run 90 days of receipts, then renew or walk.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a Costco Canada membership in 2026?

Costco.ca lists Gold Star at $65 a year and Executive at $130 a year, plus applicable sales tax (checked 20 Oct 2026). Both include 2 membership cards, one of them for someone in your household. Fees can change, so confirm at the membership counter.

How much do I need to save for Gold Star to pay off?

A little more than $65 plus tax a year, about $1.25 a week, in unit-price savings on things you would have bought anyway, after you subtract food you throw out and extra driving.

Is Gold Star worth it for a single person?

Sometimes, if you finish bulk pantry, coffee, and frozen goods and live near a warehouse. Often not, if produce wilts, meat will not fit in a small freezer, or the trip is an hour. Run a 90-day receipt audit before you renew.

Does Costco beat No Frills on everything?

No. Costco often wins on bulk pantry items, family packs of meat, large-format dairy, and paper goods you finish, and often loses on a small produce run or a one-off sauce. Compare unit prices on the items you actually buy.

Should I get Executive in year one?

Usually no. The extra $65 needs about $3,250 of eligible purchases a year at 2% to pay for itself, and purchases at Costco gas stations, food courts, and pharmacies do not count. Start with Gold Star and check your receipts first.

Can I get my membership fee back if Costco is not worth it?

Costco.ca says its 100% satisfaction guarantee lets it cancel and refund your membership at any time if you are dissatisfied. Check the current terms at the membership counter before you rely on it.
