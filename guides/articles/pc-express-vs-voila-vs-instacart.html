PC Express vs Voilà vs Instacart: when Canadian grocery delivery is worth the fees

Loblaw vs Sobeys ecosystems, not U.S. Instant Cart advice · About 16 minutes · 16 Sep 2026

Convenience fees and substitutions silently erase discounter savings. A $8 delivery charge on a $90 No Frills cart is a 9% surcharge before tips, bags, or a swapped avocado. This comparison is PC Express (Loblaw) versus Voilà (Sobeys / Empire) versus Instacart — Canadian ecosystems, not a U.S. Instant Cart roundup.

Disclosure: PC Insiders Mastercard (Express Pass), Scene+ co-branded cards, and grocery gift cards are offer types. Saving Optimizer may earn a commission if we later add partner links. We do not currently claim those partnerships. Run the fee audit with your own last five invoices.

Key takeaways

  • Pickup at the banner you already shop is usually cheaper than any delivery pass.
  • PC Express Pass is commonly $99.99/year. It pays when you were already paying slot fees every week.
  • Voilà is a fulfilment-centre product with Scene+ and typically no tip. Coverage is city-limited (Ontario, Quebec, Alberta pockets — check the site).
  • Instacart’s silent cost is markup plus tip. Use it for a store the others do not offer.
  • Produce you care about, and Flashfood, stay in-store.

Independent testers (Just Keeping It Real, 2026) found Instacart more expensive than store apps on matched baskets, with faster arrival as the trade. That matches the fee stack, not a morality tale.

PC Express pass / Insiders break-even

Without a pass, a weekly future pickup at $1 is about $52 a year — cheaper than $99.99. Same-day delivery once a week at ~$10 is about $520 a year. The pass is a delivery habit product.

Break-even sketch (labelled): $99.99 ÷ $8.99 same-day fee ≈ 11 deliveries a year if that is the slot you would have paid. If you only pickup, the pass is harder to justify unless promotional ($19.99-style offers appear on RFD and partner sites — read the fine print).

The PC Insiders World Elite Mastercard ($120 fee) bundles an Express Pass. If you already wanted that card for 40 points per $1 at grocery, the pass is included value. If you do not want the card, buy the pass separately. Details in maximize PC Optimum. Note: starting some Express Pass trials can opt you out of a legacy PC Optimum Insiders subscription permanently.

Voilà and Scene+ considerations

Voilà is Empire’s e-commerce grocery: Sobeys / Safeway / IGA-branded depending on province, packed in a warehouse, delivered in a temperature-controlled truck. Scene+ help pages: link the membership, earn on eligible purchases, redeem 1,000 points = $10 at checkout. Some in-store extras (seniors days, certain stamps, some bonuses) do not carry over — read the regional FAQ.

Delivery Pass options on Voilà’s public page (reviewed Sep 2026) include 1-month Anytime at $9.99, 12-month Anytime at $99.99, a midweek pass from $8.99/month or $89.99/year, and occasional 50%-off annual promos. A $35 order minimum applies to pass orders in Ontario and Quebec per that page. Coverage is postal-code gated. If Voilà does not serve you, this column is theoretical.

Shelf strategy still applies: Voilà is not a discounter. FreshCo in person can beat a pretty Voilà cart after the fee. Use Voilà when Empire is already your banner and time is the scarce resource. Scene+ accelerators on a Scotia Amex still fail at Loblaw tills — they can work in this Empire lane; see cards by banner.

Instacart surge and tip realities

Instacart’s product is any store on the map, including Costco in some cities (membership rules still apply). Costs: delivery fee, service fee, often marked-up item prices, and a tip. Membership waives some delivery over a minimum. It does not make Costco Instant-cheap if the app added 10% to Kirkland.

Surge periods (bad weather, dinner) raise fees. Substitutions from a gig shopper are a quality risk on produce. If you use Instacart, lock “refund if unavailable” on sensitive items and compare five staples to Flipp before checkout.

Pickup vs delivery decision tree

  1. Can you stop at the store this week? Prefer in-store or Express pickup. Cheapest, best produce control, Flashfood possible.
  2. Is time the constraint, not weather, and you already shop Loblaw? PC Express pickup, then delivery only if the slot is free under a pass you already pay for.
  3. Is Empire your banner and Voilà serves your postal code? Voilà when you want FC accuracy and no-tip culture.
  4. Do you need a store neither ecosystem covers? Instacart, with a markup check.
  5. Is this a $40 convenience cart? You are paying restaurant-adjacent prices. Name it that.

Items that should stay in-store (produce, Flashfood)

Berries, avocados, herbs, and bakery you are picky about: your eyes beat a shopper. Flashfood is an in-store zone. Price matching with a Flipp screenshot still happens at a human till — see Won’t Be Beat. Online grocery is for oats, cans, milk, and household paper you already know.

Monthly fee audit worksheet

  1. Export or photograph the last four weeks of Express / Voilà / Instacart invoices.
  2. Sum: delivery, service, bags, tips, and any visible markup versus a Flipp staple list.
  3. Divide by four. That is your weekly convenience tax.
  4. If it exceeds a discounter trip’s time-cost in your life, keep the pass. If it is $60 a month on $400 of groceries, you are funding a driver instead of a discounter gap.
  5. Revisit when a promo pass price appears — then cancel if you slip back to one order a month.

Delivery is a tool for weeks you cannot shop. It is a leak when it becomes the default on a cart you could have picked up for a dollar.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a PC Express Pass?

Public 2026 explainers list $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year. The PC Insiders World Elite Mastercard bundles a pass (listed around $99.99 in annual value). Promotional first-year prices appear often — read the offer you are actually buying. Fees at checkout still depend on slot and order minimums.

Does Voilà use in-store prices?

Voilà is fulfilled from dedicated centres, not a random store shelf. Public FAQs say flyer prices are what you pay at checkout, and pricing is similar to the related banner, with some in-store offers missing. There is typically no gig-worker tip. Confirm in your postal code.

Why is Instacart often more expensive?

Delivery fee plus a service fee plus item markups plus an expected tip. An Instacart+ membership (often $99/year) can waive some delivery over a minimum but usually not markup or tip. Use Instacart when you need a store the others do not cover — not as a default discounter.

Is pickup worth it without a pass?

Often yes. A $1 PC Express future pickup fee on a $100 list is cheap compared with a same-day delivery slot. Pickup still misses Flashfood zones and some produce you wanted to handle. Hybrid: pickup staples, in-store for meat and fruit.

Do I still earn PC Optimum or Scene+ online?

PC Express is the Loblaw ecosystem — load offers as you would in store. Voilà earns Scene+ when the account is linked; redeem 1,000 points = $10 on eligible orders. Instacart may pass some loyalty at some banners — do not assume. Check the checkout screen.
