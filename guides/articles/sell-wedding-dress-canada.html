Selling a Wedding Dress: A Canadian Cost Guide

Pick the selling route that leaves the most money in your pocket · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

You can sell a wedding dress online, through a consignment shop or locally. Online marketplaces like Stillwhite charge a listing fee and a commission, consignment shops typically keep half, and a local sale keeps the most but takes more of your time. Price it 40% to 80% below what you paid, and have it cleaned first.

Key takeaways

  • Stillwhite: $25 listing plus a 7% success fee, plus payment processing (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Sixpence Bridal, a consignment boutique, pays sellers 50% of the sale price.
  • A local Marketplace sale has no fees, but you handle the buyers.
  • Price 40% to 80% off original retail, and include the receipt if you have it.
  • Example: a $1,000 sale keeps about $875 online, $500 on consignment, $1,000 locally.

Before you list

  • Dry-clean the dress; consignment shops require it.
  • Take clear photos in daylight, front, back and details.
  • List the designer, style name, size, alterations and original price.
  • Keep the original receipt; it helps prove value.

Pricing

The Wedding Swap suggests pricing 40% to 80% off original retail, and notes most popular dresses sell for under $1,000. Newer styles in common sizes sell faster.

Example payouts

Example: a dress sells for $1,000. Stillwhite: $1,000 minus $25 listing, $70 success fee and about $30 payment fee leaves about $875, before postage. Consignment at 50% leaves $500. A local sale leaves $1,000.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I sell my wedding dress in Canada?

Online on Stillwhite, through a bridal consignment shop, or locally on Facebook Marketplace and similar sites.

How much does Stillwhite charge?

A $25 Standard listing fee plus a 7% success fee, plus payment processing, when checked on 24 Nov 2026.

How much do consignment shops pay?

Sixpence Bridal pays sellers 50% of the sale price. Other shops set their own splits.

How much should I ask for my wedding dress?

A common guide is 40% to 80% off what you paid.

Do I need to clean my dress before selling?

Yes for most consignment shops, and it helps any sale.
