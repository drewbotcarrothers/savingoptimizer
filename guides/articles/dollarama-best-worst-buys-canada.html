Dollarama: best and worst buys in Canada

Small packs, usable units and a list before you shop · About 8 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Dollarama is useful when you need a small quantity of something simple. It is less useful when a low sticker distracts you from a small bottle, a thin bag or a purchase you did not need. The best buy is the item that does the job at the lowest usable-unit cost; the worst is a bargain that stays in the drawer.

Key takeaways

  • Dollarama’s Canadian price points run up to $5 in its September 2026 financial statements. It is not a one-dollar store.
  • Start with gift wrap, plain storage and simple consumables you actually need. Check dimensions and count.
  • Compare per bag, per square metre, per 100 mL or per battery of the same chemistry.
  • For personal care, read the Canadian label and check regulated products and recalls. Price does not replace those checks.
  • The product snapshots below are not a promise of local stock or a test of quality.

Best places to start, with limits

Gift wrap and party basics: buy the quantity for this event. A plain roll can work across birthdays and holidays. A larger multipack is not cheaper if the spare designs go unused. Compare square metres for wrap and actual count for bags, tissue and cards.

Storage and stationery: measure the drawer first. A basket that fits is useful; three almost-right baskets are clutter. Check handles, lids and material before paying. For pens, tape and adhesives, a small pack can be sensible when you will not finish the warehouse version.

Simple household consumables: garbage bags, foil, sponges and cleaning cloths deserve a unit-price check. Match bag dimensions and thickness, foil width and area, and sponge count. Laundry products need a dose comparison; advertised loads are not a standard measure of cleaning performance.

What to skip or investigate more carefully

  • Unplanned seasonal extras: an inexpensive item is still the full price if it was not on the list.
  • Small toiletries with a high unit cost: compare per 100 mL or 100 g. A larger supermarket bottle on sale can win.
  • Anything with unclear safety or compatibility information: especially electrical accessories, child-safety products and regulated health products. Use the required Canadian certification or product identifier and official recall search.
  • Food or personal care you cannot finish: check package condition, storage and expiry where relevant. Do not buy an unfamiliar bulk case to save a few cents.
  • Battery packs compared only by count: carbon-zinc and alkaline are not interchangeable performance claims. Follow the device instructions and check the date.

How to compare Dollarama with Walmart and Costco

Use the three-banner household guide for the full worksheet. Costco’s fee matters only if this shopping decision causes you to buy a membership; storage and waste always matter. Walmart’s rollback may beat either store for one week. None of those facts makes one banner the permanent winner.

Example: a $3 pack of 20 bags is 15¢ a bag; a $5 pack of 50 is 10¢. If the larger bags do not fit your bin, the lower arithmetic price is irrelevant. These are example inputs, not Dollarama shelf prices.

A five-minute shopping rule

  1. Write the item and quantity before entering.
  2. Read the metric size, count and material.
  3. Compare a matched item at the store you already use.
  4. Check any safety identifier, expiry and recall status before choosing a health or electrical product.
  5. Leave the extra stop out if the saving is smaller than its time and travel cost.

Frequently asked questions

Is everything at Dollarama one dollar?

No. Dollarama’s September 2026 financial statements describe Canadian fixed price points up to $5. The shelf sticker is not a unit-price comparison.

Which Dollarama buys are worth checking first?

Low-risk items you need in small quantities: gift wrap, simple storage, stationery and household consumables. Compare dimensions, count, material and usable quantity rather than assuming the banner is cheapest.

Are Dollarama toiletries safe?

Check the Canadian label, ingredients, warnings, size, expiry where relevant, and any DIN or NPN against Health Canada’s databases. A low price is not evidence of a recall or a guarantee of safety.

Are the online prices the same as my store?

Not necessarily. The price snapshots here name their source and date. Delivery-platform prices, fees, stock and local shelf prices can differ. Confirm your own store before buying.

Should I buy cheap carbon-zinc batteries instead of alkaline?

Not just because the pack is cheaper. Match battery chemistry and device requirements, check expiry and compare expected use. This guide does not claim a tested runtime advantage for any pack.
