Price Match Etiquette at Canadian Grocery Checkouts (Get the Saving, Keep the Peace)

Be ready, be quick, and know the store's rules · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Price matching is fine, as long as you're ready. Have each competitor's flyer open on your phone before you reach the till, put the matched items first on the belt, and tell the cashier how many you have. Match only identical items, know each store's rules, and if a match is refused, ask once for a manager and then let it go.

Key takeaways

  • Have flyers open before you reach the till.
  • Put matched items first and say how many.
  • Match the same brand and size only.
  • Know the rules: Walmart Canada doesn't match competitors; Giant Tiger and FreshCo beat by 1 cent (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Example: four matches a week save about $5.

At the till

  • Use a staffed checkout, not self-checkout.
  • Shop at a quieter time if you have many matches.
  • Group matched items at the front of the belt.
  • Show one flyer at a time, with the price, size and dates visible.
  • Accept quantity limits without arguing.

Store rules at a glance

Grocery price match rules. Checked 24 Nov 2026. As of 24 Nov 2026.
Store Rule
No Frills Matches major supermarket competitors in the store's trade area
FreshCo Beats competitor flyer prices by 1 cent
Giant Tiger Beats local competitor ads by 1 cent; no private label or loose produce
Save-On-Foods Matches identical items in competitor flyers; not for online orders
Walmart Canada No competitor ad match since 2020

If the answer is no

Ask politely whether a manager can check. If the answer is still no, accept it, buy the item elsewhere next time, and ask the store for its list of competitors so you know for next week.

For store details, see No Frills Won't Be Beat and a Flipp price matching system.

Frequently asked questions

Is it rude to price match at the grocery store?

No, if you're ready and quick. Have flyers open and group the items.

Can I price match at self-checkout?

Usually not. Use a staffed till.

Does Walmart Canada price match?

Not competitor ads. Walmart ended its ad match program in 2020.

What if the cashier refuses a price match?

Ask once for a manager, then accept the decision.

Which grocery stores beat competitor prices?

FreshCo and Giant Tiger beat eligible competitor flyer prices by 1 cent.
