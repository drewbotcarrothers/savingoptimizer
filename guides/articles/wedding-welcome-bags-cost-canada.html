Welcome Bags: A Canadian Cost Guide

One bag per room, filled with things travellers actually use · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Welcome bags are a nice touch for out-of-town guests, but they're optional. Give one per hotel room, not one per guest, and fill it with water, snacks, a local treat and the weekend schedule. In this example that's about $9 a bag, or $271 for 30 rooms, compared with $497 if you made one for each of 55 guests.

Key takeaways

  • Welcome bags are optional, usually for out-of-town guests.
  • One bag per room costs much less than one per guest.
  • Dollarama kraft paper bags: $1.25 for a 2- or 3-pack (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Ask the hotel whether it charges to hand out bags.
  • Example: $9.03 a bag; 30 rooms $271, 55 guests $497.

Hotel logistics

  • Ask the front desk whether it hands out bags at check-in and whether there's a fee.
  • Label each bag with the guest's name.
  • Drop off bags a day before the first guests arrive.
  • Skip perishable items unless they'll be handed out the same day.

Ways to save

  • One bag per room or household.
  • Buy snacks in bulk at a warehouse store.
  • Replace the printed schedule with a QR code to your wedding website.
  • Skip the bags and host a casual welcome drink instead.

Frequently asked questions

Are wedding welcome bags necessary?

No. They're a courtesy for out-of-town guests, not an expectation.

Who gets a welcome bag?

Usually out-of-town guests staying at the hotel block.

How much do welcome bags cost?

In this example, about $9 a bag. Giving one per room keeps the total down.

What goes in a wedding welcome bag?

Water, snacks, a local treat and the weekend schedule are the basics.

Do hotels charge to hand out welcome bags?

Some do. Ask the front desk before you drop them off.
