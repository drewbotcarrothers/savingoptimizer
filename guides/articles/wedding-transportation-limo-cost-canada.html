Wedding Transportation: A Canadian Cost Guide

The minimum hours set the real price, so plan the timeline first · About 6 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

Wedding transportation in Toronto usually costs $80 to $300 an hour, depending on the vehicle, plus gratuity and HST. Most companies set minimum bookings of 3 to 5 hours on wedding days, so the minimum, not the hourly rate, sets your real price. A stretch limo for a Saturday wedding comes to about $960 in this example; a sedan for just the couple, about $205.

Key takeaways

  • Stretch limos: about $115 to $150 an hour in Toronto (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Weekend wedding minimums are often 5 hours; weekdays 3.
  • Gratuity, HST, waiting time and 407 tolls are usually extra.
  • A coach bus can move 48 guests for $150 an hour (5-hour minimum).
  • Example: Saturday stretch limo $960; sedan for the couple $205.

Plan: an example

Example: a Saturday stretch limo at $150 an hour for the 5-hour minimum is $750. Add an example 15% tip ($112.50) and 13% HST on the base ($97.50): $960. A Towncar for the couple only, 2 hours at $80, is $160, or $205 with the same tip and HST.

Ways to save

  • Book only the legs you need: getting to the ceremony, or the exit from the reception.
  • Hold the ceremony and reception at the same venue.
  • Use a coach bus for guests instead of several limos.
  • Ask whether the quote includes gratuity, HST, tolls and waiting time.
  • Borrow a friend's nice car for photos.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a wedding limo cost in Toronto?

Stretch limos listed $115 to $150 an hour, with 3- to 5-hour minimums, when checked on 24 Nov 2026. Tax and tip are extra.

What is the minimum booking for a wedding limo?

Often 5 hours on weekends and 3 hours on weekdays.

Is gratuity included in limo prices?

Usually not. Ask for an all-in written quote.

How can I get guests to the venue cheaply?

A coach bus can carry 48 guests for $150 an hour with a 5-hour minimum at the company checked.

Do I need a limo at my wedding?

No. Many couples use their own car, a friend's car or a single sedan.
