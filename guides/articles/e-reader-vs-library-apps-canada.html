E-Readers vs Library Apps: Reading for Less

Start with your library card, then pick a device that works with it · About 5 minutes · By Andrew · Updated 24 Nov 2026

The cheapest way to read in Canada is your library card. Library apps like Libby lend e-books and audiobooks free on a phone or tablet. If you want an e-reader, pick one that borrows library books: Kobo e-readers do, starting at about $180. Kindles can't borrow library e-books in Canada.

Key takeaways

  • Library apps lend e-books and audiobooks free with a library card.
  • Kobo e-readers borrow library e-books directly.
  • Kindles can't borrow library e-books in Canada.
  • Kobo Clara BW: $179.98; Kindle 16 GB: $189.99 (checked 24 Nov 2026).
  • Example: three years of buying books costs far more than a device.

Getting more from your library

  • Get a free card at your local library.
  • Place holds on popular titles early.
  • Check if your library has more than one app.
  • Use audiobooks for commutes.
  • Buy only books you'll reread.

Costs over time: an example

Example: buying 2 e-books a month at $12 for three years is $864, or $1,044 with a $179.98 Kobo. Borrowing only from the library on that Kobo costs $179.98.

Frequently asked questions

Can I borrow library e-books on a Kobo?

Yes. Kobo e-readers borrow library e-books directly.

Can I borrow library e-books on a Kindle in Canada?

No. Libby's Kindle option works only in the US.

Is Libby free?

Yes, with a library card from a participating library.

How much is a Kobo in Canada?

Kobo Clara BW was $179.98 at Amazon.ca when checked on 24 Nov 2026.

Do I need an e-reader?

No. Library apps work on phones and tablets.
