Spotify vs Apple Music in Canada: Individual, Student, and Family Plan Math

CAD list prices, student seats, and the family break-even · About 15 minutes · 25 Sep 2026

Two music apps in one household is usually one app too many. Spotify’s Canada price page, read 25 Sep 2026, lists Individual at $13.99 a month, Duo at $19.99, Family at $23.99, and Student at $6.39. Apple’s Canada music page, read the same day, lists Student at $5.99, Individual at $11.99, and Family at $19.99. The cheaper plan is the one that matches who actually presses play, not the one with the longer feature list. Prices move. The account page is the bill. These figures are the public lists on that read.

Podcasts you could hear free, and a library card that already borrows albums, belong in the math before a second subscription does. Cancel and downgrade steps for Spotify are the Spotify cancel guide. The free-stack test is the paid-or-not guide. A quarterly pass over every charge is the subscription audit.

Disclosure: Music gift cards and student-discount portals are offer types only. Saving Optimizer does not claim a partnership with Spotify, Apple, a gift-card seller, or a student portal. A gift card is a way to pay. It is not a reason to keep two apps. Education only. Not a ranking of cards or portals.

Key takeaways

  • List prices read 25 Sep 2026, before any tax your account adds: Spotify Individual $13.99, Duo $19.99, Family $23.99, Student $6.39. Apple Music Student $5.99, Individual $11.99, Family $19.99.
  • Spotify Student is not offered to users in Quebec on the student page we read, lasts up to four years, and moves to Individual at $13.99 when eligibility ends. Apple’s student price is up to 48 months, then the individual price.
  • Two full-price listeners already beat two Individual plans on both brands. A couple on Spotify should price Duo at $19.99 before Family at $23.99. An eligible student plus one Individual can still be cheaper than Family.
  • Spotify includes 15 hours of audiobook time for the Individual plan and for the Duo or Family manager, not for student plans and not automatically for other members. Podcasts play on the free tier.
  • Library Hoopla is a partial substitute with a monthly cap set by your library. Cancel or downgrade before the renewal if the second app was only overlap.

Compare CAD individual prices and feature differences that matter day to day

Start with the bill, then the two features you will notice on a weekday. Spotify’s premium page says every Premium plan includes ad-free music, offline downloads, playing songs in any order, lossless audio, listening with friends in real time, and a queue you can organize. The free plan does not include those. Apple’s page says the catalogue is ad-free on every Apple Music plan, with offline downloads, lossless and hi-res lossless, Spatial Audio, lyrics, and the Apple Music Classical app included at no extra charge. Both will play in the car. The day-to-day difference for a middle-aged household is usually whose phone already holds the playlists, and whether anyone needs lossless or Classical as a separate habit. It is rarely a catalogue war.

Individual, a year at the list prices we read: Spotify $13.99 × 12 = $167.88. Apple $11.99 × 12 = $143.88. That is $24 a year, $2 a month, before tax. If one person already lives in Apple’s apps and the other does not care, Apple Individual is the smaller bill. If the household’s playlists, Blend, and podcast habit are on Spotify, $2 a month is not a reason to move everyone. Do not pay both Individuals “for the features.” Pick the app people open.

Spotify also sells Duo at $19.99 for two people at the same address, each with a separate account. Two Spotify Individuals are $27.98. Duo saves $7.99 a month, $95.88 a year. Apple has no Duo on the price answer we read. Two Apple Individuals are $23.98, and the Family plan is $19.99, so the couple plan on Apple is Family. Confirm the tax line on the account. These list prices are what the public pages stated. They are not a quote with GST or HST added.

Student plans: eligibility and when to graduate to Family instead

Spotify’s student page says you must be over 18 and enrolled at an eligible accredited institution, verified through SheerID, for up to four years. The charge is $6.39 a month. When you graduate or leave, access can continue for up to 12 months from the date you subscribed or last re-verified, and then the plan switches to Individual at $13.99. The same page’s offer line says it is not available to users who have already tried Premium, or to users in Quebec. Read the checkout. A Quebec student who only sees Individual should not expect the $6.39 button to appear. Student plans do not include the audiobook listening time that Individual includes. Re-verify when Spotify asks. Missing the window is how a student price becomes $13.99.

Apple’s support page, published 17 Nov 2025, says a verified student in a degree-granting college or university can have the student price for up to 48 months, and must re-verify. If you do not re-verify, or you are no longer a student, the plan renews as Individual. The music page states the student price at $5.99 and says students get the same Apple Music features as individual members, with Classical included. The marketing line also mentions Apple TV with the student offer. Confirm that on the student checkout you are shown. Do not budget for a TV bundle that the payment sheet does not list.

Graduate to Family when the student price is gone and at least two people would otherwise pay Individual. While the student price still exists, do the addition. Spotify: Student $6.39 plus Individual $13.99 is $20.38, which is less than Family at $23.99 and $0.39 more than Duo at $19.99. Duo does not keep the student discount, so a couple who still qualifies for Student should compare $20.38 with Duo before they merge. Two eligible students at $6.39 each are $12.78, far under Duo or Family. Apple: Student $5.99 plus Individual $11.99 is $17.98, which is less than Family at $19.99. Two Apple students are $11.98. Move to Family when those student seats have become full Individual prices, not on the day someone in the house turns eighteen and still has a student email.

Family plan break-even: how many active listeners justify the upgrade

Count people who press play in a normal week. A login that exists for a teen who uses YouTube in the car is not a listener. Spotify Family is $23.99 for up to six people who live at the same address, one bill to the plan manager. Two Individuals are $27.98, so the second full-price adult already justifies leaving Individual, but Duo at $19.99 is the cheaper two-person product. Family beats Duo when a third person listens, or when you need managed accounts for listeners under 13, under 14 in Quebec. Three Spotify Individuals are $41.97. Family saves $17.98 a month against that, $7.996 per person if all three listen. Six Individuals are $83.94. Family is $23.99, about $4 a person, only if six people actually listen. Empty seats are not savings. They are a plan you bought for a headcount.

Apple Family is $19.99 for up to six people through Family Sharing, you plus five. Two Individuals are $23.98, so two active listeners are the break-even: Family saves $3.99 a month. Three Individuals are $35.97 against $19.99. The support page says that if a family member already has an individual or student subscription, that subscription’s renewal is cancelled when the organizer subscribes to Family. You should not keep paying the old Individual the following month. Check the subscriptions list anyway. Spotify’s support page says plan members can only switch plans once every 12 months. Do not shuffle a teen off and on as a savings trick. The address check is real: members confirm they live at the same address. A student in another city is not a Family seat on the Spotify page we read. They stay on Student or Individual until they move home.

One bill does not mean the cost is shared fairly. The manager pays $23.99 or $19.99. If you split only among people who listen, write the split down. A thirteen-year-old with a managed Spotify account is a listener the manager is choosing to fund, which is fine, and it is not a reason for every adult to pay an equal share of a plan they did not ask for.

Podcasts and audiobooks overlays—what you already get free vs paid

Spotify’s free plan plays podcasts. Premium’s comparison table advertises ad-free music, not an ad-free promise for every podcast. If the household’s Spotify time is shows, the Premium upsell is the wrong product. Apple Podcasts and CBC Listen are free. The decision to drop Premium when the last three months were mostly podcasts is the free-stack guide.

Audiobooks are the overlay people miss. Spotify’s premium FAQ says an Individual plan includes 15 hours a month from the subscriber catalogue. Unused hours expire at the end of the month. Duo and Family give those 15 hours to the plan manager only. Other members need an add-on, priced on the account, not in the $23.99 headline. Student plans do not include that listening time. Fifteen hours is one book for one person, not a family audiobook service. A public library Libby or Hoopla audiobook is the substitute when you can wait for a hold. The cap is the library’s, described on the library-card guide. Do not pay for 15 hours you will not use, and do not cancel a plan you use for music because the hours went unused. Unused hours are a reason to stop treating Spotify as your audiobook app, not an automatic cancel.

Library music streaming (Freegal/Hoopla where available) as a partial substitute

Some Canadian libraries offer Freegal or another download-to-own music service. Halifax Public Libraries’ movies and music page, read 25 Sep 2026, does not list Freegal. It does list Hoopla at three borrows a month, Kanopy at 10 tickets with Kanopy Kids unlimited, Medici.tv for concerts and opera, and the National Film Board collection at nfb.ca. Ottawa Public Library’s Hoopla page says four borrows a month, with music albums lent for seven days and counted as one item. That is a few albums, not a commute catalogue. It is a real substitute for the week you want one record, and a poor substitute for a teenager who skips songs all evening.

Use the library for the album you would have streamed once, and for audiobooks, before you add a second paid music app. If Hoopla’s cap is gone by the tenth of the month and someone still needs offline music in the car, the paid plan earned its seat. If the cap sits unused, the paid plan did not. Caps differ by library and they change. Ottawa cut Hoopla from six to four and Kanopy from 12 tickets to eight on the pages we read. The card page is the cap. A national “unlimited library music” claim is not a thing.

Cancel and downgrade paths on both platforms before renewal

Downgrade before you cancel if a smaller plan fits. On Spotify, a couple moves from Family to Duo, a graduate moves from Student to Individual only when the student price has ended, and a single listener does not need Family. Spotify’s change-plan help says you manage a direct plan from the account page. A plan billed by a phone or internet company has to be cancelled with that partner first. Members can only switch plans once every 12 months. Read that note before you experiment.

On Apple, the organizer changes the plan in Settings, their name, Subscriptions. Moving from Individual to Family is the organizer’s subscription, and it is meant to stop the other Individual renewals. Moving from Family back to Individual is the same screen, done before the renewal. Apple’s cancel steps, support page published 14 Sep 2026, are Settings, your name, Subscriptions, the plan, Cancel Subscription. A trial should be cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends. If the receipt is from a carrier, the carrier cancels it. The click path and the playlist question are the Spotify cancel guide and, for an Apple-billed plan, the same Settings list as any other Apple subscription on the trial checklist.

Put the renewal on the household calendar seven days early, which is also when Spotify says it reminds you before a trial ends. A gift card can pay a plan you have already decided to keep. It should not be the reason two Individuals survive another year.

Frequently asked questions

What do Spotify and Apple Music cost in Canada?

Read 25 Sep 2026, before tax the account may add: Spotify Individual $13.99, Duo $19.99, Family $23.99, Student $6.39. Apple Music Student $5.99, Individual $11.99, Family $19.99. A year is that monthly price times 12. Confirm the renewal screen.

Is Spotify Student available in Quebec?

The student page's offer line says it is not available to users in Quebec, or to users who have already tried Premium. Eligibility otherwise is 18 or over, SheerID, up to four years, then Individual at $13.99. Apple's student price is $5.99 for up to 48 months.

When does Family beat Individual?

On Spotify, two full-price listeners should look at Duo at $19.99 before Family at $23.99. Family wins at three listeners, or when you need managed child accounts. On Apple, two Individuals are $23.98 and Family is $19.99, so two active listeners are the break-even. Empty seats do not count.

Do I get audiobooks with Premium?

Spotify includes 15 hours a month for Individual and for the Duo or Family manager. Hours expire monthly. Other family members need an add-on. Student plans do not include that time. Library Libby or Hoopla is the substitute if you can wait on a hold.

Can a student in another city join Spotify Family?

Spotify's family page says members live at the same address and confirm it. A student away at school stays on Student or Individual until they live at that address. Plan members can only switch plans once every 12 months.
