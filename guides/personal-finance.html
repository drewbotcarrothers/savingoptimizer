Personal Finance

Personal Finance savings for Canadian households

Personal finance here means the household money system — not day-trading. Park emergency cash on a 12-month yield, and move the week a HISA promo dies. Waive or leave a package fee instead of parking dead money, and design the account so NSF charges stop being a calendar bug. Give every dollar a job before hydro and property tax swing. Pay high-interest balances in an order that still leaves a buffer and a small registered skim. Give a tax refund a written job. Read TFSA room before you automate, and decide RRSP versus TFSA from your bracket. Calm beats clever. Education only.
