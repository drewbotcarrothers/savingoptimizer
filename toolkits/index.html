Frequently asked questions

Is this financial advice?

No. The toolkits are education only. They explain rules, give you the math and the words to use, and point to official sources. They aren't financial, tax, legal, insurance or medical advice, and they don't recommend any company or product. No savings are guaranteed.

What if it isn’t useful to me?

Email contact@savingoptimizer.com within 30 days of purchase and you'll get a full refund. No forms.

Do I get updates?

Yes. Buyers get free updated files for the 2027 edition, including an update when the CRA posts new yearly figures such as the 2027 TFSA and RRSP limits. The new download link goes to the email you used at checkout.

How do I get the files?

Checkout is by Stripe. After you pay, you land on a download page with a folder link and a one-click zip. If anything goes wrong, email us and we'll send the link.

Who wrote it, and how?

Andrew, the founder of Saving Optimizer. Each toolkit was drafted with AI assistance, then fact-checked against dated official Canadian sources listed at the back. Worked examples use example numbers and are labelled Example.

Can I share it?

The files are for personal use by the purchaser. Please don't share or resell them.
