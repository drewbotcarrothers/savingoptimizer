Editorial policy

How we create content

Saving Optimizer guides are researched and drafted with AI assistance, then checked against official Canadian sources before they are published. This page explains that process, how dates work, and how affiliate disclosures work.

Does Saving Optimizer use AI?

Yes. AI tools assist with research, outlines, and first drafts of Saving Optimizer guides. We say so on every guide, in a short note near the end of the page.

How is each guide checked?

Each guide is checked against official Canadian sources: the Canada Revenue Agency, canada.ca and other federal departments, provincial and territorial government sites, and regulators such as the CRTC, the Financial Consumer Agency of Canada, and provincial energy and insurance regulators. For product terms, we use the provider's own page. Sources are listed on the guide, usually in a "Sources" section with the date they were checked.

When a figure could not be confirmed on an official page, the guide tells you where to check instead of guessing.

Are the numbers real?

We do not invent figures. Limits, rates, fees, and dates come from a named source. When a guide uses a worked example to show the arithmetic, the example is labelled "Example with made-up inputs" so it is never mistaken for a quote, an average, or your result.

How do dates work?

Each guide shows "Updated" with a date. The published date is the day the guide went live. The updated date changes only when the content changes, such as a new limit, rate, or rule. We do not change dates to make a page look fresh.

Is this financial advice?

No. Saving Optimizer is education only. It is not financial, tax, legal, or insurance advice, and it does not know your situation. Confirm anything that matters with the official source or a licensed professional before you act.

How do affiliate disclosures work?

Some guides mention types of offers, such as a kind of bank account or credit card. If a page contains an affiliate link, it says so in writing near the top of the page, before the first link. Some guides link products they already mention to Amazon.ca, right in the text, such as a snow brush in a winter car kit guide. We only link products with strong ratings from many buyers on Amazon.ca, and we do not quote Amazon prices because they change. As an Amazon Associate, Saving Optimizer earns from qualifying purchases. An affiliate relationship does not change the math or the advice in a guide, and a guide never tells you to buy something you do not need because a link pays.

Who is behind Saving Optimizer?

Saving Optimizer is run by Andrew. Guides carry his byline.

How do I report an error?

If a number is out of date or something is wrong for your province, contact us. Corrections are made on the guide, and the updated date changes when the content changes.

In short

  • AI assists with research and drafting
  • Checked against official Canadian sources
  • No invented figures; examples are labelled
  • Education only, not financial advice
  • Affiliate links are disclosed before the link
