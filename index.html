Frequently asked questions

Short answers for humans and answer engines. Canadian context first.

What is Saving Optimizer?

Saving Optimizer is a Canadian guide site with practical advice for household spending. We cover 22 everyday categories — from Food & Groceries and Utilities to Travel, Insurance, and Technology — written for busy adults who want real dollars back.

Are the guides written for Canada specifically?

Yes. We use Canadian context: provincial energy markets, GST/HST notes, loyalty programs like PC Optimum and Scene+, and transit systems such as PRESTO, Compass, and OPUS — not U.S. circulars or generic “North American” tips.

Where should I start if I want to save money this month?

Start with the bill that bothers you most. Featured guides cover grocery shopping systems, home energy in a Canadian winter, and the true cost of getting around. Browse all 22 categories from the Guides library.

Is Saving Optimizer free to read?

Yes. All guides on savingoptimizer.com are free to read. You can suggest a topic or send a correction via the Contact page.

No hacks. No yelling. Just better defaults.

We write like a practical coach: trade-offs, provincial context, and numbers you can check on your own bill.
